# Three PointCast bookshops

User-authorized scope: create and publish an art-heavy minimalist Siddhartha shop/resource, plus Den of Thieves by James B. Stewart and Playing for Pizza by John Grisham. Parent coordinates the release lane; no changes to gated intern PR #1304.

## Product

A quiet three-book front door at `/books/`, a river world at `/siddhartha/`, and distinct companion shelves at `/books/den-of-thieves/` and `/books/playing-for-pizza/`. New, used and library discovery stays outbound. PointCast owns no stock, payments, fulfillment, prices or affiliate arrangements for this feature.

Publisher and author records establish title/author, genre, translator, format and ISBN. Used-copy links are verified bookseller routes, not availability promises. Library records and entry points are labeled with matching-edition and membership limits. Reading pathways and discussion prompts are original editorial interpretations, with deeper plot material inside closed native disclosures.

## Rights and evidence

Four original artworks were generated with OpenAI's built-in image generator, inspected, and optimized to WebP. Public provenance carries prompts and generation boundaries. No Hesse paintings, publisher covers, historical photographs or copyrighted modern passages are reproduced. The New York scene is contemporary imagined atmosphere, not evidence of a historical event. The Parma scene is an imagined literary setting, not a travel photograph.

Siddhartha's German first publication in 1922 does not establish every English translation as free. Gutenberg 2500 credits Gunther Olesch, Anke Dreher, Amy Coulter, Stefan Langer and Semyon Chaichenets; it is not Rosner's translation. Gutenberg marks 2499 and 2500 public domain in the USA, and territorial checks remain the reader's responsibility. No full text is hosted.

WorldCat responses were inconsistent during research, including a temporary rate limit for Siddhartha ISBN searches. The Siddhartha page uses a verified Libby Bernofsky ebook and the current official-linked El Segundo Vega library catalog entry point instead; it explicitly does not claim exact local print holdings. New Directions gives inconsistent format dates for Rosner's ISBN across its pages, so that card omits its publication date. Modern Grisham/Stewart texts are not presented as public-domain downloads.
