# Nouns Drum Club Bandmates and Everybody’s in the Band

Mike requested a companion Nouns NFT set and an iconic, accessible multi-format campaign for PointCast, Industry Next, and the existing ad network.

## Delivered source

- Twelve native Noun artwork cards, 1600px PNG + SVG originals +800px WebP previews, each coupled to a distinct valid Drum Club score.
- Three original campaign master images from OpenAI’s built-in image generator. Exact prompts and SHA-256 hashes are in `docs/campaigns/nouns-drum-club/`.
- Thirty PNG, editable SVG, and HTML creatives across300×250,336×280,728×90,970×250,300×600,160×600,320×50,320×100,1080×1080,and1080×1920.
- Collection gallery, campaign gallery/size selector, two verified downloadable archives, public provenance, preview metadata, guide links, Block0605, and homepage/Nouns discovery.
- Opt-in exact-size portable ad support and PointCast music/Nouns rail selection. Industry Next changes are maintained in a separate PR in `mhoydich/nouns5.4`;39footer mounts are repinned while build/verify scripts preserve the pin.

## Boundaries

The collection is an unminted preview: no contract, token, supply, price, royalties, wallet transaction, or sales claim. Playing and remixing never require ownership. Ads are static and silent. Only PointCast and Industry Next placements will be reported as live after browser verification; availability in inventory is not proof of another publisher’s delivery.

Underlying Nouns artwork is attributed separately. Nouns describes its art as CC0 in its [official introduction](https://www.nouns.com/learn/what-is-nouns-dao). No additional formal license is selected for the new cards/scores.

## Validation and release gates

Focused instrument, score, metadata, network, and exact-dimension asset tests pass. Package verification checks dimensions, safe paths, expected counts, referenced assets, full extracted-file hashes, and deterministic ZIPs. Browser checks cover desktop and390px layouts, exact300×250rendering, banner containment, selector links, and a Bandmate link opening silently at its specified tempo. Canonical deployment IDs/hashes and publisher evidence will be saved in the final release report; this source note alone does not claim deployment.

The poster-image-engine scripts were unavailable as unloaded iCloud placeholders. Built-in generation remained available; a declarative brief/manifest was maintained directly, and the checked-in generation/packaging scripts validate dimensions, source hashes, and final outputs. No alternative image model/API was used.
