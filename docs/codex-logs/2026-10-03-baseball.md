# A game you can sit inside — a look at baseball

Mike's directive: “create a pointcast a look at baseball”.

Built `/baseball` as a PointCast editorial about shared attention: field geometry, pauses, scorekeeping, the history of belonging, and the people beside the spectator. Credited to PointCast / Codex; no personal Mike memories are invented.

The feature includes an original AI-generated ballpark illustration (prompt and provenance in `public/images/baseball/README.md`), six source notes, the complete essay, a six-batter composed half inning, three viewing lenses, keyboard-operable controls, a text transcript, and a browser-local memory card with plain-text export. The illustration is interpretive rather than documentary. The plays are fixed examples with explicit advances rather than a complete baseball rules engine.

Block 0688 and homepage, New Today, Apps, Play, sitemap and LLM discovery entries lead to the feature. `/baseball.json` contains the essay, sources, and demonstration plays; visitor notes never enter it. The page uses isolated BlockLayout mode to keep global presence, wallet/session and chrome effects out of this self-contained reading experience.

Verification:

- Three model tests prove batter conservation, forced walk movement, sacrifice fly scoring, end state, replay bounds, and malformed saved-note handling. The existing Pocket Rocks discovery regression also passes.
- Independent factual review checked geometry, integration language, source paraphrases, attribution, and the legal six-batter sequence: 2 runs / 2 hits / 3 outs / 1 left on base. It prompted consistent “half inning” teaching labels.
- Independent DOM review passed 87 assertions against rendered preview markup: all states and lenses, back/replay, safe literal text, save/reload/clear, corrupt and denied storage, and text download content/MIME/filename/URL cleanup.
- In-app browser review checked desktop and 390×844 mobile layouts, six-batter play, keyboard Back, lens switching, memory persistence across reload, clear, and download initiation. Mobile document width was 390px with no horizontal overflow and no broken images.
- `git diff --check` passes. Compilation status and the final review snapshot are recorded in the PR.

Publication follows `docs/OPERATIONS.md`: merge approval first, then `scripts/deploy.sh` from fresh origin/main, then production verification. A preview is not a live publication.
