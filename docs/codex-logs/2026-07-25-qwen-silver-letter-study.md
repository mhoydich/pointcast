## Regional frame

The work is grounded in Kaiping, Jiangmen, western Guangdong and the relational
history of qiaopi or “silver letters”: family news and remittances carried
between Wuyi home villages and overseas communities. The interface uses four
signals—Tan River level, kitchen warmth, distance from home, and neighbors
helping—rather than generic national motifs.

The page explicitly states that it is a contemporary artwork inspired by
regional histories, not an archive or a representation of every local
experience.
