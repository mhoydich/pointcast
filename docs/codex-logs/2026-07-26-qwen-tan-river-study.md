## Premise

`潭江记忆潮汐 / Tan River Memory Tides` turns five cultural-memory
categories—rain, travel, kitchens, migration, and river crossings—into an
evolving spatial sound map. The regional frame is the Tan River through
Kaiping and the wider Jiangmen area in Guangdong.

Qwen Token Plan generated five bilingual poetic seeds. They are visibly
labeled model-authored prompts, not testimony, oral history, quotations, or
evidence from residents. The sound layer is procedural browser Web Audio, not
Qwen Audio, and begins only after an explicit listener gesture.

## Public safety boundary

- This is cultural memory, not live hydrology or a flood-warning system.
- It contains no forecast, water-level, hazard, or emergency guidance.
- The public PointCast edition stores only five numeric counts in localStorage.
- No name, voice, account, location, or free text is collected.
- Current river or emergency information belongs with the relevant local
  public authority, outside this artwork.
