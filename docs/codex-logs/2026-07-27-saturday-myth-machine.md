# Saturday Myth Machine — Mascot Atlas 001

Date: 2026-07-27
Owner: Codex
Requested by: Michael Hoydich

## Outcome

PointCast 25 now has a playful discovery companion at `/mascot-battler`:

- 25 permanent school mascot field cards
- 25 adjacent CORS-open JSON card receipts
- 8 original creature-class illustrations
- 6 arena conditions
- 3 deterministic battle rounds
- conference-versus-class deck selection
- browser-local card keeping and atlas filters
- permanent Block 0522

## Editorial model

Every card combines a mascot identity with a school place, local resources,
tradition, current PointCast rank evidence, and five intentionally transparent
game stats: Locality, Legacy, Myth, Form, and Balance.

Form is derived from PointCast 25 Board 000 rank. The other scores are editorial
game balance, not assessments of institutions, students, performers, or living
animals. Each mascot fact links to an official athletics source checked on
2026-07-27.

## Visual system

The eight 1024×1280 field plates were generated and verified through the local
poster-image-engine workflow. They depict broad creature archetypes rather than
school logos, trademarked graphics, or official costume designs.

## Product boundaries

Saturday Myth Machine is unofficial and unaffiliated. It has no wagering,
prizes, account, wallet, purchase, or server-side collection ledger. Kept cards
remain in localStorage.
