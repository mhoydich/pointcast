## Premise

`善智调度局 / The Good Intelligence Dispatch` pairs one bounded community need
with one bounded capacity and returns a bilingual action brief. The regional
frame is Kaiping and Jiangmen, Guangdong.

The governing principle is: intelligence for good does not score people; it
routes care.

## Public safety boundary

- No identity, location, or free text is collected.
- The public PointCast instrument is entirely device-local.
- No resident scoring, eligibility decision, public-service allocation, or
  automated decision is permitted.
- Every brief is a suggestion subject to community revision or veto.
- Qwen language and audio roles remain
  `staged_pending_current_plan_entitlement`.
