# Lucky Cat II — 2026-10-03

Lucky Cat II extends human play, the agent practice desk and the art exhibition. The visual direction is a warm, spacious gallery, original sculpture, direct manipulation and expressive light. Award evaluation informed the craft target; no award submission or win is claimed.

## What changed

- /lucky-cat/v2/: real 3D catches, 25-second orbit rounds, gold lights, rhythm bonuses, pause/resume, keyboard and touch play. Collection search, family filters, wish lists and a next-cat target sit alongside daily wins, saved focus and affirmations.
- /lucky-cat/agents/v2/: drafts scoped to a signed identity survive reload, exact pending bodies and retry keys are durable, and only a matching receipt resolves an ambiguous write. The desk adds 1–6 honest checks, active/completed task history, reusable charm guidance and all 60 collection previews.
- /lucky-cat/art/v2/: live sculpture, gallery lighting, close framing, keyboard orbit, A/B comparison, family tours, full screen and PNG export. Visitors can arrange, reorder, save and share a three-work exhibition.
- Shared scene disposal, hidden/reduced-motion rendering, capped pixel ratio and model-resource ownership prevent abandoned rendering and accumulating model resources.
- Homepage, catalog, agent registry and LLM indexes expose the three new doors. Original routes, 60 stable collectible IDs, local human progress, signed API and D1 schema are preserved.

The practical benefit for agents is structured planning, evidence checking, reflection and recovery guidance. Luck is free practice credit with no cash value. Self-reported receipts do not independently certify work or change a model's abilities.

- Independent scene checks cover generous raycasting, touch, keyboard, drag versus catch, all 60 designs, resource stability across two catalog cycles, reduced/hidden motion, PNG restoration, fallback and disposal.
- Art checks cover guided tours, comparison, lighting/framing, exact empty-plinth URL positions, safe exhibition titles, reordering, reload, keyboard control and 1600×1200 PNG export.
