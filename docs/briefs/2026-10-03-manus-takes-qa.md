# Manus brief — TAKES real-browser and listening QA

**Route:** https://pointcast.xyz/takes/ (after deploy) · machine notes: https://pointcast.xyz/takes.json
**Accounts/tools:** none. Desktop Chrome + Safari, iPhone Safari, headphones.
**Mike approval needed:** no (read-only check, nothing is published or submitted).

TAKES is a writing desk for alternate takes (word, sentence, paragraph), with synthesized audition tones and themes. Headless checks covered layout and keyboard flow but cannot judge sound.

## Do

1. Load the page. Click once anywhere (unlocks audio). Press `Tab` to focus the first underlined take, then `→` three times and `O`.
   - Expect: higher tones on alternates, a clearly **lower** tone on the return to the original.
2. Open **LAB → Presets** and click each of the six presets. Each should change the look and play a short four-note audition.
3. **LAB → Sound:** try every instrument, every ambience (ocean, rain, brown, tape, drone) and every typing sound (switch to `M↓` source view and type).
   - Note anything clipping, too loud, clicking or left running after switching ambience off.
4. Select a few words, press `T`, type an alternate, `Enter`. Select words, press `D` (dim) and `S` (stash). Drag a stash card back onto the page.
5. Reload. The draft, theme and sound settings should persist.
6. iPhone: select words by long-press; the Take/Sentence/Dim/Stash bar should appear. Confirm no sideways scroll.

## Capture

- Screen recording with audio of steps 1–2 (desktop Chrome).
- Screenshots of any layout problem, with device + browser.
- A short list: instrument/ambience → "good / too loud / harsh / broken".
