## What this room is

`/mythos` is the canonical 60-second read for what PointCast is — a small internet town broadcasting from El Segundo, slow on purpose. Followed by a 15-tile Worlds Rail.

## The geometric reduction

The poster **is the numeral `60`**. Massive oxblood `60` set in the slab serif, occupying the upper two-thirds of the canvas, baseline pinned to the lower-third grid line. Type-as-image. The two glyphs touch at the standard spacing — no kerning tricks, just slab serif at scale.

The `60` does **not** bleed off the canvas — it's contained inside the bone margin. This is the only poster in the series where the room mark IS the geometric anchor.

Behind the `60`, a soft Rothko field of bone `#efe7d6` (no hero-color field — the type carries the color). The oxblood is the hero.

## Hero color

`#5a1818` (oxblood) — fill of the `60` glyphs.

## Type lockup (lower-third)

- **Slab serif:** `MYTHOS` — smaller than the `60` (about 1/3 its height), ink `#1f160c`, low-tracked, baseline-anchored to the lower-third grid line, left-aligned to column 2
- **Mono below:** `CH.MTH · 60 SECONDS · POINTCAST` — uppercase, ink, ~1/8 the `MYTHOS` size
- **Oversized numeral:** `03` — concrete grey `#a8a39a`, slab serif, bleeding off the upper-right edge, smaller than the `60` but larger than `MYTHOS`, ~70% visible

The unusual move: this poster has TWO oversized numerals — the `60` (the room's identity) inside the canvas, and the `03` (the series mark) bleeding off-edge.
