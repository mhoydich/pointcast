# OFF-65 phone QA — draft only

Open the pinned file-proxy preview in the PR (or serve its public directory
locally, `/sketches/fireside-cards/`). No account or wallet needed. Do not deploy.

On a real iPhone/Safari and Android/Chrome, test 320–430px portrait and landscape:
- Initial card silent/still. Sound begins only from Light it; silent remains silent.
- Hear whether .25 fire recording volume is comfortable; confirm no unexpected
  speaker playback on returning from another app, lock screen or navigation.
- No next/open/stay controls or swipes before thirty foreground seconds;
  pause fire & sound and Escape always stop playback. Resume is explicit.
- Three exact attributed quotes, source links correct, horizontal swipe both
  ways, vertical scrolling/zoom usable, no trapped focus or horizontal overflow.
- OS reduced motion gives a still image; scripts off give a readable first card
  and source. With VoiceOver/TalkBack, check focus and the ready announcement.

Capture device/browser/version, screenshots and issues in
`docs/manus-logs/2026-11-14-fireside-cards.md`. Real-device audio, accessibility and
comfort have not been checked by Ada. No posting or live edits required.
