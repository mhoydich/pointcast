# HOODRAT — Agency Package

**Date:** 2026-09-01
**Prepared by:** The expert advertising agency (Claude session, campaign branch)
**Client subject:** $HOODRAT, a memecoin on Robinhood Chain
**Deliverables:** Situation brief (McKinsey-style) + creative campaign (Chiat/Day-style) + copy bank, taglines, personas, music direction

> Compliance note: HOODRAT's own site states the token has no intrinsic value, is for entertainment only, and has no relationship with Robinhood Markets. All creative below keeps that frame — culture, not investment advice. Nothing here is a promise of returns; the "millionaire math" campaign is explicitly framed as arithmetic-as-folklore, with a disclaimer lockup on every asset.

## Part I — The Brief (McKinsey-style)

### 1. Situation

- Robinhood launched Robinhood Chain in July 2026 to put stocks and real-world assets on-chain. The chain's early economic activity was instead dominated by memecoins — HOODRAT chief among them, topping DEX Screener's trending list for the network.
- HOODRAT positions itself as the mascot "from the mud": underdog identity, raw community energy, a return to authenticity.
- Holder base: ~5,150 wallets — small, early, evangelist-dense.
- Founding lore already exists and is verifiable on-chain: a trader turned ~$50K into nearly $1M on HOODRAT (documented by Arkham, covered by CoinDesk/Yahoo Finance). Lore is the moat.

### 2. Complication

- Memecoin attention cycles are measured in weeks. Robinhood Chain's "memecoin summer" headline window is already aging (July–August 2026 coverage).
- HOODRAT has lore but no *narrative infrastructure* — no repeatable story format that converts a headline into a durable identity.
- The category's default marketing (rocket emojis, price talk) is legally fraught and creatively exhausted. Differentiation requires refusing the category's own language.

### 3. Key insight

**A billion dollars is not a number. It is a crowd.**

$1,000,000,000 ÷ $1,000,000 = **1,000 millionaires**. The same billion is 4,000 quarter-millionaires, 20,000 people with $50K, or 200,000 people with $5K. Wealth headlines report billions as monoliths owned by one person; a community token can invert that: the collective *is* the denomination. This is the only memecoin story that is simultaneously (a) pure arithmetic, (b) emotionally about the crowd rather than the chart, and (c) native to Robinhood's own founding myth (the platform "for the little guy").

### 4. Strategic recommendation

Position HOODRAT not as a coin but as **a census**: the running count of ordinary people standing in the same spot. Every asset counts people, never price.

Three horizons:

| Horizon | Move | Metric |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Now (0–30d) | Launch "The Math" campaign (Part II). Own the 1,000-millionaires arithmetic. | Earned mentions of the math itself |
| Next (30–90d) | Lore engine: serialize chain history — the $50K trader, first 5,150 wallets, "memecoins beat the stocks" irony — as numbered episodes | Episode completion / reshares |
| Later (90d+) | Community rituals: holder-count milestones celebrated like town population signs | Holder count, not market cap |

### 5. Risks

- Regulatory: any implication of expected profit. Mitigation: every asset carries the entertainment disclaimer; the math is framed as trivia, never projection.
- Trademark: no Robinhood marks, ever; "Robinhood Chain" only as factual chain name.
- Community capture: campaign must be CC0/remixable or the community will (correctly) reject agency polish. Ship editable templates.

## Part II — The Campaign (Chiat/Day-style)

### Campaign: **"THE MATH."**

The "1984" move for this category is refusing to show a chart. Black background. White type. One equation per placement. The rat appears only as a small signature in the corner — the way a crown or swoosh would.

**Manifesto film (60s, narrated flat and slow over a single held shot of a sewer grate at dawn):**

> A billion dollars.
> Say it out loud and it sounds like one person's money.
> It isn't. It never was.
> A billion dollars is a thousand millionaires.
> It's four thousand people who paid off the house.
> Twenty thousand people who quit the job they hated.
> Two hundred thousand people who covered rent this month.
> The number never changed. Only who's holding it.
> We came from the mud. We count in people.
> HOODRAT. Do the math.
> *(super, small: No intrinsic value. Entertainment only. Not affiliated with Robinhood Markets.)*

**Out-of-home / static series** — each poster is only an equation:

1. `$1,000,000,000 ÷ 1,000 = everyone you know`
2. `1 billionaire = 1,000 millionaires. Choose your denomination.`
3. `5,150 wallets. 0 suits.`
4. `$50,000 → $1,000,000. On-chain. Look it up.` *(lore poster — cites the Arkham finding)*
5. `They built the chain for stocks. The rats moved in first.`

### Taglines (easy, ranked)

1. **Do the math.** *(campaign line)*
2. **From the mud. In the millions.** *(counting people, not dollars)*
3. **A billion is a crowd.**
4. **Count people, not dollars.**
5. **The little guy, plural.**
6. **We are the denomination.**

### Copy bank (social-ready, remixable)

- "Nobody needs a billion. A thousand of us need a million. Same number."
- "Population sign energy: HOODRAT — pop. 5,150 and counting."
- "The chain was built for Wall Street's stocks. Check what's actually trending on it."
- "Lore check: someone turned $50K into $1M here. The receipts are public. That's the whole point of a chain."
- "Rich is a rounding error. Together is a market cap."

### Personas to consider

| Persona | Who | Why they buy the *story* | Channel |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **The Robinhood Native** | Retail trader, 24–38, has had the app since GME | Already believes the "little guy" myth; HOODRAT is that myth with a mascot | In-feed, Discord, X |
| **The Lore Collector** | Crypto-native, archives screenshots, quotes on-chain receipts | The $50K→$1M story and "memecoins beat the stocks" irony are collectible artifacts | X threads, Farcaster |
| **The Exit-Liquidity-Wary Skeptic** | Been burned; hates rocket emojis | The anti-chart, disclaimer-forward, count-people posture is the only pitch that doesn't insult them | Long-form, podcasts |
| **The Normie Bystander** | Doesn't hold anything; loves a heist narrative | "Rats moved into Wall Street's new building first" is a story you retell at dinner | Earned media, TikTok |

### Music direction (generation brief)

- **Anthem:** "Do The Math" — slow, sparse boom-bap; a counting hook ("one for the mud, a thousand for the crowd"); no price talk in lyrics. 85 BPM, minor key, church-basement piano.
- **Sting (3s):** four ascending clicks (an abacus, a turnstile counter) resolving on a rat squeak one octave up — the sonic logo. Usable as a notification sound.
- **Remix policy:** stems released CC0 on day one. The community writing verse two *is* the media plan.
- Production path if we execute: Higgsfield/Suno-class generation, one anthem + one sting + one 15s loop for shorts.

### Chain history / lore calendar (serialized, one per week)

1. Ep.1 — "They built it for stocks." (July 2026 chain launch, memecoin takeover)
2. Ep.2 — "The $50K trader." (Arkham receipts, told as folklore, not as a promise)
3. Ep.3 — "Pop. 5,150." (the first wallets, census framing)
4. Ep.4 — "Trending #1." (DEX Screener summit, screenshot as artifact)
5. Ep.5 — "The Math." (the millionaire arithmetic, campaign capstone)

### What "enjoy" means operationally

The campaign never asks anyone to buy. It asks them to *count*. Every asset ends with the census number, the disclaimer, and the line: **Do the math.**
