# Manus brief — Chapter City playtest

**Route:** https://pointcast.xyz/chapter-city/ (after deploy) · rules: https://pointcast.xyz/chapter-city.json
**Accounts/tools:** none. Desktop Chrome, Safari, and an iPhone (Safari).
**Mike approval needed:** no. This is play only; nothing is published or submitted.

Chapter City is a seaside city-builder. Each turn simulates a year and writes it up as a chapter of a novel: recurring characters, a newspaper headline and a yes/no dilemma. Automated checks covered the rules, the save and the layout. They can't judge whether it's fun or whether the writing holds up over a long game.

## Do

1. Read the prologue, then open **How to play**.
2. Build a starter town: a road, homes beside it, industry, a power plant and a water tower. Press **Write Chapter 1**.
3. Play at least **12 chapters**, answering every dilemma. Build services when the hint line suggests them. Try one high-tax year (12% or more) on purpose.
4. Each chapter, note:
   - whether anything you did that year shows up in the text
   - any line that repeats word for word from an earlier chapter
   - any line that contradicts the map (for example, a fire station mentioned when there is none)
5. Try every overlay (pollution, land value, services, crime) and every tool, including Clear.
6. Reload mid-game; the book and the map should come back. Then use **⤓ Novel** to download the Markdown.
7. On iPhone: build with taps and drags. Check that the map doesn't scroll the page while you paint, and that the dilemma buttons are easy to hit.

## Capture

- Screenshots of the map at Years 1, 6 and 12, and of any layout problem.
- The downloaded `.md` novel.
- A short list of repeated or contradictory lines, with the chapter number for each.
- One paragraph: was it fun, and where did it drag?
