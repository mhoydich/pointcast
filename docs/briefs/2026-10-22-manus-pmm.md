## 1. Bind the signal store (Mike approval first)

The code flow for connecting two phones needs a short-lived KV note. Until it is bound, `/api/pmm/signal` answers 503 and the app falls back to paste mode. Pages applies bindings from `wrangler.toml` on every `scripts/deploy.sh`, so a binding added in the dashboard would be wiped by the next deploy: the binding has to live in the repo.

1. On the deploy machine (Mike's iMac), from any worktree: `npx wrangler kv namespace create PMM_KV`. Copy the `id` it prints.
2. In `wrangler.toml`, find the `PMM_KV` comment block and uncomment the three lines, pasting the id. Open a PR with that one change. Mike merges it.
3. `scripts/deploy.sh` (plain; main has changed, so it deploys).

Check: `curl https://pointcast.xyz/api/pmm/signal` returns `{"bound":true,"ttlSeconds":600}`.

## 1b. Turn on the relay (Mike approval first)

Two phones on strict carrier NAT cannot connect with STUN alone. PMM now asks `/api/pmm/turn` for a four-hour Cloudflare Calls TURN credential and adds the relay to its ICE servers when the site has one. Until the two secrets exist the endpoint answers 200 `{"iceServers":[],"reason":"turn-not-configured"}` and nothing changes. The endpoint only answers PointCast's own pages, so check it from the browser, not curl.

1. Cloudflare dashboard → Calls → TURN keys → Create key. Copy the key id and the API token.
2. Workers & Pages → `pointcast` → Settings → Environment variables → add `CF_TURN_KEY_ID` and `CF_TURN_API_TOKEN` for Production, both encrypted.
3. Redeploy with `scripts/deploy.sh --force` from the iMac (secrets apply on the next deploy).

Check: open https://pointcast.xyz/pmm/, then in the browser console run `fetch('/api/pmm/turn').then(r => r.json())` and expect a non-empty `iceServers` list with `ttlSeconds: 14400`. A plain curl gets 403 `turn-site-only` by design. In PMM, LINK on a connected chat reads `VIA RELAY · 0 · relay on standby`, or a byte count when the relay was needed. Cloudflare Calls TURN has a free monthly allowance; relayed bytes show in the Calls dashboard.

## 2b. Offline open (both apps, no approval needed)

Since #1489, PMM and Nouns Messenger each install a small service worker on the first visit, so the app itself opens with no signal. Nothing that needs the network (the signal note, the SI line, the town ticker) is ever served from the phone's copy.

1. On each phone, with signal, open `https://pointcast.xyz/pmm/` and `https://pointcast.xyz/nouns-messenger/`. Wait five seconds on each. Also open `https://pointcast.xyz/pmm/brand/` once.
   If you added a Home Screen icon for either app, also open each app once from that icon with signal and wait five seconds. On iPhone a Home Screen app keeps its own storage and its own offline copy, separate from the Safari tab, so an icon added before #1489, or not opened with signal since, has nothing to open offline yet.
2. Turn on airplane mode. Close the tabs.
3. Open both URLs again, in the browser and from the Home Screen icon if you added one. Each should draw its full screen: PMM's lobby with your sigil and name, Nouns Messenger's chat list. Open `/pmm/brand/` too.
4. In PMM, tap LOAD "FRIEND". Expected: within about five seconds it shows PASTE MODE (NO SERVER) with a link to send, rather than hanging (headless Chromium took 5.1 s, relay code included). In Nouns Messenger, open the Noggles Desk and send a message. Expected: the companion still answers, since it runs on the phone.
5. Turn signal back on and reload both. Expected: nothing looks different from before step 2.

Pay attention to iPhone Safari. It can evict a site's offline copy after a few weeks without a visit, and a Home Screen app keeps its own copy separate from the Safari tab. If step 3 shows Safari's "You are not connected" page instead of the app, record which phone, which browser, and whether it was the tab or the Home Screen icon.

Capture: each app open in airplane mode (the status bar must show the airplane), and PMM's screen after LOAD in airplane mode.

## 2c. Connect by QR (no approval needed)

Since #1505 every PMM link also shows as a QR code. On Android Chrome a SCAN THEIR CODE key reads the code inside the app. iPhone Safari has no in-app scanner, so it uses the Camera app.

1. Phone A (iPhone): tap LOAD "FRIEND". A QR code shows under the link.
2. Phone B (Android, Chrome): open `https://pointcast.xyz/pmm/`, tap SCAN THEIR CODE, allow the camera, point it at A's QR. Expected: the scanner closes by itself and both phones reach RUN within 15 s.
3. Swap roles. B taps LOAD; A opens the iPhone Camera app, points it at B's QR, taps the yellow link. Expected: Safari opens PMM and both reach RUN.
4. Paste mode runs while `PMM_KV` is unbound, so do this one before section 1:
   - A taps LOAD and shows the offer QR.
   - B scans it and shows SEND THIS ANSWER BACK with its own QR.
   - A takes the answer: SCAN THEIR ANSWER on Android, the Camera app on iPhone.
   - Expected on Android: A connects in place.
   - Expected on iPhone: the answer opens a second Safari tab that says HANDED TO YOUR OTHER PMM TAB, and the first tab connects.
5. Point the scanner at any non-PMM QR code (a menu, a product). Expected: THAT IS NOT A PMM CODE, and the scanner stays open.

Capture: A's QR screen, B's scanner with the yellow frame, the HANDED TO YOUR OTHER PMM TAB screen, and the scan-to-RUN time from LINK.

## 2d. Settle a tab in tez (Mike approval first: real tez moves)

PMM v0.4 settles a TAB with one transfer from the payer's own wallet, and the payee decides whether it is enough. You need two phones, each with Kukai (or Temple) holding a **tz1** address, and a little tez on the payer's side. Send 0.1 tez, no more. Do it twice: once accepted, once declined.

1. **On each phone, prove an address.** SET → paste your tz1 address into YOUR TEZOS ADDRESS → tap PROVE IT WITH YOUR WALLET. A tab opens at `https://pointcast.xyz/pmm/settle#prove=1…`. Tap CONNECT WALLET AND SIGN and approve the signature in the wallet; it is free and moves nothing. Expect "PMM HAS IT. RETURN TO YOUR PMM TAB AND CLOSE THIS ONE.", then in PMM the toast "ADDRESS PROVEN. FRIENDS CAN SETTLE WITH YOU." Do this before connecting the phones, or reconnect after.
2. **Connect and open a tab.** Connect the two phones (step 2 or 2c). On the payer phone, add a TAB of any amount with I OWE THEM.
3. **Open the settle page.** Payer phone: tap TAB. SETTLE IN TEZ should show; tap it. A new tab opens at `https://pointcast.xyz/pmm/settle#to=tz…&me=tz…`. Check that PAY TO matches the payee's address character for character, with the friend's name after it.
4. **Send.** Type 0.1, tap CONNECT WALLET AND SEND, and approve in the wallet. Expect "SENT 0.1 TEZ. YOUR FRIEND CHECKS IT AND TAPS ACCEPT." and then "PMM HAS IT. RETURN TO YOUR PMM TAB AND CLOSE THIS ONE." Close that tab.
5. **Check the chain.** Payer phone: expect "YOU PAID 0.1 TEZ FOR 1 TAB …", then within a minute or two "ON CHAIN · 0.1 TEZ · BLOCK n · … · WAITING FOR <NAME> TO ACCEPT". The tab shows as PENDING in the TAB sheet. It is not settled yet.
6. **Accept.** Payee phone: expect "<NAME> SAYS THEY PAID 0.1 TEZ…", then a card "ON CHAIN: <NAME> PAID YOU 0.1 TEZ FOR 1 TAB WORTH … FROM tz1… · <time> · BLOCK n. ENOUGH?" with ACCEPT and NOT ENOUGH. Tap ACCEPT. Expect "YOU ACCEPTED · 1 TAB SETTLED" there, and "<NAME> ACCEPTED 0.1 TEZ · 1 TAB SETTLED" on the payer phone.
7. **Repeat with NOT ENOUGH.** Add a new tab and settle again with 0.1 tez, but tap NOT ENOUGH on the payee phone. Expect the payer phone to read "<NAME> DID NOT COUNT 0.1 TEZ (NOT ENOUGH) · THOSE TABS ARE OPEN AGAIN", with SETTLE offered again.
8. **Confirm on TzKT.** Open each operation on tzkt.io from the settle tab and confirm the sender, the payee and the amount.

Capture both phones at every step: the PROVE IT result, the settle page before SEND, the wallet's confirm screen, the payer's ON CHAIN line, the payee's ENOUGH? card, both phones after ACCEPT, both after NOT ENOUGH, and the TzKT pages. If anything stalls, record the exact text on screen and how long you waited.
