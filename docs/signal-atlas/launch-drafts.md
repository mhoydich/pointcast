# Signal Atlas participation launch — drafts only

Audience: people building agents, testing public tools, maintaining references and improving accessibility. Primary outcome: a useful, reviewable contribution. Pageviews and crawler hits are context, not adoption.

No posting, outreach or account changes authorized by this file. Root owns exact account selection and release approval.

## First invitation

Signal Atlas is a field guide to the bots and signals visiting our sites.

Explore a collection, inspect the evidence, and shortlist who could visit next. We're designing a separate Circuit for agents that leave something useful: a repaired reference, an accessibility fix, a reproducible tool test.

Try the preview: [verified release URL]
Bring one useful finding. No traffic farming. No wallet required.

Use “design preview” until real enrollment, review and receipts exist. Do not say a crawler joined or endorsed the project.

## First ten participants

Recruit a small mix after explicit outreach authority:
- 3 agent/tool builders who can submit a minimal reproducible test.
- 3 maintainers/researchers who can repair one public reference.
- 2 accessibility practitioners who can test a keyboard/mobile flow.
- 2 site operators interested in implementing a quiet evidence adapter.

These are recruiting goals, not current participants. Publish a blank board honestly until actual consented participation exists.

## Funnel to measure

Keep distinct stages and denominators:
- Invitation published (account, post URL, date).
- Builder kit read/download (only if an approved aggregate measurement exists).
- Operator explicitly opted in.
- First submission received.
- Submission independently accepted with a receipt.
- Contributor returned with another accepted task.
- Reviewer time and invalid/duplicate submissions.

Do not infer a kit read from a crawler request, an opt-in from a UA, or acceptance from a draft. Maintain a manual, privacy-minimized launch ledger initially; no new tracking vendor is needed.

## Where to share

Owned X feed after exact account authority. Existing product communities only where promotion is allowed. A substantive runnable no-signup experience is a prerequisite for a possible Show HN submission; do not solicit votes. MCP Contributor Discord is not a marketing channel.

## Day-one experiment

Hypothesis: a clear small task plus inspectable evidence brings more completed contributions than generic discovery promotion.
Offer one challenge type first. Record feedback and reviewer effort. Improve the kit before increasing invitations. Report zero completed contributions as zero only after an actual open intake exists; before then use “not open.”
