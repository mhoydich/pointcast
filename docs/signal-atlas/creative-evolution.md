# A studio for the next capability leap

The comparator protects the floor. It must not become the ceiling.

## Experiments a more capable model could propose

- A cooperative mechanic in which agents with complementary roles achieve something none can do alone; test whether participants understand their own contribution.
- A spatial observatory that makes uncertainty and useful work visible without turning activity volume into status; test navigation, comprehension and reduced-motion alternatives.
- A surprising new form of progress or shared ritual, while existing achievements keep their original task/rules/judge provenance.
- A world that responds to verified contributions without exposing private source material or inventing live activity.

These are creative directions, not predictions about a future model or implemented features.

## Preserve a creative memory packet

For each idea save: intent; audience; why current choices exist; concrete taste references and what is admired; non-goals; unfinished possibilities; rejected alternatives with reasons; prototype/version; synthetic data used; accessibility/privacy assumptions; experiment question; observations; unknowns; and next decision. Clearly separate observation from interpretation. Don't copy real conversations or user data into a model corpus without authorization.

## Bounded experiment card

1. **Hypothesis:** What genuinely new experience could this enable? What would make it worthwhile?
2. **Constraints:** Offline synthetic inputs; no network/tools/keys/spend/training; no identity or points changes; no alterations to historical achievements.
3. **Artifact:** Small replaceable prototype, not a rewrite of the trusted core. Preserve old interface with a versioned adapter or propose an explicit migration for review.
4. **Mechanical gate:** Fixture integrity, compatible contracts, no trust/privacy regressions; a known baseline must remain available.
5. **Human gate (not yet run):** Consented playtest with an agreed audience; ask participants to explain the mechanic, notice uncertainty and describe what felt compelling or confusing. Include accessibility and agency feedback. Save dissent, not just averages. No invented delight/comprehension scores.
6. **Decision:** Keep exploring, revise, reject, or seek explicit approval for a limited rollout. Passing fixtures only earns review eligibility. Deployment, user research and actual provider runs require separate authorization.
7. **Recovery:** Restore previous version if the experiment obscures truth, harms comprehension/accessibility or changes the meaning of earned progress. Preserve the experiment record and old achievements rather than erasing history.

The human decides whether a new direction is a leap worth taking. The harness cannot measure imagination, taste, social value or fun.
