# BLOOM SHOP — Flowers Machined in El Segundo

**Status:** Draft concept + prompts. No images generated yet. Checkout inactive until Mike approves prices, editions, fulfillment and terms.
**Label on every work:** *AI-generated digital art. Concept and curation by PointCast, El Segundo.*

## Visual direction (lead)

Flowers treated like precision parts. Every bloom is built from flat color planes, hard edges, rulers, rivets and stripes — then softened by the hazy, salt-white light of the South Bay coast. Grid from Zurich, quiet from Stockholm, heat and rhythm from Mexico City, drama from Milan, grease and enamel from a 1958 Grand Avenue garage.

**Shared system (what makes 20 works one show):**
- Format: 4:5 portrait, generous margin, one dominant botanical form.
- Shared accent: *Signal Orange* `#E8552B` appears in every work, however small.
- Shared ground: *Marine Haze* `#EDE8DD` or its dark counterpart *Hangar Navy* `#1F2A3A`.
- Texture: flat matte print with faint screenprint registration offset. No photorealism, no gradients beyond soft coastal haze.
- Forbidden: text, numerals, logos, brand marks, signatures, vases-on-tables décor, imitation of any named artist's work.

## Curatorial story

El Segundo grew flowers before it built airplanes — and then it built parts for things that flew past the sky. BLOOM SHOP imagines a garage on the edge of the refinery and the runway where the mechanics started machining flowers instead of fenders. Each family is a different bench in that shop, run by a different design tradition. The botany is invented, the light is real: 4 p.m. marine layer burning off over Dockweiler.
