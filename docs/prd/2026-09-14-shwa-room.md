# Shwa — a little room for possibility

## Product direction

A place to think out loud and make things together. Shwa names both the room and its AI companion. PointCast is its home. Start on the mobile web inside PointCast, then earn a native app through useful repeat visits.

The scene is the main surface. Persistent controls are small: account, estimated cost, microphone/call, and tool navigation. Conversation outputs are temporary, useful objects: an image, cited research, a chart, a question, a vote, a survey, or a direction check. They can arrive after a short delay and remain scrollable for the current call.

## This release

- PointCast route `/shwa/`, homepage entrance, and `/shwa.json` manifest.
- Supplied floating-island scene. Desktop image studio at the top right; compact call dock at the bottom; ideas collect over the scene.
- Phone layout with a persistent call dock, image preview, and Canvas / Images / Music sheets. Separate scroll areas, safe-area spacing, keyboard-aware image entry, reduced motion, and a transcript drawer.
- Existing PointCast cookie restores login. Google sign-in returns to this room.
- Optional personal Spotify consent using the existing registered callback and `user-read-currently-playing` scope. Credentials are encrypted per PointCast user. Returning users can restore the connection and current track. A separate Spotify player sign-in may still be needed; audio never autoplays. The shared broadcaster connection remains independent.
- Voice, live captions, interactive conversation cards, images, and cited research use the existing OpenAI gateway. Trial quotas and expiry are preserved; runtime availability is the source of truth. Images and research need an explicit click within an eligible call.
- Shwa receives bounded app context and visitor choices. Spotify metadata and audio remain outside model inputs. The room player is removed throughout a voice connection to avoid audio overlap.
- Estimated costs stay visible. x402 requires explicit wallet approval, and hiding the wallet retains pending requests and receipts. It does not automatically fund the OpenAI trial.

## The useful mobile loop

1. Open a shared link; land in the scene without onboarding.
2. Tap once to call Shwa and approve the microphone in the browser.
3. Talk naturally. A relevant object arrives: a picture idea, a question, or something to adjust.
4. Tap Images or the corner preview to make and save an image. Return to the conversation without navigating away.
5. Pull up earlier objects or the live thread. Sign in to reconnect personal services when the call ends.

Success means a visitor can complete this loop on a phone without hunting for call controls, losing a pending payment, or believing the AI sees services it cannot access.

## Next experiments, not shipped

- A stronger pocket sketchbook: save selected artifacts to an account, resume a room, and share a specific artifact with explicit privacy choices.
- Installable web app, then native iOS/Android once retention and session reliability justify it. Native audio routing, interruptions, background behavior, camera/video, push notifications, and purchase rules require their own implementation and testing.
- Shared rooms, real multi-person votes, collaborative games, video calls, modules, and a module store. Current votes and surveys are local preferences, not multiplayer totals.
- Bring an existing AI/runtime through PointCast's connection system; do not imply consumer subscription credit transfers to API calls.
- Phone number/SIP entry, receiving paid call/image requests, funding budgets, and service payments as separate explicit flows. No telephone number or autonomous merchant funding exists in this release.

## Verification boundary

Automated fixtures prove session restoration, account isolation, OAuth state, token refresh failures, metadata separation, and player removal during calls. A real personal Spotify consent and playback round trip still needs the account owner. This release does not expand the capped voice experiment or automatically start paid calls.
