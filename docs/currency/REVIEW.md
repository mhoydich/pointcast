## Economics

Reference scenario: 25 three-note sets; three 25-note jobs × $39 = $117; inbound freight $12; retail $30 plus $6 charged shipping. Order assumptions: packaging $1.25, outgoing postage $5, eight minutes of labor at $30/hour ($4), aggregate fee 2.9% + $0.30 on $36, reserve 5% of product sales ($1.50), acquisition $0. One-time artwork $300; overhead $75; setup/vendor tax $0.

Contribution per order $22.906; total modeled fixed/batch cost $504. At 25 sets sold: $68.65 remains. At 15 sold: −$160.41. Break-even: 23 sets. Unsold inventory does not undo the batch cost. All costs except the vendor baseline and fee benchmark are editable operating assumptions. Vendor tax, income tax and unmodeled costs can reduce results. Sales tax collected for remittance is excluded from spendable revenue. Changing capacity does not automatically change whole-batch print cost.
