# Modular carry companies: research notes (2026-09-29, verified by cc's research agent)

Prices are from the makers' own stores on 2026-09-29. Image URLs were picked by filename and haven't been looked at, so check each one before use (no people).

## Carry: strap, clip and attachment systems

1. **Topologie** (topologie.com)
   - Registered office in Hong Kong. Stores in Paris, Tokyo, Taipei and Hong Kong.
   - Founded 2018 by French climber Carlos Granon, who designs from Japan. Another source says it was co-founded by climbers from France and Japan. Treat the founder as unverified: say "founded 2018 by climbers."
   - System: the Topologie Wares System. Climbing-rope straps end in quick-release swivel carabiners that clip onto any Wares bag, pouch or phone case. The brand claims 120+ straps and 1,000+ combinations (source: fashionunited.com/fashion-jobs/work-at/topologie-jobs/about).
   - Prices: Bottle Sacoche Medium $82–92 (bag only), Phone Strap Adapter $12, straps from $30 (8.0mm Wrist) to $110 (Cargo).
   - Lesson: the strap is the product and the bags are the pods. A $12 adapter lets a customer into the system through their phone. Collaborations (Café du Cycliste, Maison Mihara Yasuhiro, Universal Works) change the look but keep the same interface.
   - Image: https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0689/2001/7069/files/FW26_BottleSacoche_M_GreenCheck_CheckWool_Cover.jpg (already on the site as /images/bags/topologie-bottle-sacoche-medium.jpg, cropped)

2. **Peak Design** (peakdesign.com)
   - San Francisco. Founded 2010 by Peter Dering.
   - System: three connectors shared across its products.
     - Anchor Links: quick-connect strap anchors.
     - Capture: a clip that holds a camera on any strap or belt.
     - SlimLink: a MagSafe-compatible phone mount used by its cases, tripods, car mounts and bike mounts.
   - Prices: Anchor Links $29.95, Mobile Tripod $79.95.
   - Lesson: pre-sells every launch on Kickstarter to test demand. Lifetime warranty. Once someone owns the connector, every other product that uses it is an easy add-on.
   - Numbers:
     - First campaign (Capture, 2011): $364,698 from 5,258 backers (en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peak_Design).
     - Roller Pro campaign (2025): $13,408,553.
     - Lifetime total: over $34M per Wikipedia versus "$60M+" claimed by the founder. Say "tens of millions."
   - Image: https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/2986/1172/products/AnchorLinks-pdp-1-HERO.jpg

3. **Tom Bihn** (tombihn.com)
   - Seattle, with its own factory. Company founded 1982 in Santa Cruz; Tom Bihn has made bags since 1972.
   - System: O-rings sewn inside every bag. Key Straps ($8) and organizer pouches clip onto them. Shoulder straps are removable and sold separately.
   - Prices: Synapse 25 $265, Absolute Shoulder Strap $30, Key Strap $8, Mystery Pouch $12 (made from leftover fabric).
   - Lesson: a cheap internal standard turns $8–$50 pouches into repeat purchases, and making bags locally is the story. No public numbers.
   - Image: https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/1089/8530/files/SYNAPSE25-BLACK-ZEST.jpg

4. **MOLLE/PALS + GORUCK** (goruck.com)
   - PALS webbing: rows of 1-inch webbing spaced 1 inch apart, stitched every 1.5 inches. Developed by the US Army's Natick labs; MOLLE introduced in 1997 (en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MOLLE). It's the original open attachment standard.
   - GORUCK: Jacksonville Beach, FL. Founded 2008 by Jason McCarthy, a Special Forces veteran, and his wife Emily.
   - Prices: GR1 USA $395 (Coastal Camo; $525 in waxed canvas or X-Pac), GR1 Field Pocket $80, event patches $5 (Taco Ruck, Donut Ruck, Coffee Ruck).
   - Lesson: it built on an open standard instead of inventing one. It offers the Scars lifetime guarantee, and its $5 patches are souvenirs of its own events.
   - Image: https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0275/4985/9940/files/401-03161600FieldPocket-GR1-Cordura-3LRangerGreen1copy.jpg

5. **Bogg** (boggbag.com)
   - Founded 2008 by Kim Vaccarella. New Jersey according to NBC (not confirmed on an official page).
   - System: an EVA-foam tote whose holes take Bogg Bits (charms), cupholders, dividers and inserts.
   - Prices: Original $90–100, Bogg Bits $15.
   - Lesson: the Jibbitz playbook applied to a beach bag. Themed charms are cheap, collectible and good gifts.
   - Numbers:
     - Revenue $1M (2018) → $4M (2019) → $54M (2023), per NBC LA (nbclosangeles.com, article 3582897).
     - $140M in 2025, about $400M lifetime, per insideretail.us (Aug 2026).
   - Only link boggbag.com; lookalike scam stores show up in search.
   - Image: already on the site as /images/bags/bogg-bag-original.jpg

6. **Crocs Jibbitz**
   - Started in a basement in Boulder, CO, in 2005–06 by Sheri and Rich Schmelzer.
   - Crocs bought it in October 2006 for $10M cash plus up to $10M earn-out (Crocs press release at investors.crocs.com). At the time it sold about $2M a month at $2.49 a charm (Spokesman-Review, Oct 2006).
   - Crocs' FY2025 10-K calls Jibbitz a "key product pillar." Crocs brand revenue was about $3.3B in 2025; Jibbitz isn't broken out.
   - System: charms that snap into the clog's holes. The holes are the standard.
   - No image (crocs.com returned rate-limit errors). Use no product image, or an illustration tile.

## Multi-use gear (Mike: "say chair, etc, for multi use")

7. **Snow Peak Iron Grill Table (IGT)** (snowpeak.com)
   - Sanjo, Niigata, Japan. Founded 1958 by Yukio Yamai.
   - System: a camp kitchen built on a unit grid. 3- or 4-unit frames take ½-, 1- or 2-unit inserts (stoves, grills, storage, worktops).
   - Prices: Entry IGT Set $375.95, Collapsible IGT 4 Unit Frame $224.95, Extension IGT $419.95.
   - Number: Bain Capital led a ¥34B (about $226M) management buyout in 2024 and took Snow Peak private (law.asia).
   - Lesson: once someone owns a frame, they buy inserts for years.
   - Image: https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0330/3299/4860/files/CK-904_20240405-SP_M4_08552_path_jpg.png

8. **Helinox** (helinox.com)
   - South Korea. Founded 2009 by Jake Lah and his son Young, as a spin-off of DAC, the tent-pole maker (another source says 2011).
   - System: a shock-corded aluminium pole-and-hub frame shared by its chairs, cots and tables, with add-ons and spare parts sold separately.
   - Prices: Chair One $109.95, Tactical Table L $194.95, cup holder $17, replacement case $10.95.
   - Number: 2020 sales of ₩41B (up 26%), with chairs about 70% of sales (Asia Economy, cm.asiae.co.kr/en/article/2021051410194491038).
   - Lesson: it grew out of a supplier's real expertise (DAC poles go into The North Face, MSR and Hilleberg gear), and selling parts makes repair routine.
   - Image: already on the site as /images/bags/helinox-chair-one.jpg

9. **WonderFold** (wonderfold.com): the local one, Irwindale, CA
   - Founded 2019 by Sabrina Ng and Kevin Xia.
   - System: one wagon chassis that takes add-on canopies, seats, table tops and bundles. The S5 table top is rated to 66 lb.
   - Prices: S5 Convertible Utility Wagon $329, S5 canopy $159, W4 Luxe Pro stroller wagon $899.
   - Number: sold in 10 international markets (spielwarenmesse.de).
   - Lesson: a beach and utility wagon grew into a stroller category, with bundles and licensed collaborations (Van Gogh Museum, Porsche at $1,999).
   - Image: https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0429/5776/6810/files/240726-S5-BLK-Product-02_6404e255-fed7-460c-8202-f27dce3b7cc6.png

## Lessons for a small local brand

1. Standardize the connector, not the bag. Topologie's carabiner, Tom Bihn's O-ring, the MOLLE grid, Bogg's holes and the IGT unit all keep the interface fixed while the pods change.
2. Offer a cheap way in and collectible add-ons: a $12 adapter, an $8 key strap, a $15 charm, a $5 patch. Local themes suit charms (a charm for each court, for example).
3. Pre-sell before ordering stock. Peak Design still funds launches through Kickstarter. Sewn goods commonly need a few hundred units per colour (not verified).
4. Make repair part of the brand: Peak Design's lifetime warranty, GORUCK's Scars guarantee, Helinox parts, Tom Bihn's leftover-fabric pouches.
5. Tie merch to events, the way GORUCK's $5 patches mark its own rucks. Here that could be a pickleball league night or a beach cleanup.
6. Build on an open standard if you can't own one: MOLLE, MagSafe, a common carabiner size.
