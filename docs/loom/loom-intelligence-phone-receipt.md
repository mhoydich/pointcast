# Reading a Loom receipt on a phone

A visitor following a Baby Bonfire handoff should be able to open the page before they need to know what a PR is. The board can keep the full record behind that first tap. [Loom intelligence design note](https://github.com/mhoydich/pointcast/pull/1796)

**What the phone shows today.** On November 18, I opened the live launch receipt in Chromium at 390 × 844. The first screen showed “Hold the Show HN post” and who worked on intake. I had to swipe the table sideways to read the decision recorded for each part. The table had 356 pixels of visible space for 734 pixels of content. “Approval or next decision” began 568 pixels from the left edge of the screen. The page itself fit, so checking for page overflow alone would miss this. [Launch receipt](https://pointcast.xyz/loom/receipts)

**What to put above the fold.** For a landing page awaiting Mike’s review, use a thumbnail of the actual revision, the line **Baby Bonfire landing page · Awaiting Mike’s review**, and one link labeled **Open the page**. That link opens the revision shown in the thumbnail. Reading the page leaves its approval state alone. After Mike accepts it, replace the status with his name and the date of acceptance.

**What to keep below.** Give each part one short credit with the maker, model if recorded, reviewer, verdict and link to the evidence. Ada’s game handoff shows why the distinction matters. Shwa supplied the six moves, Sid supplied the portraits, and Ada assembled them. The receipt records a successful Chromium phone-width check and leaves physical-phone play open. All three makers keep their credit and the next reviewer knows what to try. [OFF-54 handoff](https://pointcast.xyz/loom?to=%23officenoun-tasks#m_3k3h3p656l)

**Acceptance check.** Hand someone the receipt at 390px. Before scrolling, can they say what was made, who still needs to review it, and which link opens it? Let them tap it and compare the destination with the picture. Then ask them to find who made one part and what is still untested, using only vertical scrolling.
