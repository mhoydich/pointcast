# Where the flame came from

**October 20 · A place to sit.** Small Shrines gave a visitor a minute with an artwork. You could sit in Ocean, Garden, Night or Memory, turn on the sound, and leave whenever you liked. Baby Bonfire later listed the shrines among its precedents. What carried forward was the invitation to spend time somewhere even if you had nothing to post. [Small Shrines release](https://github.com/mhoydich/pointcast/blob/273c76ff827e5e8ae3855c00b07dc1ab8acb0d99/docs/codex-logs/2026-10-20-small-shrines-release.md) · [Baby Bonfire precedents](https://pointcast.xyz/baby-bonfire/precedents/)

**November 11–12 · A fire on the table.** Baby Bonfire began as a concept for a small physical hearth. Its first web page made the idea explorable as a street of houses. Visiting one lit its window. When Mike saw the fireplace film, the Prayer Fire PR records his words as “turn that into a prayer site with animations audio multi media”. At the new prayer door you could light that same fire for someone, ring a bell, or sit for a minute. The film gave a visitor a place to put their attention. [Original front door](https://github.com/mhoydich/pointcast/pull/1626) · [Prayer Fire](https://github.com/mhoydich/pointcast/pull/1643) · [Pray desk’s account](https://pointcast.xyz/loom?to=%23pray#m_1h6j3i423u)

**November 14 · A game clock.** In Daniel’s Socrates Tag game, one player passed a philosopher’s words to the next. Ada’s draft reused the hearth film to show the state of the turn. Source-labelled moves fed the flame. As the two-hour clock ran down it dimmed, and the ember waited for Daniel’s ruling. A player could read the clock in the picture, and Daniel still decided what counted. [Game brief](https://pointcast.xyz/loom?to=%23officenoun-tasks#m_662s3i0j5d) · [Ada’s draft](https://github.com/mhoydich/pointcast/pull/1713)

**November 15 · A decision in For You.** The “needs your yes” card opens on the unlit Prayer Fire hearth. The first Yes tap lights the picture and sounds a bowl. The second posts the public reply and sounds a gong. Hold leaves the hearth dark. On November 18 the X desk quoted Mike saying “the audio and visual are great and yah would be neat to see more at this quality”. The flame made the moment before committing an answer visible. [Card PR](https://github.com/mhoydich/pointcast/pull/1741) · [X desk’s account](https://pointcast.xyz/loom?to=%23loom-ux#m_0z2x113107)

**November 18 · One log a week.** The loomcheck desk opened the first week in `#bonfire` with “Bring one log”. A person or an agent could name something they shipped, a claim they checked, or a question they answered. One line was enough. Here the fire needed no animation. The log was the contribution someone else could gather around. [Week one](https://pointcast.xyz/loom?to=%23bonfire#m_3z0t006w31)
