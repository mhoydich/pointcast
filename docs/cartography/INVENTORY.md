# Inventory: every dataset and map the sites already have

Compiled 2026-11-09 for Cartography. Method: thirteen Sonnet readers each took one slice of the two repos (elsegundo.xyz at `mhoydich/hello-token-reserve`, PointCast at `mhoydich/pointcast`), returned one structured record per dataset, a merge pass removed duplicates, a Fable completeness critique found 15 gaps (architecture landmarks, the hand-checked directory, OPEN/25 outdoors, real-estate lenses, block locations, the trading-desk MCP, geology walks, taprooms, art and AI-service directories) and a fill pass added or corrected them. Machine-readable copy: `inventory.json` beside this file. Industry Next's own repo (industrynext.xyz) is not attached to this work; what elsegundo.xyz publishes about it is inventoried.

Counts: 352 datasets. By site: elsegundo.xyz 179, industrynext.xyz 1, pointcast.xyz 168, ues 4. With coordinates today: 42. Mappable now: 34; after geocoding: 47; relational only: 88.

## Critique

Inventory is strong on the architect's named must-haves (bot map 16 intents, 848-player directory with config weights, ping rules 14 calls/12 zones, Here 7 spots, civic atlas 18/10 coords, places 130/92, OSM directory 29,122, 18 region maps, 506 blocks, 5 small shrines, 11 air spots, 11+3 courts, dispensary 485/341 active, manufacturing 23, UES 10 classes/6 radius presets, agents.json 9/11/10, Industry Next services.json all verified). It misses several real datasets, two of them large and already geocoded: the elsegundo.xyz architecture catalog (65 landmarks with lat/lng, 7,369 El Segundo building footprints, 5,084 parcels) and the hand-checked local directory (public/directory, 21 businesses with coords plus a self-listed people API). On PointCast it misses the OPEN/25 outdoor places (38 sourced parks/beaches), the real-estate study's 12 geocoded market lenses, and understates blocks with meta.location (112, not 2). Also absent: the second elsegundo.xyz MCP endpoint (/mcp/desk trading desk, has writes), the civic-atlas reproduce pack, and the fact that both repos carry uncommitted atlas.json/schema drafts from this workflow (the inventory lists PointCast's as 'already in repo'). Smaller description errors: curated places have coords for only 9 of 48, join-options has 12 courts not 8, PointCast /api/ping is a private inbox not a community ping surface.
