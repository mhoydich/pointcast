# Collect v1 — Roadster acquisition notebook

## Scope

An editorial acquisition-goal hub at `/collect`, with the new Tesla Roadster as item 001. Public price is TBD, funding unverified, and no reservation or purchase confirmed. The local notebook separates prospective leads, realized unallocated resources and explicitly allocated owner-attested records. It is not an account balance, a financial sync or a shared ledger.

Entries stay in browser storage; exports/imports are versioned JSON. Evidence and explicit confirmation are required before an allocation can be recorded. Invalid/corrupt data is never overwritten, and stale tabs preserve the newer saved draft while offering export of session-only edits. No external account integration, seller contact, subscription, payment or purchase is added.
