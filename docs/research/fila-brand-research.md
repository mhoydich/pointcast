## Precision notes for copy and captions

- **Do not merge 1973 and 1974.** Current corporate history gives 1973 for the first sportswear collection and F-BOX. Current museum pages repeatedly give 1974 for White Line. Use those distinct milestones; some secondary histories conflate them.
- **Borg's titles:** Wimbledon singles 1976, 1977, 1978, 1979, 1980. French Open singles 1974, 1975, 1978, 1979, 1980, 1981. Do not imply all six French titles occurred in the 1976–1980 window.
- **Seles context:** Three French Open singles titles (1990–1992), four Australian Open singles titles (1991–1993 and 1996), two US Open singles titles (1991–1992). The museum supports her role in FILA's colorful late-80s/early-90s visual history; avoid inventing an exact first contract year.
- **Ownership:** “Italian-born, globally stewarded from Korea” is a useful editorial shorthand. ANTA's regional business is not evidence of ANTA owning FILA globally. Misto's history separately lists Korea establishment in 1991, Korea management buyout in 2005, and global rights acquisition in 2007.
- **2018 revival:** The award supports renewed sneaker visibility. It does not by itself support invented sales totals, an exact date for the Disruptor's original release, or a claim that all markets shared one revival.
- **2024 campaign date:** Archive header is July 11; release body says collection available July 10. Use “July 2024” in timeline copy.
- **Current retail:** On 2026-10-02, [fila.com](https://www.fila.com/) displayed a paused-shop message. Do not promise US shopping availability or infer that every regional FILA business is paused.
- **Museum:** [The foundation](https://www.fondazionefila.com/chi-siamo/) dates its establishment to 2010. Its present museum is the [FILA Brand Experience Center](https://www.fondazionefila.com/museo/) at Via C. Cavour 17B, Biella, in the historic FILA headquarters.

## Editorial lessons for RALLY

Interpretation, not sourced corporate claims: an enduring brand can carry a small visual vocabulary across very different uses; athlete movement can guide garment proportions; an archive can be a starting point for new work rather than a bank of graphics to copy; geographic origin and present ownership deserve separate sentences. Use RALLY's own marks, cuts, artwork, and stories.
