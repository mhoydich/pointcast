## Empirical and care checks

- [AWARE II primary abstract in UT Health San Antonio’s author record](https://scholars.uthscsa.edu/en/publications/awareness-during-resuscitation-ii-a-multi-center-study-of-conscio/): confirms 567 in-hospital arrests, 53 survivors, 28 interviews and 11 interviewees with reported memories/perceptions. The manuscript keeps these selected denominators and locates the inquiry during resuscitation. Survivor memories are not evidence of personal consciousness after established irreversible death. Abstract access is sufficient for the restricted claims; full-journal access was blocked for the research author.
- [AAN/AAP/CNS/SCCM institutional guidance summary](https://www.aan.com/PressRoom/Home/PressRelease/5119), 2023-10-11: supports permanent cessation of brain function after catastrophic injury, trained assessment, and distinction from coma. The manuscript avoids an individual determination and retains the US clinical/legal setting.
- [Xu et al., PNAS original study](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2216268120), online 2023-05-01: four comatose dying patients, increased gamma activity/connectivity in two after withdrawal of ventilatory support. The Discussion explicitly states the activity does not demonstrate subjective experience and none survived to report it. The text accurately preserves those limits.
- [NASA carbon cycle](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/the-carbon-cycle/): plant/plankton decomposition supports material cycling, not persistence of personal awareness. Original 2011-06-16 and site-update 2026-02-23 are distinguished.
- [WHO palliative care](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/palliative-care), 2020-08-05; [NHS end-of-life guidance](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/end-of-life-care/what-it-involves-and-when-it-starts/), reviewed 2026-05-06; [NCI cancer-specific care](https://www.cancer.gov/about-cancer/advanced-cancer/care-choices/care-fact-sheet): support multidimensional care, wishes, varying needs and uncertain trajectories. Historical WHO numerical estimates and clinical management instructions are not reproduced.
- [NHS advance-statement page](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/end-of-life-care/planning-ahead/advance-statement/), reviewed 2023-09-19: distinguishes wishes from advance decisions. Its listed next-review date had passed; the manuscript responsibly identifies that and derives no current legal-validity conclusion.
