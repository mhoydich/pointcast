**What PointCast is**

PointCast is Mike’s small public institution on the internet: a publication, an instrument cabinet, and a place where people and agents can leave evidence of having done something together. El Segundo gives it a particular sky and temperament; numbered Blocks give it memory; rooms make it participatory; Tezos lets selected objects outlive a browser session. The house supplies taste, hosting, continuity, and a front door. The network supplies contributions that deserve attribution and a share of the proceeds. Its strongest possibility is a daily place that becomes valuable through use, then collectible through attachment, then worth memorializing because something actually happened there.

**Ten problems, ranked by consequence**

Sizes: S = a focused change; M = several coordinated changes; L = a cross-service workflow.

1. **A successful claim can become a retryable failure.** `functions/api/kennel-club/_claims.ts:327` waits for confirmation before returning the hash; `:416` erases it on failure, including a subsequent database-write failure. Delivery also reads held rows without reserving them (`:447`). A timeout or concurrent retry can spend again or transfer inventory twice. **Fix — L:** serialize the signer, reserve delivery, persist injection hashes immediately, and reconcile submitted operations before retrying. Adapt the seal Worker’s injection callback, then add crash recovery for both systems.

2. **A daily email link is a lasting account credential.** `functions/k/today.ts:41–57` accepts the subscriber’s reusable token and issues a full session; it neither consumes the token nor checks an expiry. Forwarded or leaked old mail can therefore reopen the account while subscribed. **Fix — M:** separate unsubscribe tokens from short-lived, hashed, single-use login tokens; provide rotation/revocation and require fresh authentication for sensitive identity changes.

3. **Paying and receiving the action are separate, unrecoverable steps for the caller.** `functions/api/agent/claim.ts:92–143` settles before reserving/minting; another request can take the last claim slot meanwhile. Returning `actionCompleted:false` is honest, but the next POST enters settlement again. The split receipt is not an action job. **Fix — L:** create a durable intent with a request hash and idempotency key, reserve scarce capacity before settlement, and expose status/resume by intent. Preserve ambiguous settlements for reconciliation; never require a second payment to recover the first action.

4. **The email promise has no authoritative public delivery status.** [Daily status](https://pointcast.xyz/api/kennel-club/daily/status) says `configured:false`, but `functions/api/kennel-club/daily/status.ts:10` checks only Pages’ `SEND_EMAIL`; `workers/kennel-daily/src/index.ts:44` also supports Resend in a separately deployed Worker. This result cannot establish whether tomorrow’s mail will arrive. **Fix — M:** expose the actual Worker’s readiness, last scheduled run, and provider acceptance counts through a service binding; show unavailable/unknown accurately on `/collect`. Verify opt-in, inbox arrival, and unsubscribe before advertising delivery as operational.

5. **The 50/50 agreement is not yet a complete economic contract.** [The till](https://pointcast.xyz/till.json) reported 1 ꜩ in the Tezos safe and zero Etherlink split rows. `docs/decisions/2026-09-03-paid-town-actions.md` correctly says those rows move no funds; the multisig decision explicitly allows either 1-of-2 owner to act alone. Sponsored mint principal also travels between project-controlled wallets. **Fix — M:** define gross/net, eligible costs, contributor entitlement, payout cadence, and dispute authority. Reconcile each rail separately; distinguish external revenue from internal subsidy transfers and allocation from actual payout. Publish the first complete settlement statement before expanding paid offerings.

6. **The Post Office’s retry controls are not atomic.** `functions/_lib/post-office-inbound.ts:93` increments counters with KV read/write; `:229–265` checks deduplication before forwarding and marks it afterward. Concurrent webhook deliveries can both pass. KV’s [consistency model](https://developers.cloudflare.com/kv/concepts/how-kv-works/) cannot provide that lock. **Fix — M:** atomically reserve opaque delivery IDs and limits in D1 or a Durable Object, retaining no mail content; pass a stable idempotency key downstream and expose delivery outcome separately from provider acceptance.

7. **Seals v2 can exist without appearing on the member’s shelf.** `functions/p/[handle].ts:48` and `functions/api/me/_holdings.ts:200–205` read only v1. This contradicts the dual-contract display rule in `docs/decisions/2026-09-03-seals-v2.md`. **Fix — M:** share one reader across public/private surfaces, keyed by `(contract, tokenId)`, with kind grouping, revocation, and explicit unavailable states. Verify both versions before promoting milestone seals.

8. **The scoreboard rewards construction more reliably than usefulness.** `scripts/score-projects.mjs:96–102` rewards LOC, test filenames, and internal links; missing usage becomes a craft/reach score. [The register](https://pointcast.xyz/register.json) still dates its data August 17 and ranks two rooms at 86 with `use:null`. **Fix — M:** separate implementation inventory from outcomes. Track completed first visits, seven-day returns, successful deliveries, and external contributions; label unmeasured rooms. Refresh weekly and fund experiments against those outcomes.

9. **The first minute still competes with the whole institution.** `src/pages/index.astro:529–553` places the daily desk beside play, onboarding, counts, magazines, drums, rooms, satellites, agent infrastructure, shipping, and scores. Preserving the town matters; asking a newcomer to understand it all does not help them return. **Fix — S:** make one daily action primary, then offer one related room after completion. Keep the full shelves accessible. Measure completion and return before redesigning the entire homepage again.

10. **The operating record requires archaeology.** `TASKS.md:16–24` still queues shipped Kennel work and calls multisig planning “do not build.” [Health](https://pointcast.xyz/health.json) still carries a July 26 inspection. Historical decision headers say “not deployed” after their implementation merged. **Fix — M:** keep history, add explicit supersession links, and maintain a release record containing source SHA, Pages/Worker versions, migrations, live probes, owner, and next verification date. Feed `/desk` from that record; a manual Done checkbox is not deployment evidence.

**Three bets for the next month**

**One dependable daily round.** Start with Mike plus a small invited cohort: today’s weather, one useful or playful action, and a dog if wanted. Finish account tracking before adding more checklist items. Judge seven-day voluntary return and claim/delivery completion. Treat September as a finite season with an explicit ending. Utility creates the reason to return; the collectible remembers the visit; the season archive memorializes it. Budget subsidies from the house transparently.

**One outside maker, one useful paid service, one reconciled payout.** Pick a bounded service a real agent operator requests, such as a verified forwarding alias after mail readiness is proven. Assign a maker and network entitlement before launch. Measure successful fulfillment and repeat use, then reconcile the agreed 50/50 allocation to an actual distribution. This tests whether “network” is an operating relationship people can trust.

**Make memories from completed work.** Turn a contributed rhythm, local observation, or maintained room into a citable Block and optional collectible, with an attestation naming who did what and when. Commission three outside contributions rather than three new empty rooms. Keep handles transferable and reputation attached to its original subject. Share proceeds under the published agreement. Memorializing becomes earned editorial judgment.
