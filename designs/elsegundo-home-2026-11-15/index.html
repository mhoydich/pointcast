Two persimmons, two ways to eat

Squat Fuyus eat firm, like an apple. Tall Hachiyas wait until nearly mushy.

Read the note

At November markets, look at the shape before you buy a persimmon. Flat, squat Fuyus can be eaten firm, like an apple, or slightly soft. The taller, acorn-shaped Hachiya is bitter until it is fully ripe and nearly mushy, so wait for that soft stage before you bite in. Ask the grower which kind is in the box.

Source: UC ANR Food Blog, Preserving persimmons (Oct 6, 2020) · fact · checked Nov 12

🌊

King tides arrive November 24

The winter's highest tides: Nov 24 to 26, Dec 23 to 25, Jan 21 to 22.

Read the note

California's King Tides Project lists the winter's highest tides statewide as November 24 to 26, December 23 to 25 and January 21 to 22. Exact local dates and times differ, so use the project's tide map for the South Bay coast. The project asks people to look for the high water only where they can do so safely.

Source: California Coastal Commission, California King Tides Project · fact · checked Nov 12

🧄

Garlic and onions go in now

Plant for a spring harvest, somewhere that stays dry in May and June.

Read the note

UC Cooperative Extension's November guide for Los Angeles County lists garlic, shallots and bulb onions among the crops to sow or transplant now for a spring harvest. Its advice is to plant them where they will not be watered in May and June, so the bulbs can dry out before a late June or July harvest.

Source: UC Cooperative Extension Los Angeles County, November Gardening Tips · fact · checked Nov 12

🦋

Seacliff buckwheat and the El Segundo blue

The dune plant the endangered butterfly needs. Look, and leave it in place.

Read the note

Cabrillo Marine Aquarium's species page describes seacliff buckwheat as a plant of coastal bluffs and sand dunes, from one to about three feet tall, found from central to Southern California. It calls the plant important to pollinators, including the endangered El Segundo blue butterfly. Look for it along dune and bluff edges, and leave it in place.

Source: Cabrillo Marine Aquarium, Seacliff Buckwheat · listing · checked Nov 12

🍷

Pomegranate molasses from four cups of juice

Slow work: two to three hours at a gentle simmer makes about a cup.

Read the note

A UC Master Food Preserver recipe turns four cups of pure pomegranate juice, a third of a cup of sugar and some lemon juice into about a cup of molasses. It is slow work: the juice simmers very gently for two to three hours until it coats a spoon. The finished syrup is stored in the refrigerator.

Source: UC Master Food Preserver Program (Jan 6, 2025) · fact · checked Nov 12

Sunday, 12:30 to 2 p.m., in San Pedro. Check sign-up before you go.

Read the note

Cabrillo Marine Aquarium in San Pedro lists a Tidepool Walk on Sunday, November 22, 2026, from 12:30 to 2 p.m. The same page gives weekend hours of 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. and says the aquarium is closed on Thanksgiving. Check the events calendar for sign-up details and any fees before you go.

Source: Cabrillo Marine Aquarium, home page · listing · checked Nov 12
