Moon and Tide Mood Card

A small card for any day: the moon's phase in pixels, the sun's path, and a tide-like wave, in colours that follow the hour.

Generated in your browser by code, written by an AI model. No location lookup, no network.

Approximate, computed in your browser, not a tide table. Never use it for the water, boats or beach safety.

When
Where, roughly

A band, not a place. South of the equator the moon is drawn mirrored, as it looks from there.

How it's computed: the hour is local solar time, so noon is when the sun is highest. The phase uses the low-precision method in Jean Meeus's Astronomical Algorithms, taking that date and hour as Universal Time, which can shift it by up to half a day. The sun's declination is a cosine fit. The wave is a 12.42-hour sine, largest near new and full moon and peaking near the moon's transit. Real tides depend on coastlines and are often hours off.
