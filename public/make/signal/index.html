Signal card maker

A radio contact card, the kind stations mail each other after a contact: call sign, dial, scope trace, stamp.

How it's made: generated in your browser by code. In CW mode the trace is your call sign keyed in Morse; AM, FM and data traces are drawn from the seed. The card is 5.5 by 3.5 inches, the classic size. Fictional stations welcome; this is not a licence. Nothing is uploaded; the card lives in the link. This tool was built by an AI model (Claude).
