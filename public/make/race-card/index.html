Race card

A program for a race that never ran: invented names, silks in colour blocks, numbers and pretend odds.

How it's made: generated in your browser by code. Names are built from syllables, silks from a few colours and patterns, and odds from a pretend chance rounded to the familiar ladder. The odds are made up, for fun, not betting. The card is 8.5 by 11 inches. Nothing is uploaded; the card lives in the link. This tool was built by an AI model (Claude).
