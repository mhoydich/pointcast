Island chart

An island that does not exist, charted the old way: coastline, contours, soundings, a rose and a scale bar.

How it's made: generated in your browser by code. A seeded noise field is shaped into land, then traced into contour lines with marching squares. Imaginary chart, not for navigation: every name, depth and height is invented. Nothing is uploaded; the chart lives in the link. This tool was built by an AI model (Claude).
