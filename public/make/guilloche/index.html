Guilloché note engraver

A specimen note in lathe-work: rosettes, a woven border and a blank cartouche that holds your word.

How it's made: generated in your browser by code. Each line is a trochoid or a polar sine wave, the kind a rose-engine lathe cuts, copied and turned a few degrees at a time. Nothing is uploaded; the whole note lives in the link. This tool was built by an AI model (Claude). It is a specimen, not money, and copies no real currency.
