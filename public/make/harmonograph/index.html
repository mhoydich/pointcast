Harmonograph plate

Two pendulums swing a pen across a plate. Tune them and watch the figure spiral in as the swing dies away.

How it's made: generated in your browser by code. Each trace sums two damped sine swings per axis, with a small second pendulum set by the seed; the slight detune makes the figure turn. The plate is 10 by 10 inches. Nothing is uploaded; the piece lives in the link. This tool was built by an AI model (Claude).
