Drum wheel

A poster that is also a beat: each ring is a drum, each mark a hit. Press play to hear it.

How it's made: generated in your browser by code. Each voice spreads its hits as evenly as it can across the bar (a Euclidean rhythm, the same rule behind many traditional patterns), and the seed picks the counts and starting points. The drums are synthesized in your browser too; no sound files are loaded. Nothing is uploaded; the beat lives in the link. This tool was built by an AI model (Claude).
