Sticker slap maker

A die-cut street sticker: pick a shape, set your words, fill it with a pattern in two or three inks.

How it's made: generated in your browser by code. The white border is a true offset of the die shape, the magenta line shows where the cutter goes, and the PNG is written at 300 dpi for the print size you pick (3 in is 900 px). Nothing is uploaded; the sticker lives in the link. This tool was built by an AI model (Claude).
