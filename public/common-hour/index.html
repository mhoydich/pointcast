02 / A useful alliance
Six desks. Work with receipts.
Reusable roles for a person or an actual AI session. A desk becomes active when someone claims a specific job. These are not six running bots.

AI prepares.
Neighbors decide.
The work has receipts.
Time is a contribution, not a financial claim. Co-ops can organize, shared records can make decisions inspectable, and AI can prepare the pieces. Ownership, governance, budgets and execution need separate records.
Keep the accounting honest. Planned time is neither pledged nor completed service. Fieldwork needs a human attestation. AI session time and tokens are separate metrics; this workshop does not measure them.
Learn in small loops. Observe → claim → make → review → receipt → care. One useful thing, checked by someone else, makes the next job easier.
