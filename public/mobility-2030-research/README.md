# Hoydich 2030 mobility and manufacturing research pack

Research date: 2 October 2026

This is a sourced concept-planning handoff for a proposed civil mobility venture. “Manufactured in El Segundo” is a vision, not evidence of an existing factory, approved aircraft, supplier relationship, funding or local-content percentage.

## Contents

- `manufacturing-directory.json`: 23 organization records with public addresses, capabilities, explicit outside-work status, sources and caveats
- `manufacturing-directory.csv`: flat reusable form of the same research data
- `manufacturing-atlas-brief.md`: work-package shortlist, directory and supplier-qualification checklist
- `mobility-regulatory.json`: structured concept comparison, evidence gates and regulatory references
- `mobility-regulatory.md`: readable U.S./California baseline, system boundaries and illustrative funding stages

## Mapping and boundary caution

Public addresses are map-ready search inputs. Coordinates are intentionally null. Distances are coarse editorial straight-line estimates from El Segundo City Hall, not geocoded measurements or driving distances. The furthest entry, CSULB, is estimated at 22 miles and must be treated as **boundary-check required** before relying on strict 25-mile inclusion. Verify all geocodes before drawing a precise radius map. Until then, use a regional schematic or city-based directory rather than precise plotted pins. Do not infer internal facility layouts or visitor access from an address.

## What the evidence does and does not establish

The sources substantiate publicly advertised capabilities, not audited capacity, current certificate validity, quote prices, supplier willingness to work with Hoydich or aviation suitability. “Yes” for outside work means a public customer product/service path exists only in the described scope. OEM presence is not automatically contract-shop access. University research is not automatically an open fabrication service.

Strong regional leads: precision machining, metal forming, electronics assembly, additive manufacture, nondestructive inspection and robotics. This pack does not establish an approved propulsion/battery/flight-control integrator, test site, certification team, vertiport, manufacturing commitment or flight performance.

## Presentation rules for the concept

- Label all concept graphics “2030 vision / conceptual architecture / not flight-certified engineering”
- Preserve ground-first as the base-case path; treat flight as a separately funded option with go/no-go gates
- Distinguish powered-lift from generic eVTOL and wingless multicopter
- Preserve the 2026 MOSAIC branch and the limitation that the accepted standard in the cited notice covered powered-lift, not multicopters at that time
- Do not turn a conceptual interface diagram into build-ready aerodynamic, structural, electrical or flight-control instructions
- No range, payload, speed, noise, battery endurance, price, certification date or investor return is demonstrated by this research
- Keep public fundraising material at the level of illustrative R&D stages, without securities terms, funds collection or an investment CTA unless separately reviewed and authorized

## Suggested next decision

Choose the first civil ground-use case and operating environment. Commission qualified classification and safety review, then request scoped nonflight prototype quotes from appropriate suppliers. Obtain project-specific regulatory advice before committing to any aviation configuration, site or commercialization date.
