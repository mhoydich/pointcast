## How to use this directory
Distances are coarse, unverified straight-line estimates, not routing or geocoded measurements. All entries are believed within 25 miles; exact boundary inclusion remains to be checked by public-address geocoding. Coordinates intentionally null.

yes = a public external customer product/service pathway exists in the stated scope; no = manufacture is explicitly outside the mission; unknown = not established. None means the organization agreed to this project.

Web-source check on stated date establishes advertised capability, not operational audit, current capacity, certificate validity, commercial acceptance or partnership.

## Qualification checklist before spending
1. Confirm present address, civil-project eligibility, actual process location and available capacity.
2. Request current quality-system certificate and scope, process approvals, traceability, first-article and inspection plans. Do not treat a website logo or AS9100 status as approval of a flight-critical part.
3. Share only authorized civil, non-controlled drawings through the supplier-approved channel; agree IP/data handling and export classification first.
4. Obtain competing written quotes with tooling ownership, lead time, quantities, acceptance criteria and nonconformance responsibilities.
5. Validate coupons and representative nonflight parts before any safety-critical sourcing decision.
6. Keep BOM provenance and configuration control. A directory entry is not a purchase recommendation or endorsement.
