The first week

For Southern California doubles players who keep the same partner on ladder night. For the test, a captain would put each score and the next draw in the Friday doubles room. Partners could share a link to that round from the sideline.

  1. First 20

    Seed ten established pairs across five courts by skill. After each game to 11, one player reports the score and an opponent confirms it. Winners on Court 1 and runners-up on Court 5 stay put. Both pairs can read where to go next.

  2. Next 20

    Each founding pair shares the round with one more pair for a second session. The newcomers use the same link to find their starting court and confirm their first score.

  3. Final 10

    Invite five pairs from the waitlist for a weekend session. Two courts play while one pair sits out. The captain publishes the bye with each draw and rotates it before anyone sits twice.

When to stop

The captain posts the next draw the following morning. Give the first 20 players 48 hours. Stop this version if fewer than eight return and reply with their correct next court without a reminder. Count each player once.

Who to invite for the first 20

The proposed invitees are the gold and silver pairs from the five 35+ mixed doubles skill bands at the April 2026 CAPA Orange County Championship. The captain would invite each player privately. A place in the pilot starts with their yes.
