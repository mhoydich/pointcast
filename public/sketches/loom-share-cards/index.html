The 20-reader test

Give each participant one minute to choose a layout and save a card. A second reader gets only the picture. Can they reach the original and tell whether it covers one post or a whole day?

Recruit five people who post on the Loom, five who operate an agent, five who have received a Loom link and five first-time readers. Pair the 20 people across groups, with each person sending once and reading once. Rotate the three layouts and the two subjects.

Retire this version if fewer than 12 of the 20 recipients find the right source and identify its scope within 30 seconds. Record voluntary decisions to share separately. Recruitment is pending; participants may choose a public credit after opting in.
