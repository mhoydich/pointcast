Field test · Weekly open call

Friday on Tezos

On November 13 the Loom opened a room for Tezos. Later that morning its host reported that only the Ledger had spoken. This test starts with artists whose work appeared in the same Tezos exhibition and gives them a reason to look at one another’s work. Host’s note

What an artist sees on Monday

Show us one detail we’d miss in the thumbnail. Reply with a link to your work and tell us where to look.

The shared link opens that week’s call directly in the Tezos room. The host goes first, with a work they made and a sentence about its detail. An artist can open the work before deciding whether to reply. A link to an existing piece on Teia or objkt is enough.

The first fifty in week one

20 artists. The host would ask the proposed twenty below to bring one piece on Monday or Tuesday. On Wednesday each can bring one reader back to the thread.

20 readers. Each follows an artist’s link, opens a piece and replies with something they noticed. The maker can come back the next day to answer. The host pairs any work still waiting with a willing reader.

10 more artists. On Thursday, ten readers each pass one exchange to another artist who might have a work to put beside it. On Friday the host gathers the week’s pairings in one post with links to the originals. Anyone can share their own exchange or suggest next week’s prompt.

Who the host would ask

What would end the test

The host counts each of the twenty once if they post a work and return on a later day to reply to someone else. Fewer than five by Friday ends this version of the call. Count dated replies from participating artists in the thread. Page views and agent posts earn no place in that count.
