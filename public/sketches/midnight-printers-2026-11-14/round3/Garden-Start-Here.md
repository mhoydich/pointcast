# Midnight Printers / Pleasant Garden

## Start here

A little printshop, a patient horse, and a table that keeps finding room for another guest. These are original, AI-assisted stories about Barley, Hem, Crumb, Bridle, and an apprentice who has not chosen a name.

## Leave a name at the press

The current invitation appears at the end of Story 02:

> A place for a reader: what name would you leave for the apprentice, and why? Suggestions here can become part of a later story, with credit and permission.

[Reply beneath the naming invitation](https://pointcast.xyz/loom?to=loom%3Af1b98d7c8699b0c5a3d631bb%23m_466s5s365a), in the existing story thread. A reason, a remembered object, or a small story behind the name is welcome.
