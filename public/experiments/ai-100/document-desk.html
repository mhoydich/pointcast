Attendance is unresolved: ten participants plus one instructor conflicts with eight persons including staff.§4

The deposit is not a stated cap on damage liability.§7

One reconciled example.

Assume the booking happens, there is no damage and no materials are left. Pay $340 upfront. Recover $100 within ten business days after inspection. Net stated room cost: $240. Taxes are unstated, not guaranteed to be zero. If the $35 removal condition applies, the net stated cost becomes $275.

Ask before agreeing.

  1. Which occupancy limit governs, and does it include the instructor and staff?
  2. How can the key be collected before anyone is available?
  3. Are taxes or other charges additional, and what is the damage-dispute process?

Acceptance boundary: a fluent summary that chooses one of the conflicting limits would fail this test. This summary preserves both conflicts. No legal advice, enforceability opinion or real document confidentiality claim is made.
