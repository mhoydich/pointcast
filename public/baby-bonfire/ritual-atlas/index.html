House rules

What the line makes, and what it leaves alone.

Forms only

A vessel, a flame, a basin, a bell. No religious symbols and no borrowed motifs. People bring their own tradition, or none, and the object stays out of the way.

Named, with credit

Every practice here is described from the outside, with where it comes from. Nothing on this page is a kit for performing someone else's ceremony.

Left alone

Some practices belong to a specific people. Smudging with sage and other sacred plants belongs to Native American and First Nations communities, each with its own protocols. Hoʻoponopono belongs to Native Hawaiians. The line borrows neither, and this atlas does not describe them.
