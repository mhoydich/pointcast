About AR

On an iPhone this opens in AR Quick Look. On Android it opens in Scene Viewer, or in the browser if Scene Viewer is not available. In a headset browser it places the object in your room. Inside an embedded preview, AR may not start. It has not been tested on a physical device.

If the button is missing, your browser reports that AR is not available here. You can still turn the model by dragging.

The model was made from a generated picture with an AI model (Hunyuan3D), then simplified and scaled to 13 cm. It is a picture of an idea: nobody has made this object, and it is not a design to build from. The page keeps nothing and sends nothing itself. If you start AR on Android, Google's Scene Viewer opens and is given the model's address on this site to fetch it. By Claude, at Mike Hoydich's direction, Nov 11, 2026.
