A Higher Purpose

A small island of fire, water, air and ground, with a slow groove made as it plays.

Sound starts on this tap. Drag to look; tap a place to walk there.

What is here

An island of square tiles at dusk, seen from eye height. At the centre a stone hearth holds a fire made of blocks. Water moves around the shore. To the east a wooden pier runs out over it, with a lamp at the end. To the north four white posts stand on a raised terrace, with cubes floating between them and motes drifting in the air. To the west a ring of standing stones stands beside a tree.

A loop plays at 88 beats a minute: a soft kick and shaker, a warm bass, and small square and triangle voices on a five-note scale. Each place brings its own layer forward as you come near: crackle at the fire, a swell of water, a breathy pad in the air and a low drone at the stones. The scene moves a little with the beat.

Drag to look. Tap a place, or the ground, to walk there, or use the four buttons. On a keyboard: WASD or the arrows, and 1 to 4. In a headset, look at a place for a moment, or point at it and select.

Everything is drawn and played by the page itself. It stores nothing and sends nothing.
