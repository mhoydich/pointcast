    How the creatures decide
    • Finches come to the window feeder at first light, if there is seed in it.
    • The squirrel comes late in the morning when the feeder is full and nobody is near. With the dog in the yard it waits on the fence.
    • A wren takes the water dish around midday, if there is water and the yard is quiet.
    • The dog follows the ball outdoors by day. Indoors, it settles at its spot once the hearth is lit and covered.
    • The cat takes the high shelf in the morning sun, and again in the evening when the hearth is lit. Whatever sits on that shelf goes to the floor.
    • An owl lands on the fence perch after dark. A raccoon tries the porch step late, if seed or water was left outside.
    • The bell rings when its place feels a landing.

    Common sense, not advice: a covered flame, a stable base, never left alone with animals around.
