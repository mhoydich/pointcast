        How the home knows

        A place is a tile or spot with a tap tag and a small swappable puck that senses heat, light and air. No cameras, no microphones, and it works unplugged. A piece is any tagged object, and carrying pieces from place to place is the ritual.

        Fire is the switch. The mantel place only notices a flame someone lit by hand; nothing lights one remotely.

        Four turn days come from the sky and two are sensed at home, so first frost and the first warm night land on a different date every year.

        A release is this sketch's word for a season's set of ideas. Nothing here is for sale and nothing ships. The sun's height follows from the latitude shown; the temperatures and the nights with a flame are illustrative.
