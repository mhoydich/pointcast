The idea

The objects are pieces. Small tiles or spots around a home are places. A place notices which piece has landed on it by a tag on the piece, plus heat and light. Never cameras, never microphones.

A lit flame is a condition met, and the room responds.

Pieces roam the house. Carrying one from room to room is part of the ritual. Any object someone loves can be tagged as a piece, so there is no single look.

Linked places let one home's light show in another: a morning light from a parent who lives alone, or a chain of beacons across the homes of one family.

The word for all of it is presence.

Four screens, for now

Everything above is about objects, not screens. These four are screens. They stand in for things nobody has made yet.

  • Film and sound

    Prayer Fire

    Light it, say what it is for, give the words to the flame, and sit for a minute. A generated film of a small fireplace, with a fire, a bowl and a gong.

  • 3D and sound

    A Higher Purpose

    A small island of square tiles with a fire, water, air and ground. Walk to each and its sound comes forward over a slow groove. Works on a phone, a desktop or in a headset.

  • 3D and VR

    The Room

    A small alcove with the fireplace lit on a table. Look around on a phone, or stand in it with a headset. Press and hold the fire to blow on it.

  • 3D model and AR

    On Your Table

    The fireplace as a model to turn, 13 cm tall. A phone that supports AR can stand it on a real table.

The workshop

Mike Hoydich asked for this to be worked on in the open: open files, trade between makers, and networked objects that each owner can switch on or off. These four pages are where that starts. All of them are drafts.

  • Draft v0

    Open spec

    What a place is, what a piece is, the four signals, the rules, and a list of what is still undecided.

  • Notes

    Open mesh

    How places could talk inside one home and between homes. Opt-in per object, no account.

  • Static v0

    Barter board

    “I make the logs, you supply the hearth.” Makers trading parts. No payments, no prices.

  • How to join

    Make a piece

    Send a scene, a piece idea, a file for a printer or laser, a test result or a ritual.

Mike's earlier work on this

Rooms already on PointCast that circle the same ground. Each is a web page, not an object.

  • The Wild: a field of illustrated spirits with altars, where a candle lit for someone stays on a wall for a day, a week or a month. That candle is paid. Nothing on these pages is.
  • Small Shrines: five art-led places to pause for a minute, with optional quiet sound.
  • Drum altars: five altars that change each week. Ring one and its candle lights.
  • The bell garden and Bell Choir: twelve chimes you play by dragging a hand across them, alone or with whoever else is in the room.
  • Prayer Candles: a shared wall where a votive burns for 24 real hours.
  • Elemental Shrine: a quiet altar of stone, light, fire and water.
  • Room Weather: ambient states for PointCast's own rooms, drawn from what is happening on the site.
  • Home Cartography: a concept for a private, owner-held index of what is in a home. It works by scanning. Baby Bonfire places do not scan anything.
