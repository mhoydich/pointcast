The Baby Bonfire room

A small alcove with the fireplace lit. Drag to look around. In a headset, press Enter VR: you start standing 1.2 m from the fire and can step to a marked spot 0.8 m away. Press and hold select on the fire to blow on it. Three doors lead out.

Concept sketch. Nothing is for sale. Do not burn anything indoors on the strength of these pages.

Sound starts only when you press Sound. This page keeps nothing and sends nothing. It was checked in a WebXR emulator only; nobody has tried it in a headset yet.

The fireplace model and the fire sound were generated with AI tools. The room and the page are by Claude, at Mike Hoydich's direction, Nov 11, 2026.
