Barter board

“I make the logs, you supply the hearth.” Nobody has to make the whole thing. Makers trade the parts.

Every card below is an example. They were written to show what a card looks like. None of them is a real person or a real offer, and nothing on this board exists yet as a made object.

No payments and no prices. This board is for trades. Anything involving a real flame has had no safety testing.

How it works for now

This first version is a plain page. To put up a real card, write to hello@pointcast.xyz with the subject “Baby Bonfire barter board”, or open an issue on the GitHub repo. Say what you can make, what you would like in return, and roughly where you are. Real cards will be added by hand and marked as real.

PointCast has a Want Ads board at /shop/wants. It was not reused here because it is built for buying: wants carry a dollar budget and offers carry a price and a link to a shop. A barter board needs neither.

Example offers

  • ExampleLogs and fuel

    Pressed logs, thumb-size

    Has: a press and sawdust from a wood shop.

    Would trade for: a hearth to burn them in, and an honest report on how they burn.

  • ExampleHearths

    Small clay hearths with lids

    Has: a wheel and clay. Can throw a dozen.

    Would trade for: kiln time, or stands to sit them on.

  • ExamplePlaces

    Stone tiles, palm-size

    Has: offcuts from a countertop shop, cut and eased at the edges.

    Would trade for: someone to work out the puck seat.

  • ExampleStands

    Bent-wire stands

    Has: a jig and a coil of wire.

    Would trade for: pieces to photograph on them.

  • ExampleScent

    Cedar and citrus peel bundles

    Has: a garden and a drying rack.

    Would trade for: a lidded vessel to try them in.

  • ExamplePrinting time

    Ten hours on a desktop printer

    Has: a printer that sits idle most evenings.

    Would trade for: design files worth printing.

  • ExampleKiln time

    A shelf in a bisque firing

    Has: a kiln that fires monthly, with room to spare.

    Would trade for: a hearth from the batch.

  • ExampleTesting

    Burn notes, written down properly

    Has: a patio, a thermometer, patience.

    Would trade for: things to test. Will publish what happens, good or bad.

  • ExampleDesign files

    A lid that fits three vessel sizes

    Has: a drawing ready for a laser or a printer.

    Would trade for: photos of it made, and corrections.

Example wants

  • ExampleWanted

    Someone who knows tap tags

    To say which kinds survive being near heat. Can trade: tiles.

  • ExampleWanted

    A flameless hearth insert

    So every flame piece has a version with no flame. Can trade: printing time.

Board rules

  • Trades only. No money changes hands through this board and no prices are listed.
  • Say what a thing is made of and whether you have tried it yourself.
  • Nothing here is certified or tested by anyone. A real flame is the responsibility of the person who lights it.
  • A card stays up until its maker says to take it down.
