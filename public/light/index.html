PointCast · Light Companion · v0

A small shared light between people

A circle is a few people who care about each other. Each person has one light. When you light yours, it glows on everyone else's screen in the circle. They see that you lit it, and when. Nothing else is sent.

This is not an emergency or monitoring service. It cannot call for help. A dark window only means a light is not lit.

Your circles on this device

    How it works

    • Tap "Make a circle". You get a page with a long link.
    • Send the link to the people you want in it. Anyone with the link is in. There is no sign-in, no email and no phone number.
    • Each person picks a name and a flame colour.
    • Press and hold your own window to light it. The others see it catch within about 15 seconds. Tap it again to snuff it.
    • A lit light shows the time it was lit. It fades on its own at midnight where you are, or after one hour if you choose that.
    • A small ember at the top shows the page is connected. If the ember is out, the page says it cannot see the circle. It does not show dark windows it cannot vouch for.

    What it is

    A way to say "I'm here" without writing anything. One person lights theirs when they get up. Another lights theirs when they are home. The row of windows is the whole thing.

    What it is not

    • It is not an emergency service, an alarm or a monitor. It cannot call anyone.
    • A dark window means a light is not lit. It does not mean anything else.
    • It has no messages, no feed, no photos and no history.
    • It does not send notifications. You see the lights when you open the page.

    What is kept

    For each circle: the names and colours people chose, whether each light is lit, when it was lit and when it fades. The circle link itself is not stored, only a one-way hash of it. No location is stored and no address is logged by this feature. A circle nobody opens for 90 days is deleted. Anyone in the circle can delete it at any time with "Put out the circle".

    The link is the key. Anyone who has it can light or snuff a light, rename one, or put the circle out. Share it the way you would share a house key.

    On a phone

    Open your circle, then use "Add to Home Screen" in the browser's share menu. It opens straight to your circle like an app.

    Drum together

    Every circle has its own room in the PointCast drum rooms. The "Drum together" button on a circle page opens it.

    For agents

    The page as data: /light.json. With a circle link, the MCP tool light_read at /api/mcp reads which lights are lit. There is no tool that lights or changes anything. The signal format is small and does not depend on how it is carried; it is described in /light.json.
