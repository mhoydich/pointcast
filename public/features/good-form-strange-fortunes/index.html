Strange fortunes.

An empty tennis court. A Porsche in pink coastal light. A flower made of chrome. A fortune measured in cacao. A bright planet with two moons.

A collection directed by Mike Hoydich, following an invitation to keep making, keep varying, and make the images feel collectible. The thread running through it: sophisticated composition with one unexpected thing.

The starting point

A beautiful promise.

The Porsche direction treats the car as an editorial subject: a sculptural silhouette, a flower and a horizon. Each composition gives an automotive idea a distinct atmosphere.

These are independent campaign concepts. Their purpose is to demonstrate an advertising sensibility: a strong image, a short line and a quiet signature.

Good form. Great ideas.

Four worlds

A collection with range.

01 / Court Society

Affluent tennis heritage, ivory paper, club green and disciplined typography. Empty courts and sporting objects carry the atmosphere.

02 / Pop Treasury

Supermarket icons, playful repetition and vivid color. SPAM studies, balm-ad experiments and collectible-money ideas turn familiar objects into graphic subjects.

03 / Dream Garage

Porsche silhouettes, 1970s and 1980s moods, velvet night, concrete, coast and flower. Automotive desire becomes a setting for an idea.

04 / Future Botanica

Chrome petals, lunar magnolias and digital mosaics. Classical craft meets an imagined future without needing a person in the frame.

The latest hundred

What might a fortune look like?

The final 25 Midjourney sets move through Aztec trade-material inspirations, Greek and Roman visual motifs, and Swedish-inspired modern design. Cacao, cloth, metal, grain and laurel supply texture and symbolism; glass, cobalt and yellow strip the idea back to light and geometry.

These are imaginative contemporary studies, not historical reconstructions or spendable currency. Their value here is visual: something worth looking at twice.
