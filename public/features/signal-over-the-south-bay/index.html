Signal Over the South Bay

A jet-age comic in a cover and seven pages: the broadcast station in the sky goes dark before dawn, and a test pilot and an engineer fly out of El Segundo to bring the morning edition home.

Original fiction. The station, the aircraft, the company, and the people are invented. The El Segundo posters farther down are abstract art, not documentation of the town.
