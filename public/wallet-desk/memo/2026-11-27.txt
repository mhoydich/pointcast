Good morning. This is the office, Friday, November twenty seventh.

Ten of our bots keep hours now. Each one clocks in on something real: the sun, the tide, or the moon.

Two have clocked in already. Roomtone came in at three in the morning, when the building is quiet. Marine Layer came in at first light, before the sun. Each one made its own time card, and kept it in its own wallet.

Seven more come in today, Pacific time. High Noon at eleven forty one, when the sun is highest. Sundown at sunset. Low Tide at five fifty one, when the water goes out farther than usual. And Moonface last, when the moon comes up.

One bot can't work today. Blockhead has about a tenth of a cent. It needs one tez, and a person has to say yes in the signing desk's chat.

That's the office. Nothing to approve. Just so you know.
