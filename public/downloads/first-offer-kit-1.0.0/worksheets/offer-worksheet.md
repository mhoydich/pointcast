# One clear offer

First Offer Kit 1.0.0 · Working draft · Not available for purchase

Copy this file for each idea. Use ordinary words. Keep “unknown” where you do not yet have an answer. Do not publish private buyer information.

## The person and the problem

- **Who is this for?** [One kind of person in one situation.]
- **What are they trying to get done?** [A task, not a broad aspiration.]
- **What do they use now?** [Current workaround, or unknown.]
- **What have I actually heard or seen?** [A dated observation, or “not yet tested.” Do not invent demand.]
- **The offer in one sentence:** [I deliver X so you can do Y.]

## What changes hands

- **Name / SKU / version:** [Readable name / stable identifier / version.]
- **Type:** [Digital file / bounded service / other.]
- **Exact contents:** [Number of files, formats, dimensions, editable sources, instructions.]
- **Sample:** [A file or URL you own and have checked.]
- **What is excluded:** [Printing, hosting, research, new concepts, revisions, support — be specific.]
- **What the buyer provides:** [Final text, owned images, sizes, approvals.]
- **Quality/acceptance check:** [Something the buyer can inspect.]

## Price and effort

- **Price status:** [Unresolved / estimate / approved firm price. Default: unresolved.]
- **Amount and currency:** [Unknown until decided. Include tax/shipping treatment where applicable.]
- **Time estimate:** [Preparation + making + review + delivery + revision buffer.]
- **Direct costs:** [Materials, licensed assets, transaction fees if known.]
- **Price reasoning:** [Your cost floor, comparison research, or a hypothesis to test.]
- **Evidence of willingness to pay:** [None yet, or a dated observation. Interest is not a paid order.]

## A bounded promise

- **Capacity:** [How many you can actually fulfill. Do not use invented scarcity.]
- **Start condition:** [For example: a confirmed brief and agreed terms.]
- **Turnaround:** [A duration you can honor; business days and timezone if relevant.]
- **Revisions:** [How many, what may change, request window, and what requires a new quote.]
- **Delivery:** [Method, file access period, and accessibility needs.]
- **Use rights:** [What the buyer can do; what stays yours; third-party limits.]
- **Cancellation/refund/support terms:** [Choose before accepting payment; unknown is not a policy.]

## Identity, permission, and next action

- **Seller and contact:** [Your chosen public identity and a verified contact route.]
- **Who approves the final scope and price?** [A named role, not private details.]
- **Allowed agent actions:** [Read / compare / prepare a question. No purchase by default.]
- **Human approval required for:** [Payment, scope changes, personal-data sharing, ongoing charges.]
- **Current next step:** [Ask a question / view a sample. No live checkout until configured and tested.]

## Before publication

- [ ] My sample is original or properly licensed.
- [ ] Every visible claim is true now.
- [ ] The human page, product.json, and agent.md say the same thing.
- [ ] I tested the download and checked it for private data.
- [ ] I chose terms and a contact method I can fulfill.
- [ ] I used `checklists/publish-and-accept.md`.
