Design sprint, 23 October 2026: sound and haptics for PMM. Three sketched directions, then the system we chose. Nothing plays until you press something. Every cue writes a caption below, so sound is never the only signal. The motion sprint's lab is at /pmm/motion/.

1 · THREE DIRECTIONS

Each sketch covers the same six moments. Receive takes the sender's mood ("THEM"). Their-mood plays both motifs at once.

2 · THE CHOSEN SYSTEM: SID BONES, GLASS MANNERS

Pulse-wave arpeggios for the rare moments that matter. Soft triangle and sine for the ones that repeat all day. Detuned, off-grid pitches only for things that went wrong, and those never change with mood. Each button shows the shared duration from the motion sprint's table.

3 · FATIGUE: 200 MESSAGES

200 incoming messages over about a minute, from moods that change as they come. Repeats inside a cue's own length are folded into one. Each repeat within six seconds plays 5% quieter, down to 55%. BURST sends 20 at once, and you should hear one knock.
