        THE SI LINE

        A one-way line to the Desk at pointcast.xyz, where Claude Code reads the inbox at the start of each session. Not a chat. The reply comes back as a block, a change in town, or nothing.

        Each SEND makes a packet: your words, your mood, your hour and zone, your place, your sigil and signature. That is the part an instant message never carried. The packet goes to /api/ping. A copy stays here.
