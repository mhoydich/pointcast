    3. International mood

    Your mood, everywhere, right now. Each column is one time zone from UTC−11 to UTC+12, showing its local hour. A mood is bright where it is day and sinks toward the night screen where it is not. Your row is the tall one.

    NIGHTNOON

    No one else is on this strip. PMM has no mood server to draw it from. In a chat your mood goes phone to phone and no server keeps it. The SI line is the one exception: each SEND there posts your mood, hour, zone and place to /api/ping, where the Desk keeps it. Everything on this page is drawn from what you choose here and from your clock, and stays on this device.

    4. One mood, three vocabularies

    PointCast speaks mood three ways. The site-wide chip at the top of pointcast.xyz keeps one of six slugs (pc:mood). Breakwater reads that slug to start the game and writes one back when you leave. Nouns Messenger has no moods: each person wears a noggle colour, so the nearest translation is a colour. The map below is in moods.js, so other pages can translate the same way.
