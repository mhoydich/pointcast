PMM — PointCast Mobile Messaging

READY.

1. The idea

Two machines that liked you. The Atari 2600 said press start and gave you a rainbow. The Commodore 64 said READY. and waited. PMM is a messenger with that manner: it waits for you, it says very little, and when it does the words are in uppercase and the stripes are on.

The brand is not a costume on a modern app. The dark blue screen is the chat. The stripes are the only accent. The prompts are the UI copy. If a screen needs more than that, it is probably wrong.

3. Type

No webfont. The system monospace is the bitmap font now: ui-monospace, "SF Mono", Menlo, Consolas. Chrome, prompts, labels and buttons are UPPERCASE with letter-spacing: .08em. What people write to each other stays in their own case. Body copy is 16px so phones never zoom the input.

4. Keys

Buttons are keys: a two-pixel border, a three-pixel hard shadow, and they press down two pixels when tapped.

5. Sigils

Every person is a keypair minted on their phone. The first four bytes of the public key hash pick four PETSCII-style glyphs from a set of sixteen. The sigil is the avatar, the ID, and the trust check: read it aloud to each other, then tap VERIFY.

6. Bubbles

Mine: a dark panel with the stripes down the left edge.

Theirs: the field-2 panel with a light blue border.

Under each bubble, a meta line in 10px uppercase: time, mood, and one of ✓ sent, ✓✓ read, SIGNED, UNVERIFIED. The eight moods, their glyphs and tunes, and the atmosphere line are in the Mood Atlas.

8. Sound and motion

Two square-wave notes: up for send (440 then 660 Hz), down for receive. Off until the first tap, toggle in SET. Motion is stepped, not eased: three frames for a bubble, four for a sheet. Cursors blink at 1 Hz. Reduced-motion turns all of it off.

9. Do and don't

  • Do keep the screen dark blue and the border light blue. That pair is the brand.
  • Do use the rainbow once per screen, as a stripe, never as a wash.
  • Do write errors as the machine would, then say the fix.
  • Don't add a bitmap webfont. The system monospace is the point.
  • Don't round corners. Nothing here is rounded.
  • Don't put a logo on it. The wordmark is the letters and the stripes.
  • Don't say Atari or Commodore in the product. People should name them unprompted.
