PMM motion lab

READY.

A design sprint, October 23, 2026: how PMM moves, and how the two moods in a chat change that. Three directions were sketched for the same six moments, then scored. The chosen system takes the best of them. Every animation here is a plan from motion.js, the module the app will load. Pick a mood for you and one for them, then press any key. Nothing here is sent anywhere.

ME
THEM

1. Three sketches

Each covers connect, send, receive (the sender's mood flavours the entrance), react, and a mood change on either side. Press a key to play it on one phone, or with the next key you press below.

2. The chosen system

A graft. Quiet's colour and restraint for the everyday moments, the brand book's stepped motion for each mood's entrance, Raster's tape border only where a line opens, and Arcade's pixel sparks only for a reaction and a sunny arrival. All sixteen events below, at the shared timings the sound sprint uses.

Mood flavour: how a message arrives

Tap one to receive a message in that mood. With reduced motion on, only the timing stays: tired still arrives late.

3. Emotes

The eight Mood Atlas glyphs as two to four frame loops at six frames a second, made from small pixel moves of each glyph. They are the reactions. Tap one to react with it on the chosen phone. With reduced motion on they hold still on their first frame.

4. Scores

Each criterion 1 to 5, 5 best. Battery and frame cost is measured, not guessed: see the sprint doc for the frame timings behind it.
