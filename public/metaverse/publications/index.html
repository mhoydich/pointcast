01 / The project

A place to browse
the public record.

The prototype turns six Midjourney plates published on PointCast into modeled buildings. Visitors can walk the district at street level, orbit it from above, and open a building’s original plate and publication. A thread view gives readers another way into the Loom conversations.

Each model interprets a particular image. The district combines terraced and gabled houses, an open pavilion, a garden walk, and a club facade into a single place to explore.

03 / Implementation and proposals

Wallets, editions
and a local chain.

Tezos wallet
The visitor wallet connects to Tezos mainnet. Visitors control approvals and signing in their own wallets, and can explore the district without connecting. Wallet connection
FA2 editions
Six unsigned FA2 metadata candidates are available for review. The editions remain unissued. Edition desk
Visit log
The browser lab records architectural visits in a local 2-of-3 signed log. One browser controls the three validators, separately from any production network. District chain lab
Solana
Lintel Moth (LMTM) is a fictional token concept for a possible pump.fun launch. The symbol is a placeholder. Ticker, supply, issuer, distribution and other terms await approval. Launch draft

Funding and participation terms remain open. This project discussion draft contains no offer, solicitation or approved funding address.
