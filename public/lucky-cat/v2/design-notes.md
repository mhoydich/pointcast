# Lucky Cat v2: interaction and sculpture

The stage is an original presentation of the collection's 60 procedural sculptures. Neutral paper, a low ceramic plinth, a broad key light, and a generated reflection room keep the silhouette and material readable. Daylight, amber, and moon are lighting studies, not recolors of the artwork. A full orbit reveals apertures, lattice shells, layered color, and suspended tokens. Detail views focus the face, token, or material without replacing the object with a flat image.

Three primary-source precedents informed the interaction principles:

- [Google Arts & Culture / David Li, Blob Opera](https://experiments.withgoogle.com/blob-opera): clear direct manipulation gives an immediate, expressive response. Here, pointer movement rotates a sculpture and a tap produces a short beckoning gesture.
- [Studio Olafur Eliasson, Your uncertain shadow (growing)](https://olafureliasson.net/artwork/your-uncertain-shadow-growing-2010/): a participant's position changes the experience of light and color. Here, authored light moods and a freely moving viewpoint expose the collection's own geometry and surfaces.
- [teamLab, The Infinite Crystal Universe](https://art.team-lab.cn/en/w/infinite_crystaluniverse/): a three-dimensional light field responds to participation. Here, four numbered, individually raycast luck orbs give a small play scene real spatial depth and immediate feedback.

These are interaction references; no artist's work, imagery, or characteristic composition is reproduced. The cat families retain their separate art-history sources in the catalog and Art Studio. The experience makes no award claim or claim of monetary value.

The scene owns rendering, orbit, picking, and visual feedback. The app owns scores, unlocks, persistence, timed rounds, and accessible numbered buttons. Touch picking has a generous sphere hit region. A drag does not count as a catch. Motion preferences stop idle movement and catch particles; keyboard controls remain available. An unavailable 3D renderer falls back to the existing portrait while keeping the app's collection and catch controls useful.

## Craft target

The 2027 award ambition is a quality target. There is no submission, nomination, or award claim. [Awwwards' current evaluation framework](https://www.awwwards.com/about-evaluation/) weights design at 40%, usability at 30%, creativity at 20%, and content at 10%. We translate that into readable object and material detail; comfortable touch and keyboard control; an original play-and-exhibition loop; and clear collection identities, provenance, and progress. Future evaluation rules can change. [Chrome's reduced-motion guidance](https://web.dev/articles/prefers-reduced-motion) informs the motion preference handling and checks that ornamental movement stops without preventing interaction.
