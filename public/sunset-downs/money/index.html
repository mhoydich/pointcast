WHY NOUNS MONEY?

A good note
travels with a story.

Nouns Money turns original Nouns art into paper objects worth keeping and passing along. This is its Sunset Downs wardrobe: six horse portraits, a film-studio palette, and your stable name where the producer’s credit would be.

The characters use exact original Nouns pixel layers and colors. Their five-part seeds are curated combinations, rather than minted Noun identities. Every portrait has a recorded seed and checksum.

The paper

Front-face vector artwork, 180 × 70 mm. The print sheet places three notes on Letter or A4 paper. Print at 100% / Actual Size; check the 50 mm reference line. Color and paper need a physical proof before any production run.

The credits

Original Nouns artwork is CC0. Independent PointCast edition; no Nouns DAO endorsement or token ownership. See the portrait seeds & checksums and source provenance.

The fine print

Reprintable collectible art. PROP SCRIP · NO CASH VALUE. No redemption, financial rights, funds, NFT ownership or ownership transfer. Race-game credits stay in the game; these notes are souvenirs.
