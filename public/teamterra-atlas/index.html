BASKETBALL · PICKLEBALL · TENNIS · MORE

Find a court, anywhere.

Search the map around a city or any location. Results come from OpenStreetMap and carry its access tags. Missing records and unmarked access are common; this is a discovery tool, with operator checks still needed.

Open worldwide court search
Use coordinates instead

Drag the map to choose another area. Search runs when you press “Find courts in this area.”

Choose a city or map area, then find courts. Public/permissive access tags are shown by default.

OpenStreetMap features can represent a court, a hall or a whole sports centre, so result counts are map features, not a count of physical courts. Private, prohibited, members-only, customer-only and disused records are excluded. Opening hours, fees, reservations and conditional access require a current operator check.

© OpenStreetMap contributors · ODbL 1.0. Area requests go to Private.coffee; map tiles come from OpenStreetMap. Searches are cached on this device for 24 hours. No device location is requested.
