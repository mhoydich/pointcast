Checked 15 November 2026. This packet contains **10 venues across 8 countries and five world regions**, spanning basketball, pickleball, tennis and six additional court-sport labels. Counts refer to venues, not individual playing surfaces.

“Special” is an editorial filter based on the sourced history, setting, accessibility or programming described below. It is not a quality ranking, professional endorsement or promise of present-day availability.

## Qualifications that must survive publication

- **Rucker Park:** preserve the construction-check note. A March 2025 community-board resolution anticipated reconstruction in late 2026 or early 2027; July 2026 final design approval is documented. Neither source establishes an actual closure date. Current court availability remains unverified.
- **Venice Beach:** the official municipal search-index extracts establish the sports list and daily public access. Direct page fetches returned 403; this is recorded in the evidence. No fee-free claim is made.
- **Memorial Park:** distinguish casual use outside city programmes/permits from paid, reservable drop-in sessions. The current city sports page supplies the schedule. The older 2023 price is not presented as current.
- **Queen Elizabeth Park:** city pages disagree on total tennis/basketball counts and give overlapping seasonal arrangements. No venue-wide court count is asserted. Some tennis courts have seasonal book-to-play operation; casual pickleball has a waiting-player time limit and no evening lighting.
- **Regent’s Park:** public court booking requires an account; fees and concession conditions apply. Listed accessible amenities do not constitute a complete accessibility audit.
- **Luxembourg Gardens:** the Senate deliberately opened recreational access through Anybuddy. The operator publishes the current EUR 18 hourly tariff, eligible EUR 14 concessions, booking window and mandatory check-in; the concession announcement alone is not treated as a current availability guarantee.
- **Carlton Gardens:** this record covers the community basketball courts. The separately managed tennis club is not silently merged into it.
- **Kallang:** the current facility page gives 8 Stadium Boulevard and an operator map point; an older ActiveSG guide has a different address/count. The current facility listing takes precedence and no court total is asserted.
- **Victoria Park:** tennis is fee-charging; basketball fees were not established by the cited listing. Park opening hours are not substituted for individual court operating hours.
- **Zoo Lake:** government tourism explicitly says tennis/basketball are available for public use, but court-specific fees, bookings, condition and hours remain unresolved.

## Geography

Two facility reference points come directly from operators’ map links: The Courts at Regent’s Park and Kallang Tennis Centre. They are not surveyed court centroids or entrances. Eight coordinate pairs remain null. Do not invent points. A separately sourced city point can be used only with an explicit city-reference label, or obtain official/OSM facility geometry before precise display.

## Recommended integration

Keep venue and individual court records separate; multi-sport layouts can overlap the same surface. Preserve each sport’s access/fee/booking evidence instead of flattening a mixed venue to “free”. Add `public access`, `booking`, `fees`, `lighting`, `surface`, `accessible amenities`, `operational status`, `last checked`, and `special reason` fields. Coverage and current availability remain different measurements.
