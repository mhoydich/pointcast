## Access and location distinctions

- **La Latteria:** London production is supported by the FAQ and foodservice page. The FAQ says its premises are not open to the public. Locally made cow’s-milk cheese must be distinguished from imported buffalo-milk products it sells.
- **Laiterie La Chapelle:** Paris workshop and cellar are visible from the street; the source publishes shop hours and workshops.
- **Dalewood:** the shop is published, but its page explicitly says it cannot offer tastings. Klapmuts is a locality reference for the farm on the Klapmuts–Simondium road. Its homepage was available through the official search index, while direct extraction initially timed out. The shop page was successfully read.
- **St-Albert:** this is St-Albert, Ontario, Canada, not St. Albert, Alberta. Its factory viewing gallery and separate Mirabel retail location are distinguished.
- **Eleftheria:** maker and postal contact city are Mumbai; the postal address is not independently established as a factory entrance.
- **Fattoria Bio Hokkaido:** the official directory identifies its factory shop in Sapporo separately from independent stockists.
- **La Delizia:** Perth is its own production-city description; East Victoria Park is the published retail locality.
- **Whitestone:** Ōamaru factory and Diner & Deli are separate sites within the same town. Factory tours have a booking route.

## Data handling

Coordinates remain null until a sourced city point is attached. No private residence locations were collected. Product types are examples from maker pages and menus, not complete ranges. No awards or certifications are counted as independently verified.

Quesos VAI was a credible maker lead but was deferred because its official contact extraction did not establish the municipality adequately. It should be revisited separately rather than given an inferred Tequisquiapan pin.

All primary URLs and reference IDs are retained in the JSON for review.
