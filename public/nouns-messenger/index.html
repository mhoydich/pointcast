      ⌐◨-◨ Nouns Messenger

      A pocket instant messenger with noggles on. Phone-first. No account, no server, no feed.

      • Local-first. Every chat is stored in this browser only. Save a backup file to keep a copy, restore it on any phone, or clear it all below.
      • Companions. The Nouns here are little scripted characters who answer on your phone. They are not people. Ask about what is new, the water, the courts or the moon and they read pointcast.xyz’s public town data, say where each number came from and how old it is, and fall back to their scripted lines when it does not answer. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.
      • Pass a note. Any message can become a link. Send the link over whatever you already use, and it lands in Nouns Messenger on the other end.
      • Install it. Add to Home Screen for a full-screen app with the noggles on the tile.
