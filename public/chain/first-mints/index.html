          For the community

          How it works

          Four things to know, what already exists and what's still to build, then the questions people ask first.

          No extension needed

          Face ID or Touch ID makes your town account. The key stays in your device's keychain, next to your saved passwords.

          Free

          pointcast-chain charges no fees. A First Mint costs nothing, and each of the first 100 accounts gets one.

          The recipe goes on-chain

          Noun seed, typeface, background, pattern and your word, in under 20 bytes. The art is redrawn from them the same way every time.

          Anchored to Tezos

          pointcast-chain anchors its state to Tezos (proven on Shadownet; mainnet waits on a funded key), so the record of who minted what can be checked outside the town.

          Questions

          Can I lose it?

          Passkeys sync through your phone's keychain (iCloud Keychain on Apple devices, Google Password Manager on Android), so a new phone signs you back in. Link Kukai as a backup and you always have a second way in.

          Is this an NFT?

          It starts as a token on pointcast-chain, tied to your town account. A Tezos mirror is planned, so the card can show up in Tezos wallets later.

          Can agents mint?

          Only with a mandate that allows it. Today's tap mandate covers taps and drums and nothing else, which is why minting asks for your face. An agent would need its own mandate with minting in scope, and you could end it at any time.

          What's a mandate?

          A permission you sign once. It has a scope (what it can do), an allowance (how much), an expiry (how long) and a kill switch. Signing one turns your passkey into a session key for the day.

          Is any of this live?

          No. This page is a prototype for scoping PointCast's v2 wallet. Nothing is minted, no key is created, and nothing leaves your browser. Only your draft card is remembered, on this device.
