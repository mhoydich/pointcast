    What's real today vs v2
    
        
    What runs now, and what waits for v2.
    
        
    pointcast-chain main already runs drop_mint and publish_block, on a local rehearsal chain. There's no public node yet, so nothing has been minted anywhere.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    Today · pointcast-chain mainv2 · proposed
    
        
    The tape's record
    Todaypublish_block (tag 1) on SPN. The node stores the JSON body, up to 64 KiB, and the chain commits its blake2b hash, with the link as media_uri.
    v2The same record. A time capsule could seal the next volume now and open it on its air date (seal_capsule and open_capsule).
    
        
    Curator's copy
    Todaydrop_mint (tag 4). The first mint creates the drop and makes the sender its creator for good. Up to 10,000 editions chain-wide, no price, and editions can't move.
    v2drop_create with a per-drop cap and a metadata hash (a batch-2 proposal) ends slug squatting and typo drops.
    
        
    Listener copies
    TodayOnly the creator can mint, so the curator's wallet signs one drop_mint per listener from the desk's queue.
    v2open_edition and edition_mint: the listener mints their own copy with a desk approval bound to their address and their listen window.
    
        
    Proof of listen
    TodayNothing on chain. The desk reads a public ListenBrainz log, which is self-reported.
    v2A listen ticket shaped like a presence ticket: an issuer-signed attestation, one per person per edition, with an issuer budget.
    
        
    Series
    TodayEach body names the hash of the volume before. Readers pin the curator's address and ignore volumes from anyone else.
    v2The series lives on a claimed station with a crew (claim_station and set_station), so only the crew can publish the next volume.
    
        
    Tezos
    TodayThe mirror pattern: a Tezos FA2 sale can be mirrored as a free first-collector certificate, tz1 and tz2 only.
    v2A seal attestation, or an FA2 claim against an anchored state root. Anchoring is proven on Shadownet; mainnet waits on a funded key.
    
        
    Status
    TodayLocal rehearsal only. "Minted on PointCast chain" isn't true until a public node runs on the final genesis.
    v2Consensus changes, a public node, and custody for the desk's attestor key.
    
      
    
      
    Resolved in the plan
    The First Mints and time-capsule designs both claimed tags 14 and 15. The Town Network tag registry settles it: capsules take 14 and 15, editions take 21 to 23 (open_edition, edition_mint, edition_transfer), and the mandate mask widens past 15 first, in batch 2.
    
    
    
  
    

  
    
    
    
      
    
        
    How it works and what's private
    
        
    What stays with you.
    
      
    
      
      
        
    • Your listening stays yours.
      The check reads the log in your browser. If the desk approves, the chain gets one edition to your address and a 16-byte hash of your listen window. Never the listen rows, and never your ListenBrainz name.
      • 
        
    • Links are public and permanent.
      The tool strips tracking parameters such as si and utm_ before it hashes anything. Don't mint private, unlisted or tokenized links.
      • 
        
    • Editions stay put.
      They can't be transferred or burned, and they don't follow a Tezos resale. A re-genesis before launch would wipe rehearsal records.
      • 
        
    • This page sends nothing.
      Nothing you do here leaves this browser. The only request it makes is for its fonts, from Google Fonts, and the only thing it remembers is your theme choice.
