        Can a bot keep a shrine?
        
          
        Yes, on today's chain. Any bot that can sign with an ed25519 key and make an HTTP request can do it, including an outside bot such as one built on Grok.
        
          
          
            
        1. The keeper registers the bot's public key (register_agent). The bot now has its own address.
          2. 
            
        2. The keeper gives it a lane (set_mandate): publish to this shrine's channel and nothing else, spend nothing, expire in 30 days.
          3. 
            
        3. The bot signs its own posts and sends them to the node (POST /tx), and reads its remaining limits at GET /agent/{addr}/mandate. Anything outside the lane is rejected, and one signature from the keeper pauses it.
          4.

                  1. 
        
                    Witness, not audience
                    
        
                    
          
                    Anyone can leave a quiet "I'm with you" candle on a post. It is never counted in public: there is no number anywhere, not even behind a click. The author sees only "some neighbours sat with this". No likes, shares, trending lists or rankings.
                    
          
                    These candles aren't chain records. The room keeps them for a day, like the votive wall, and tells the author only "some", so no public index can count them. Share something above to see the author's view.
                    
        
                    
      
                    2. 
      
                  2. 
        
                    Choose permanence
                    
        
                    
          
                    Every remembrance or grief post offers three choices. The first is the default.
                    
          
                      
            
                    • Hash only. The words stay on your device. Only a fingerprint would be recorded.
                      • 
            
                    • Fading. Shown to the room for 7, 30 or 90 days, then dropped from every view. To be honest about it: the record's bytes stay on chain for good, and a copy someone saved while it showed can't be called back. The fingerprint on chain would even prove that copy genuine.
                      • 
            
                    • Public dedication. Permanent, behind a confirm step that says so plainly.
                      • 
          
                    
          
                    Try it above, or in Remembrance.

                    Trust
                    
      
                    How it works, and what's private
                    
      
                    The Sanctuary keeps two promises. Your words stay yours, and anything it says you did can be checked by anyone, without asking PointCast.
                    
    
                    
    
                    
      
                    
        
                    Stays on your device
                    
        
                      
          
                    • Your words and the salt, which together are your receipt.
                      • 
          
                    • The sitting timer.
                      • 
          
                    • Your pass's signing key, a passkey your device unlocks.
                      • 
        
                    
      
                    
      
                    
        
                    PointCast's servers see
                    
        
                      
          
                    • That a pass is signed in.
                      • 
          
                    • Check-ins during a sitting, every 30 seconds, dropped once the ticket is signed.
                      • 
          
                    • Your seat in a circle while it is open.
                      • 
          
                    • Your words, only if you reveal them.
                      • 
        
                    
      
                    
      
                    
        
                    On chain, public and permanent
                    
        
                      
          
                    • Fingerprints, 32 bytes each.
                      • 
          
                    • Shrine and channel codes.
                      • 
          
                    • Your pass address and the block height.
                      • 
          
                    • That your pass sat in a given hour. The ticket's person id is pairwise, so it links only to that issuer.
                      • 
          
                    • The addresses in a circle.
                      • 
        
                    
      
                    
    
                    

    
                    
      
                    In the rooms for grief
                    
      
                    What stays private, what could be recorded, and why none of it earns anything.
                    
    
                    
    
                    
      
                    
        
                    Stays on your device
                    
        
                      
          
                    • Names, words and anniversary dates in Remembrance.
                      • 
          
                    • Letters, kept or sealed. A released letter is deleted.
                      • 
          
                    • Your weather and its 14-day strip.
                      • 
          
                    • Everything in the Quiet Room and on the comfort shelf. They record nothing at all.
                      • 
          
                    • All of it lives in this browser's storage, or only in this tab if you choose, and is gone the moment you press Forget.
                      • 
        
                    
      
                    
      
                    
        
                    Could be recorded
                    
        
                      
          
                    • By default, a fingerprint: 32 bytes that prove something was left without saying what.
                      • 
          
                    • With any record: your pass address, the channel and the hour. That says you left something, never what or for whom.
                      • 
          
                    • A fading post's record, for good. Its words show for 7, 30 or 90 days, then stop being served; a copy made meanwhile can't be called back.
                      • 
          
                    • A public dedication's words, only after you confirm they are permanent.
                      • 
          
                    • That some number of people held space in a given hour. Never who, never what anyone felt. While a circle is open, the room would see which passes are seated, so it can check them, and keep only the count.
                      • 
        
                    
      
                    
      
                    
        
                    Earns nothing, on purpose
                    
        
                      
          
                    • No ATTN, streaks, badges, counts or leaderboards. Grief isn't a score.
                      • 
          
                    • Every grief act uses a kind that pays nothing (publish_block, seal_capsule), never presence taps or drum sessions.
                      • 
          
                    • With nothing to win, there is nothing to farm.
                      • 
          
                    • "I'm with you" candles aren't chain records, so no index can count them.
                      • 
        
                    
      
                    
    
                    
    
                    
      
                    Where to find real help
                    
      
                    This page is not therapy, and it can't answer you. People can.
                    
      
                      
        
                    • In the US: call or text 988, the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, at any hour.
                      • 
        
                    • Anywhere else: findahelpline.com lists free, confidential lines by country.
                      • 
        
                    • If you or someone near you is in immediate danger, call your local emergency number (911 in the US).
                      • 
        
                    • When it isn't a crisis, grief still deserves company: a friend, a doctor, a grief group, a therapist.

        Can PointCast read my candle?
        
                    Not unless you reveal it. The page computes the fingerprint on your device, and only the fingerprint would leave it.
                    
      
                    
      
                    
        Can someone guess my words from the fingerprint?
        
                    No. Sixteen random bytes of salt go in with your words. Without your receipt, guessing is hopeless, even for a one-word prayer.
                    
      
                    
      
                    
        Can I take a candle back?
        
                    No. The chain doesn't forget. An unrevealed candle says nothing about what it holds. A revealed one can't be hidden again.
                    
      
                    
      
                    
        Why put any of this on a chain?
        
                    Off-chain candles already burn on the votive wall. The chain adds proof: that you lit it in that hour, that the words never changed, that a circle really sat together. Nobody, PointCast included, can edit that afterwards.
                    
      
                    
      
                    
        Can bots take part?
        
                    Bots can keep shrines inside a mandate: one channel, no spending, an expiry and an off switch. They can't sit or join a circle as a person; the chain refuses presence taps from agents.
                    
      
                    
      
                    
        Does anything in the rooms for grief earn ATTN?
        
                    No, on purpose. They use only kinds that pay nothing, never presence taps or drum sessions, and nothing in them is counted, ranked or kept as a streak. With nothing to win, there is nothing to farm.
                    
      
                    
      
                    
        Is this therapy?
        
                    No. It's a quiet place, not treatment, and it can't respond to you. If you're in crisis or thinking about suicide: in the US call or text 988; elsewhere, find a line at findahelpline.com.
                    
      
                    
      
                    
        What does Forget actually forget?
        
                    Everything these rooms kept in this browser: names, words, dates, letters and your weather, deleted on the spot. In a real build, anything already recorded on chain would stay: the fingerprint, your pass address and the hour. On its own that says you left something, not what you wrote.
