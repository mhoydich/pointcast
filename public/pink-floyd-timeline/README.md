# Pink Floyd — Sound, Image & Time

An independent audiovisual listening history for PointCast, researched through 15 November 2026.

## The edition

- 60 selected milestones in seven chapters, beginning in 1965.
- All fifteen studio albums, with Ummagumma counted once as a live/studio hybrid.
- 59 primary-source pages and a source/date disclosure on every milestone.
- Nine licensed archival and place photographs, with exact source dates, attribution, licence links and original-file links.
- Ten official Pink Floyd video selections, loaded only after a visitor acts; direct watch links remain visible.
- Search, era and topic filters; a 35-entry audiovisual path; related-event links; album navigation; shareable query state; a print-friendly reading edition.
- Decorative motion can be paused and respects reduced-motion preferences. The text and source disclosures remain usable without JavaScript.

This is a comprehensive selection of major milestones, not an exhaustive concert list, sessionography or solo discography. The final entry records the 5 November 2026 announcement of Animals 50. The scheduled 22 January 2027 release remains a future event at the cutoff.

## Images and audio

The abstract opening image was generated with the built-in image-generation tool for this edition and is labelled on the page. Its final prompt is in `Cover-Art-Prompt.md`. It is decorative artwork, not a historical reconstruction. Album-shelf graphics and screening posters are original code-drawn index illustrations, not reproductions of album covers.

The nine photographs are licensed CC BY, CC BY-SA or CC0 as recorded in `media.json`. Display copies are resized and re-encoded as WebP without altering their composition or colours. Retain all individual credits and licence links when reusing them. ShareAlike derivatives retain the source licence. Abbey Road (1987), Pompeii (2015) and Battersea (2012) are explicitly later place photographs. Docklands 1989 is not Venice 1989; Knebworth 1975 is not the 1990 concert. The optional Toronto photograph is qualified as source-supplied metadata and is not used to document the Ottawa tour opening.

Music and film remain with their official provider; no recordings or lyrics are redistributed. YouTube oEmbed metadata confirmed the official channel identity for ten selections. In shared Chrome, the PULSE and Pompeii embedded players reported “This video is unavailable.” Player creation, metadata display, direct links and closing/unloading were verified, but successful video playback was not. Availability may differ by browser, location or provider policy. The 2026 Crystal Voyager link did not return available oEmbed metadata and remains a source-linked note rather than a default player.

An attempt to generate original spoken chapter introductions was declined by ElevenLabs because free generation capacity was busy. The provider stated that the take did not start and nothing was charged, and instructed against another attempt that turn. No narration audio is included or claimed; all seven written introductions remain visible.

## Source decisions

Dates retain day, month or year precision. The Dark Side of the Moon uses March 1973; Waters’ departure uses 1985. Dated contemporary announcements take precedence where newer landing-page metadata conflicts. Related solo projects, original recordings, film premieres, restorations and reissues are separated. `Source-Budget-Audit.json` records the attribution audit for original summaries and chapter introductions. The factual review is in `Independent-Review.md`.

## Files

`index.html` is the complete primary experience. `Timeline-Reader.html` is a plain, print-friendly reading edition. `timeline.json`, `chapters.json` and `media.json` carry the portable research and attribution. Local assets are in `assets/` and `media/`. No keys, user accounts, backend, build runtime or external fonts are required by this static edition. YouTube is contacted only on an explicit player-open action.
