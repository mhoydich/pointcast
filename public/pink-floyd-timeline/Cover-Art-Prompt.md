# Original cover artwork

Created with the built-in image-generation tool for this independent educational timeline. This is abstract decorative art, not an archival photograph or a recreation of an album cover. The web asset is `assets/light-and-time.webp`, a compressed display copy of the generated original.

## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: cinematic cover artwork for an independent, carefully sourced Pink Floyd historical listening timeline, landscape 16:9.
Primary request: An original, mesmerizing abstract light installation conveying sound, time, space and analogue experimentation. No imitation album cover.
Scene: an immense nearly black room, a hovering thin circular metal ring at right of centre, an exquisite ribbon of luminous spectral color threads passing through the ring and fanning outward into deep space, with gentle atmospheric haze, near-black graphite shadows and subtle silver film grain. The threads feel like real long-exposure optical light, not neon UI or a data chart. A tiny warm amber point in the distance.
Composition: widescreen, dramatic artistic restraint, left 45 percent predominantly dark and quiet for large cream typography added separately by the website. Strong sculptural ring and luminous motion on the right. Beautiful enough for an art museum exhibition poster, genuinely spatial and tactile.
Lighting: soft real volumetric light, violet, muted cyan, warm orange and a little white. Deep black high contrast without crushed highlights.
Constraints: purely abstract editorial artwork; no people, no text, no lettering, no watermark, no logos, no triangular prism, no album-cover recreation. No historical photograph claims.
