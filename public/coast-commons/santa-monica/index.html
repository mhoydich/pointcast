The rules, with the city's own pages

Short stays

Home-sharing means renting bedrooms for 30 days or less in the host's primary residence with the host living on site throughout. It needs a home-share permit and a business license. The City's rules treat a stay with no host on site as a prohibited vacation rental.

Sources, read 2026-11-11: City of Santa Monica: how to apply for a home-share business licenseCity of Santa Monica: Home-Sharing Ordinance rules and regulations (PDF, revised Nov 21, 2019)
