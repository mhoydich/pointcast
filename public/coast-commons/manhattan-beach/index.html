Manhattan Beach

Governing body: City of Manhattan Beach

Depends on the Coastal Zone. Banned outside it; a court held the ban does not reach inside it.

The water and the sand

More than two miles of ocean frontage, a 900-foot pier, and volleyball nets running south to Hermosa. The north end, El Porto, is a popular surf spot where lessons are offered.

Source, read 2026-11-11: LA County Beaches and Harbors: Manhattan Beach

The rules, with the city's own pages

Short stays

The City defines a short-term rental as under 30 days and bans it in residential zones (Ordinance 19-0007, 2019). The page says a Court of Appeal opinion of April 4, 2022 held the ban does not apply inside the Coastal Zone, where operators need a business license and must remit hotel tax. When the page was read it also described a temporary World Cup allowance outside the Coastal Zone as under Council consideration in January 2026; the outcome was not verified.

Source, read 2026-11-11: City of Manhattan Beach: short-term rentals

Backyard units (ADUs)

Ordinance 25-0004 took effect May 1, 2025. ADUs are allowed in single-family and multi-family zones and JADUs in single-family zones, inside or outside the Coastal Zone. Until the Coastal Commission certifies the matching coastal amendments, the page says units in the Coastal Zone follow state law only.

Source, read 2026-11-11: City of Manhattan Beach: ADUs and JADUs

Coastal permits

The City publishes its Local Coastal Program, including Chapter A.96 on coastal development permit procedures and application instructions. Recent amendments are listed as awaiting Coastal Commission certification.

Source, read 2026-11-11: City of Manhattan Beach: Local Coastal Program

Zoning

The zoning code is Title 10 of the Municipal Code. The City posts a zoning map that shows the coastal zone, and an interactive parcel map.

Source, read 2026-11-11: City of Manhattan Beach: zoning and development regulations

Five questions for the planning counter

  1. Is this address inside the Coastal Zone, and who issues the coastal permit here?
  2. What is this parcel zoned, and is a club, meeting room or lodging use allowed in that zone?
  3. Is a stay under 30 days lawful at this address, and under which permit?
  4. What does the city count as an event or a gathering, and when does it need its own permit?
  5. Which business license and occupancy tax apply, and who has to hold them?
