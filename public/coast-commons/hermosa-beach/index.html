The water and the sand

Almost two miles of city-owned beach, known for surf and swimming, with The Strand along the sand and a concrete pier first built in 1913.

The rules, with the city's own pages

Short stays

Stays under 30 days are allowed only in older, lawfully established homes that sit in commercial zones C-2 and C-3 and specific plan areas 7, 8 and 11, and only with an administrative permit. Operating without one carries fines that start at $5,000.

Source, read 2026-11-11: City of Hermosa Beach: short-term vacation rentals

Backyard units (ADUs)

ADU rules are in Municipal Code Chapter 17.21. Rentals must run at least 30 days, so an ADU cannot be a short-term rental.

Source, read 2026-11-11: City of Hermosa Beach: accessory dwelling units

Coastal permits

Hermosa Beach has a certified coastal land use plan but not a fully certified Local Coastal Program. The City approves a project in concept and the Coastal Commission's South Coast District issues the coastal development permit.

Source, read 2026-11-11: City of Hermosa Beach: Local Coastal Plan

Five questions for the planning counter

  1. Is this address inside the Coastal Zone, and who issues the coastal permit here?
  2. What is this parcel zoned, and is a club, meeting room or lodging use allowed in that zone?
  3. Is a stay under 30 days lawful at this address, and under which permit?
  4. What does the city count as an event or a gathering, and when does it need its own permit?
  5. Which business license and occupancy tax apply, and who has to hold them?
