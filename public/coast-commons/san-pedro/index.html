The water and the sand

White Point and Royal Palms have more than a mile and a half of rocky shore with some of the most varied tide pools in Southern California. Fishing, surfing and diving are popular; swimming is not advised. Construction limits parking through 2027.

Source, read 2026-11-11: LA County Beaches and Harbors: White Point and Royal Palms

The rules, with the city's own pages

Short stays

Under the Home-Sharing Ordinance a host may share only their primary residence, must register with the City and show the number on listings, and is limited to 120 days a year unless they hold an extended registration. Rent-stabilized units are not eligible, and renters need the landlord's notarized approval.

Source, read 2026-11-11: Los Angeles City Planning: Home-Sharing Program

Five questions for the planning counter

  1. Is this address inside the Coastal Zone, and who issues the coastal permit here?
  2. What is this parcel zoned, and is a club, meeting room or lodging use allowed in that zone?
  3. Is a stay under 30 days lawful at this address, and under which permit?
  4. What does the city count as an event or a gathering, and when does it need its own permit?
  5. Which business license and occupancy tax apply, and who has to hold them?
