Torrance Beach

Governing body: City of Torrance

Hosted rooms only. A resident host must be home. No whole-house weekends.

The water and the sand

About 40 acres of soft sand under the cliffs that run to the Palos Verdes Peninsula. The stretch shared with Redondo is often called RAT Beach.

What a commons could be here

A sketch by Claude, not a plan and not a listing.

A hosted house with a garden. Torrance allows a home share when the host lives on site, so the pattern here is a member family that hosts, plus a shared vegetable plot and a tool shed. The public-good share could be a seed and tool library.

The rules, with the city's own pages

Short stays

In residential zones the only short-term rental allowed is a home share with the host living on site for the whole stay. Commercial zones allow them with or without a host. A City permit and business license are required. In the Coastal Zone the rules follow the Coastal Commission approval the page cites as CDP 5-20-0031.

Source, read 2026-11-11: City of Torrance: short term rentals

Backyard units (ADUs)

ADU rules are in Municipal Code section 92.2.10, amended in October 2025. An ADU may not be rented for less than 30 days. For the Coastal Zone the page sends applicants to the Coastal Commission.

Source, read 2026-11-11: City of Torrance: accessory dwelling units

Five questions for the planning counter

  1. Is this address inside the Coastal Zone, and who issues the coastal permit here?
  2. What is this parcel zoned, and is a club, meeting room or lodging use allowed in that zone?
  3. Is a stay under 30 days lawful at this address, and under which permit?
  4. What does the city count as an event or a gathering, and when does it need its own permit?
  5. Which business license and occupancy tax apply, and who has to hold them?
