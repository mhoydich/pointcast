The rules, with the city's own pages

Short stays

The City lists short term or vacation rentals in the R1 zone as a common code violation and cites Municipal Code section 18.04.021. The code section itself was not opened.

Source, read 2026-11-11: City of Palos Verdes Estates: common code enforcement violations

Backyard units (ADUs)

Local ADU rules are in Municipal Code Chapter 18.45, adopted by Ordinance O20-747 in October 2020. The process can include a recorded deed restriction and a yearly rent certification.

Source, read 2026-11-11: City of Palos Verdes Estates: ADUs

Five questions for the planning counter

  1. Is this address inside the Coastal Zone, and who issues the coastal permit here?
  2. What is this parcel zoned, and is a club, meeting room or lodging use allowed in that zone?
  3. Is a stay under 30 days lawful at this address, and under which permit?
  4. What does the city count as an event or a gathering, and when does it need its own permit?
  5. Which business license and occupancy tax apply, and who has to hold them?
