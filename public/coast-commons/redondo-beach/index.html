Redondo Beach

Governing body: City of Redondo Beach

No short stays in homes. Short stays are barred in residential zones.

The water and the sand

A mile and a half of beach south of the horseshoe pier, good for surfing, swimming and windsurfing, with King Harbor to the north. A statue of surfing pioneer George Freeth stands at the pier.

Source, read 2026-11-11: LA County Beaches and Harbors: Redondo Beach

The rules, with the city's own pages

Short stays

A City document treats lodging for 30 days or less as a hotel or motel use, which the municipal code bars in the single-family and multi-family residential zones (sections 10-2.402, 10-2.501 and 10-2.511). The document carries no date.

Source, read 2026-11-11: City of Redondo Beach: short-term rentals not permitted in residential zones (PDF)

Five questions for the planning counter

  1. Is this address inside the Coastal Zone, and who issues the coastal permit here?
  2. What is this parcel zoned, and is a club, meeting room or lodging use allowed in that zone?
  3. Is a stay under 30 days lawful at this address, and under which permit?
  4. What does the city count as an event or a gathering, and when does it need its own permit?
  5. Which business license and occupancy tax apply, and who has to hold them?
