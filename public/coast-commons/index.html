Coast Commons

What if a beach town kept one house, shed or boat in common? Ten neighbors, open books, a share set aside for everyone else. This is the homework for 15 towns within about 25 miles of El Segundo: what the place is like, what a commons could be there, and what each city actually allows.

A study and a public resource, not an offer. Nothing is for sale, no money is being raised, and none of this is legal advice. Read the plain box.

Start at home: the El Segundo house

El Segundo is the flagship. The city only permits hosted rooms, so the first step there is not the usual rented weekend. The page works out what it could be instead.

Two longer studies

The same idea followed all the way through, twice. Each is a study and a public resource, not an offer, with every source linked beside the claim and what could not be verified marked where it matters.

  • SB Surf House. What a community-run surf house on the Santa Barbara coast would take: place, market medians and indices, city, county and coastal rules, eight ways to own together, the securities question, what happened to earlier attempts, three schematic plans, a per-member cost calculator and a nine-step ladder that starts with a rented weekend.
  • Fresh Milk, Local. California dairy law in plain terms, what federal and state health agencies say about raw milk, which South Bay towns allow cows, goats or sheep, what dairy exists near Los Angeles, precedents elsewhere, a ladder of six options from a buying club to a teaching farm, and a calculator for how many animals a group of households would need.

How the circle was drawn

Distance is a straight line over the earth's surface (great-circle, haversine) from El Segundo City Hall to one reference point in each town, usually its pier or main beach. The reference points are approximate coordinates chosen by Claude. It is not driving distance, which is longer everywhere on this coast.

Checked and left out

  • Pacific Palisades. About 11 miles out and inside the City of Los Angeles. Left off for now while the neighborhood rebuilds after the January 2025 fire.
  • Sunset Beach and Huntington Beach. Sunset Beach is about 24 miles out and is part of Huntington Beach, whose pier is about 29 miles out. Treated as outside the circle.
  • Western Malibu (Zuma, Point Dume and beyond). Zuma is about 24 miles out and the city continues west past 30. Malibu is measured at Surfrider.
  • Carson, Lomita, Rolling Hills, Rolling Hills Estates. Inside the circle but with no ocean beach of their own.

What the reading turned up

  • The rented weekend is not available everywhere. Several towns bar short stays in homes or allow only a resident host renting rooms. In those towns the first step has to be a campsite, a day room, a month-long lease or a clubhouse with no beds.
  • Backyard units are not a way around it. Every city page that addressed the question said an ADU cannot be a short-term rental.
  • The coast has two permit desks. Some cities issue their own coastal permits. In others the Coastal Commission still does. Each town page says which, where that was confirmed. California Coastal Commission: permit applications
