What a commons could be here

A sketch by Claude, not a plan and not a listing.

A small clubhouse off Main Street rather than a house, since only a few dozen permitted rentals exist. Boards, a table, a pot of coffee. The public-good share could be free pier-side surf mornings for local kids.

The rules, with the city's own pages

Short stays

Stays of 29 nights or fewer need a City permit and business license, are allowed only in the Coastal Zone south of Westminster Boulevard, and are capped at one percent of homes there. The FAQ counts 31 permits, first awarded by lottery in May 2023, with a waitlist. It lists a $600 first-year fee and $400 renewal. Permits do not transfer on sale.

Coastal permits

Seal Beach has no certified Local Coastal Program; a revised draft land use plan went to the Coastal Commission for comment on May 9, 2023. The page says the City takes over permitting only after certification, so permits appear to remain with the Commission.

Five questions for the planning counter

  1. Is this address inside the Coastal Zone, and who issues the coastal permit here?
  2. What is this parcel zoned, and is a club, meeting room or lodging use allowed in that zone?
  3. Is a stay under 30 days lawful at this address, and under which permit?
  4. What does the city count as an event or a gathering, and when does it need its own permit?
  5. Which business license and occupancy tax apply, and who has to hold them?
