The rules, with the city's own pages

Short stays

The City Council affirmed in 2016 that rentals under 30 days are prohibited in residential zones, and Ordinances 592U and 593 also bar advertising them. Fines listed run from $2,500 to $7,500.

Source, read 2026-11-11: City of Rancho Palos Verdes: short term rentals

Backyard units (ADUs)

ADUs are allowed on lots zoned for single-family or multi-family housing and JADUs on single-family lots, under Municipal Code Chapter 17.10. The page noted a Council item on October 6, 2026 to begin updating the ordinance.

Source, read 2026-11-11: City of Rancho Palos Verdes: ADUs and JADUs

Coastal permits

The City has its own Coastal Permit application, required for most improvements on property seaward of Palos Verdes Drive West and South.

Source, read 2026-11-11: City of Rancho Palos Verdes: planning applications

Five questions for the planning counter

  1. Is this address inside the Coastal Zone, and who issues the coastal permit here?
  2. What is this parcel zoned, and is a club, meeting room or lodging use allowed in that zone?
  3. Is a stay under 30 days lawful at this address, and under which permit?
  4. What does the city count as an event or a gathering, and when does it need its own permit?
  5. Which business license and occupancy tax apply, and who has to hold them?
