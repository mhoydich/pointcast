The water and the sand

Mother's Beach is a human-made beach inside the harbor with nearly half a mile of sand and no surf in the swimming area. Kayaking and windsurfing are the main activities.

Source, read 2026-11-11: LA County Beaches and Harbors: Marina (Mother's) Beach

The rules, with the city's own pages

Short stays

The County's short-term rental ordinance took effect October 7, 2024 for unincorporated areas, with registration of a primary residence only. The County page says it does not apply in coastal areas, Marina del Rey included, until the Coastal Commission approves it and it is written into each Local Coastal Program. Whether that has happened since was not verified.

Source, read 2026-11-11: Los Angeles County Treasurer and Tax Collector: short-term rentals

Backyard units (ADUs)

The same County page says ADUs and junior ADUs cannot be used as short-term rentals under the ordinance, and that the County's Title 22 ADU rules do not apply in the coastal zone. Marina del Rey's own coastal rules were not opened.

Source, read 2026-11-11: Los Angeles County Treasurer and Tax Collector: short-term rentals

Coastal permits

Marina del Rey has a certified Local Coastal Program (1984, amended 2012), so coastal permits are handled by the County, with the Coastal Commission keeping tidelands and certain appeals. This comes from a County archive page that points to the current planning site for updates.

Source, read 2026-11-11: Los Angeles County Regional Planning: coastal (archive page)

Five questions for the planning counter

  1. Is this address inside the Coastal Zone, and who issues the coastal permit here?
  2. What is this parcel zoned, and is a club, meeting room or lodging use allowed in that zone?
  3. Is a stay under 30 days lawful at this address, and under which permit?
  4. What does the city count as an event or a gathering, and when does it need its own permit?
  5. Which business license and occupancy tax apply, and who has to hold them?
