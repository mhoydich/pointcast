The water and the sand

Surfrider Beach has almost a mile of ocean frontage and became famous through the surf films of the 1950s and 60s. Next to it, Malibu Lagoon is 22 acres of wetland used by more than 200 bird species.

Source, read 2026-11-11: LA County Beaches and Harbors: Malibu Surfrider Beach

Topanga Beach, at the east end, breaks over cobblestone and reef and suits surfing more than swimming.

Source, read 2026-11-11: LA County Beaches and Harbors: Topanga Beach

Malibu is 21 miles long. It is measured here at Surfrider Beach; the western end past Zuma is more than 25 miles out.

What a commons could be here

A sketch by Claude, not a plan and not a listing.

A board barn more than a bunkhouse: a shared rack of longboards and wetsuits near the point, a rinse station, a bench, and a logbook. Members drive in for the morning and go home. The public-good share could be a monthly lagoon bird walk and beach clean.

The rules, with the city's own pages

Short stays

Since January 15, 2021 a City short-term rental permit is required to advertise or operate one (Ordinance 468, Municipal Code Chapter 17.55). A stricter hosted ordinance (472), with an on-site host and primary residence rule, was adopted in 2020 but waits on Coastal Commission certification; the page calls it in progress.

Source, read 2026-11-11: City of Malibu: short-term rental program

Backyard units (ADUs)

ADU rules are in Municipal Code 17.44 and the coastal implementation plan. The page says all of Malibu is in the Coastal Zone and its certified Local Coastal Program governs new development, ADUs included. A coastal amendment for ADUs was sent to the Coastal Commission in February 2024; its status was not stated.

Source, read 2026-11-11: City of Malibu: accessory dwelling units

Coastal permits

All of Malibu is in the Coastal Zone and the City has a certified Local Coastal Program, per the City's ADU page. The City's coastal permit page was not opened.

Source, read 2026-11-11: City of Malibu: accessory dwelling units

Five questions for the planning counter

  1. Is this address inside the Coastal Zone, and who issues the coastal permit here?
  2. What is this parcel zoned, and is a club, meeting room or lodging use allowed in that zone?
  3. Is a stay under 30 days lawful at this address, and under which permit?
  4. What does the city count as an event or a gathering, and when does it need its own permit?
  5. Which business license and occupancy tax apply, and who has to hold them?
