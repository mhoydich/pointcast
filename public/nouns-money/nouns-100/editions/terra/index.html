WORKPLACE COMEDY · 10 × 12 MINUTES

Making a movie is easy.
Making this movie is a group project.

Behind the cinema in Offscreen, a forgotten backlot contains three flats, a tea kettle and an eighty-foot cardboard moon. When an optimistic producer promises the picture of the decade, a crew of practical little companions must finish it before dawn—without a script, a safety rail, or enough blue paint.

Every episode turns one ordinary production problem into an enormous miniature ordeal. The fix is never a genius move. It is a better listen.

PILOT SCENE · “THE MOON IS A REHEARSAL” · COMPLETE 20-BEAT SET PIECE

01 · INT. STAGE 4 — PRE-DAWN. A paper moon turns above three half-painted flats. CAM checks a call sheet printed on the back of a menu.

02 · CAM: The moon is narrower than the drawing.

03 · GRAIN: The drawing was ambitious.

04 · SPLICE: We have fifteen minutes before the real moon offers notes.

05 · SCOUT: It is already outside. It looks extremely available.

06 · CAM: If we move the camera seven feet back, the moon becomes convincing.

07 · GRAIN: It becomes a convincing small moon. That’s allowed.

08 · INDEX flips a card: MOON: SMALL / FEELING: LARGE.

09 · TWINKLE: I can light the corner we are pretending is the sky.

10 · GHOSTLIGHT slides a single lamp into frame. It squeaks with authority.

11 · WIDE EYES: Does anyone need a muffin before we make the moon emotionally responsible?

12 · DOUBLE: The actor can take one careful step backward. That is the stunt.

13 · CAM: Wonderful. The stunt has a sequel.

14 · SPLICE: Positions, please. We are shooting the moment after a great idea, which is usually where we are.

15 · GRAIN paints one orange edge on the moon. Everyone watches it become less perfect and more itself.

16 · SCOUT: Now it looks like it belongs to somebody.

17 · CAM: Roll camera.

18 · The paper moon wobbles. The kettle clicks off. Nobody moves.

19 · TWINKLE: Was that our sound cue?

20 · SPLICE marks the slate GOOD. SPLICE: It is now.

The crew looks up. For two seconds, the small moon is enormous. CUT TO: a very pleased kettle.
