[A desk lamp clicks on. It illuminates one chair. Ghostlight stands beside it holding a sign that says APPLAUSE, PLEASE. Grain sets a metronome. The ventilation grate hums in B-flat.]

GHOSTLIGHT Good evening, viewers. If you are watching this, you have found our station. If you are not watching this, please tell a friend who is.

GRAIN We have exactly four minutes and seventeen seconds before the projector warms up. I have divided them into beats.

GHOSTLIGHT Wonderful. Our first segment is called “A Room Full of Applause.”

[Ghostlight turns the APPLAUSE sign toward the empty chairs. Nothing happens. The vent hums.]

GRAIN That was the building settling.

GHOSTLIGHT It's a very generous building.

[Grain taps the metronome. Tick. The vent hums again, two notes higher.]

GRAIN It has a surprisingly good sense of timing.

GHOSTLIGHT Let's try our applause button.

[They reveal a large red button. A handwritten label reads APPLAUSE. Twinkle leans in from behind the curtain.]

TWINKLE Should I press it?

GHOSTLIGHT Only if you'd like to.

[Twinkle presses it. A small desk bell rings. The vent stops. From somewhere in the projection booth: one soft clap. Then another.]

GRAIN That is not the button.

TWINKLE It might be the room.

[They wait. The room offers a third clap: a radiator ping. Ghostlight lowers the sign.]

GHOSTLIGHT Thank you, room. You're welcome to stay for the whole show.

GRAIN That's not on the run of show.

GHOSTLIGHT It's on the room's.

[Twinkle turns the sign over. The other side says TAKE YOUR TIME. The three sit down. The lamp's light widens by one comfortable inch.]
