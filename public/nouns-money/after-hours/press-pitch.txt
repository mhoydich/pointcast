Subject: Nouns After Hours: visual companions become comics and objects you can keep

Hello,

I’m developing Nouns After Hours, an original world that connects ten visual companions with comics, printable keepsakes, art notes and apparel designs. The question behind it is simple: what happens when a digital companion gets a life after work?

The project uses original Nouns pixel art, with new stories and graphic compositions. The first collection offers a tangible entry point: read a story, choose a companion and make a printed object of your own.

I’d be glad to share the season, artwork and a short walkthrough of the making process if this fits your coverage of design, independent comics or creative technology.

Michael Hoydich
