Receipt notes Printing

The weave: each buy the office makes prints a thermal-receipt note on Tezos. The coin side keeps its receipt. The note side gets a gift to hand out. The first twelve are printed and waiting at the signing desk.

  1. The office wallet makes a buy on pump.fun and posts its receipt to the Loom.
  2. The printer turns that receipt into a note: the coin's ticker is what it's good for, the buy number is the serial, and a whole Noun is the portrait.
  3. The note mints free on Tezos from officenoun.tez, one edition per buy.
  4. It goes to the first person who replies "for ______" naming someone. The note is finished when someone names who it's for.

HOUSE LINES · Notes are free editions only, never priced. No prices on a note. A note is not a coin and can't be swapped for one. The coins carry no promise of value and are not a signal to buy. Nobody here will ask for your keys.

Not legal tender. Just tender.
