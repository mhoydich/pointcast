An evolving collection

Keep making
the details better.

These are versioned building blocks, not an automatic change to every PointCast app. Existing experiences keep their current behavior until an upgrade is reviewed.

  1. Try an experiment. Give it a focused scene, source history and a clear behavior contract.
  2. Compare and critique. Review composition, input response, silence, reduced motion and real-device performance.
  3. Promote a version. Keep a change log, tests and a migration note. Each app adopts it deliberately.
  4. Listen and refine. Device observations and user feedback guide the next iteration.
