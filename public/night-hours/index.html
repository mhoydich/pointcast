The station in the frame follows the real night over El Segundo, from sunset to sunrise. PRAY rings the station's bell, turns the image and adds one to a ledger shared with the same shrine at elsegundo.xyz/night-hours.

The night wing of the morning hours and the evening hours. The open hours follow the working day. The first prototype is kept as it was.

Imagery is AI artwork: Krea 3 stills and Seedance 2.5 clips, directed by Mike Hoydich and built with Claude. Sunset, sunrise and moon phase are computed from the date, not observed.
