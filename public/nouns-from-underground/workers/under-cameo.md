# Going Up

*The Noun elevator operator polishes a button marked SKY.*

**MOON:** Does this go to the sky?

**NOUN:** Eventually. We stop at every floor where somebody has pressed Up.

**MOON:** How many people live here?

**NOUN:** Nobody. The buttons press themselves after midnight. They get lonely.

**MOON:** I was in the sky yesterday. Could you take me there yesterday?

**NOUN:** That's the other elevator. It's always just left.

**MOON:** What if I climb the cable?

**NOUN:** You're a moon. Don't you have a tide to catch?

**MOON:** That's what got me here. I followed it into the basement.

**NOUN:** Was it carrying laundry?

**MOON:** Just one enormous sock. It had your initials.
