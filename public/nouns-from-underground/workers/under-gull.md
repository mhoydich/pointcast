At carousel six, Gull watched a suitcase make three solemn laps. Each time it passed, something inside broke against the zipper. The Noun hauled it down and opened it beside a pillar. Inside, ninety tiny oceans rolled in their compartments. One held a ferry with its windows lit. Gull had packed that ocean last, while the ferry was still coming in. Now a woman at the rail raised her hand. Gull set the suitcase down, took off his honey glasses, and waved back with both hands so she could see.
