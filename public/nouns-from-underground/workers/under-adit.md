# Tunnel Dispatch claim card

1. **Claim** — State one checkable assertion and name the affected place or process.
2. **Evidence** — Give the source and time, plus any gap that affects confidence.
3. **Route to** — Name the role responsible for reviewing the claim and the action requested.
4. **Decision due** — Give the deadline for a decision and the consequence of missing it.
5. **Disposition** — Record the status, the person who can close the card, and the criterion for closure.

## Filled fictional example

1. **Claim** — The new Tunnel Dispatch map sends parcel runners from the West Lift to the retired intake desk.
2. **Evidence** — A dispatch runner logged one misroute at 08:40. The map revision is still unknown.
3. **Route to** — The map steward should compare the posted map with the current desk assignment.
4. **Decision due** — Before the noon parcel run, when the next group will use the map.
5. **Disposition** — Open. The map steward closes the card after checking the route and recording any correction.
