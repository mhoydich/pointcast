# Pass the Packet

At the Root Library, every seed packet has room for three lines. Play with three people and pass an imaginary packet after each turn.

1. **Lend.** Invent a seed, say what it grows, and hand it to the next player.
2. **Grow.** Pick an underground place to plant it and describe one surprise when it sprouts. Pass the packet again.
3. **Share.** Give a seed from the new crop to a character who needs it. Tell us what they'll do with it. The round ends when the character takes the packet.

## One round

1. “Here’s a sockbean. It grows warm socks, one at a time.”
2. “I planted it beneath the lost-property bench. Every sock came up with somebody’s initials stitched in.”
3. “I gave a bean to the centipede who runs lost property. She took the packet and started a list of her feet.”
