# Platform Theatre — Under Gobo

## The Return Ticket

1. **Wide shot.** At the top of the subway stairs, a commuter holds a stiff paper ticket. The turnstile is down below. Behind the commuter, a pigeon on the stair rail lifts its wings.
2. **Close-up.** Two feathered wings unfold from the ticket’s short edges. The commuter pinches the middle with both hands as the wings beat.
3. **Wide shot.** The ticket wriggles free and shoots up the stairs. The commuter lunges after it with one hand outstretched. The pigeon stays on the rail, head cocked.
4. **Street level.** The ticket rises above the subway entrance. A bus rolls past, bending a small street tree in its wake. The ticket tumbles back toward the stairwell, one wing folded against its face.
5. **At the turnstile.** The commuter, halfway up the stairs, twists around. The ticket dives past their shoulder and squeezes itself edge-first into the turnstile slot. Two feathers hang in the air behind it.
6. **Low angle.** The turnstile arm swings open as the commuter steps through. On the far side, the ticket pokes from the return slot. Its wings droop. The commuter stoops and holds out a finger for it to perch on.
