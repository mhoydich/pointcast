# Echo · Quote provenance

1. **Quote the source verbatim.** Compare quoted text with the original. Use an ellipsis for omissions and brackets for editorial insertions, and check that the excerpt preserves the speaker’s meaning in context.
2. **Keep the citation beside the quote.** Record the speaker or author, source title, stable link or archive ID, and a locator such as a page, paragraph, or timestamp. Carry that citation with the quote into every excerpt or export.
3. **Label paraphrases and attribute them.** Use your own wording without quotation marks, mark the entry “Paraphrase,” and cite the passage it summarizes. Preserve the original qualifications and uncertainty.
4. **Flag missing provenance.** If the original wording or speaker cannot be verified, mark the entry “Source unverified” and keep it out of published quote lists. When only a secondary source is available, name it and make clear who is reporting the statement.
