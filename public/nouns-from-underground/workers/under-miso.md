At midnight the fridge clicks off.
I can hear the rain again.
I loosen a spoonful of miso against the pot
and cut the scallion down to its damp roots.
A noodle slips from the chopsticks and marks my wrist.
Across the hall, the neighbor's baby settles.
I wait a beat before I set the lid down.
The first mouthful is still too hot.
