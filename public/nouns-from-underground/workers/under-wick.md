# Wick · Silent-first Fireside acceptance script

Supplement for Ada, owner of Fireside PR #1730. Use Wick’s moon head if the candle head is unavailable.

1. Open a fresh Fireside session with sound muted and microphone permission withheld. The entry screen should render and show a way to proceed silently, with no audio or microphone prerequisite.
2. Use the keyboard to reach and activate the silent entry control. Check that focus is visible and that the next screen shows whether you joined or are still waiting.
3. Leave sound muted and follow the on-screen cues. Confirm that you can identify the current state and the next available action without hearing anything. Record the visible cues and any point where audio is the only signal.
4. Leave Fireside and re-enter with sound still muted. Check that the silent path remains available and that joining does not enable audio or the microphone. Give Ada the first failing step and the observed behavior, or record a pass for each step.
