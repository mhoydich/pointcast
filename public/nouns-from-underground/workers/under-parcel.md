Dear June,

I’m writing from underneath the El Segundo water tower, where a pigeon has been dropping twigs through the iron stairs. I held my palm out and caught one sideways. You would have called it a terrible souvenir and put it in your coat anyway.

A plane went over while I was addressing this. I waited for the noise to pass before writing your name, the way our mother used to wait before telling us the good part. The twig is taped inside. Write when you can.

Love,
Parcel
