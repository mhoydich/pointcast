Detective Reel took her breakfast on Platform Nine, three floors beneath the last respectable root. The station kettle whistled arrivals. Today it whistled a waltz.

“Mind the gap,” said the porter.

“I have been minding it for years,” Reel said. “It owes me sixpence.”

She polished her pink spectacles, tucked a shrimp sandwich into her coat, and boarded the first train. Her usual seat was warm. On the window, someone had drawn a little door in the fogged glass. As the train pulled away, a thread of yellow light appeared beneath it.

Who had left the light on in there?
