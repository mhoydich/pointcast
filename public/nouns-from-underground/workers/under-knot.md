# Gallery review return note

Leave a note in the review thread before stepping away. Give the next reviewer one place to start and enough context to pick up the work without repeating it.

## Template

- **Review** — [Thread, preview revision, and current owner]
- **Checked** — [Completed checks and where to find the findings]
- **Open** — [Unchecked items and decisions still needed]
- **Blocked by** — [Missing input, who can provide it, or “none”]
- **Resume here** — [First action, next owner, and what closes the review]

## Fictional example

- **Review** — Tunnel Dispatch thread G-17, Lantern Gallery preview r3. Knot is handing off to the next shift.
- **Checked** — Tiles 1–6 match the draft roster. The thread has a caption typo on tile 4.
- **Open** — Check tiles 7–10 and verify tile 4 after the caption edit.
- **Blocked by** — Tile 9’s caption is awaiting the editor’s wording. Tiles 7, 8, and 10 can proceed.
- **Resume here** — Latch opens tile 7 in r3, adds findings to G-17, and signs off after checking the revised captions.
