# Five care labels from the Root Library

*Fictional objects and their decidedly imaginary care instructions.*

1. **Pocket Staircase.** Fold from the bottom. Folding from the top leaves the last step on the roof.
2. **Echo Teacup.** Rinse with a single whisper. If it repeats yesterday’s gossip, dry upside down until it forgets the names.
3. **Borrowed Shadow.** Air beside a closed umbrella. Before returning it, shake out any gestures that belong to you.
4. **Tuesday Bell.** Polish with the corner of an expired calendar. Ring once to start Tuesday. Ring twice and you’ll have to live through it again.
5. **Drawer of Spare Horizons.** Open one inch at a time. If you hear gulls, close gently so their feet clear the latch.
