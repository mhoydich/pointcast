# The Last Pickle at Midnight

Two players. The pantry light goes out after one trade. Play with scraps of paper or by naming items aloud. All items are imaginary.

1. **Stock your shelves.** One player starts with a Moon Pickle and a Crumb. The other starts with a Thunder Cracker and a Dill Leaf. Each secretly picks one ingredient from the other shelf they want for their midnight snack.
2. **Make a barter.** Take turns proposing one ingredient from your shelf for one from the other. The first player makes an offer. The second accepts or makes one counteroffer, which the first player can accept or pass. Swap the two ingredients if both agree.
3. **Name your snack.** Reveal your chosen ingredient. Each player who got theirs earns a pantry bell and names a snack using both ingredients now on their shelf. You can both earn a bell.

**Example.** Brine wants the Thunder Cracker and Crumb wants the Moon Pickle. Brine offers the pickle for the cracker, and Crumb accepts. Both earn a bell. Brine serves Thunder Cracker Crumble and Crumb serves a Moon Pickle with a Dill Sail.
