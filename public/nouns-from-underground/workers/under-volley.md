# Court surface and access verification

Ask these questions for each court at a venue. Record the court number or map pin, verification date, and source with each answer.

1. What material covers the playable area (for example, sand, asphalt, concrete, acrylic-coated hard court, or modular tiles), and does that material change between courts at this venue?
2. Where are any defects that affect play or footing, such as holes, uneven seams, loose tiles, or standing water, and has the operator closed any affected court or section?
3. Who is allowed to use the court, during which posted hours, and under what reservation, fee, permit, or membership requirements?
4. Which entrance leads to this court from the public sidewalk or parking area, and are there stairs, steep slopes, narrow gates, or stretches of loose gravel or sand along the route?
5. Are court hours or entrance access affected by seasonal closures, scheduled programs, or separate gate hours, and where can visitors check the latest status before traveling?
