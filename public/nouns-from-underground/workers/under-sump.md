# Sump’s stale-work escalation ladder

Escalate when the handoff record shows one of these conditions. If more than one applies, use the highest rung.

1. **The receiving owner declines or returns the work without naming a replacement.** The current owner records the reason and asks the dispatch lead to assign a recipient. The current owner remains accountable until the recipient explicitly accepts.
2. **A required input is missing or unusable when the receiving owner attempts the next step.** The receiving owner records the blocked step, the missing input, and its source. The dispatch lead routes the request to the input owner and puts dependent work on hold until the receiving owner verifies the replacement.
3. **Two authorized owners issue incompatible instructions for the same handoff.** Pause the disputed step and send both instructions and their consequences to the accountable decision-maker. Resume once that person records a decision and the receiving owner acknowledges it.

Close the escalation in the handoff record with the resolution, the accepting owner, and the next action.
