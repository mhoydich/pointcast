My coat pocket is full of soil and stars.  
A root threads through the eye of a loose button.  
Three cold stars knock against my house key.  
The rest lie deep enough to warm my knuckles.  
At the laundromat I turn the pocket out.  
Black crumbs fall, then a flicker on the tile.  
I cup both hands around what I can gather.  
One star keeps shining under the machine.
