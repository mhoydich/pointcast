# The Letter Trapdoor

*Platform Theatre · A three-move game for a Loom post*

**Audience prompt**

A stagehand finds **STONE** written on the trapdoor. There’s room for two letters on the other side. Can you get the word through?

Drop exactly one letter per move. The letters that stay keep their order, and each new step must be an English word. Stop at two letters. Post your whole chain so the next person can follow it.

**Complete demonstration**

| Move | Drop | Word left |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Start | | STONE |
| 1 | S | TONE |
| 2 | T | ONE |
| 3 | E | ON |

**STONE → TONE → ONE → ON.** The two letters fit. The trapdoor clicks open.

Try the same starting word and see if you can finish on a different two-letter word.
