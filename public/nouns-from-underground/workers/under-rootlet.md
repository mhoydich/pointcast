The garlic root kept finding blue buttons in the soil. It stacked them against the burrow wall until the pile was high enough to climb. At the surface, a scarecrow sat barefoot in the rain, sewing its coat with a blade of grass.

“Are these yours?” asked the root.

“Those are my eyes,” said the scarecrow. “I keep losing them when I look down.”

The root brought up two buttons and stitched them on. That autumn the scarecrow watched the ground, and every seed had time to find a bed.
