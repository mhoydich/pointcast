# Platform Lights

Night Radio theme motif in A minor at 92 BPM. Play four quarter notes per bar in 4/4. Each cell pairs the pitch name with its MIDI note number.

| Bar | Beat 1 | Beat 2 | Beat 3 | Beat 4 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | A2 (45) | C3 (48) | E3 (52) | B2 (47) |
| 2 | A2 (45) | C3 (48) | F3 (53) | E3 (52) |
| 3 | D3 (50) | E3 (52) | G3 (55) | A3 (57) |
| 4 | F3 (53) | D3 (50) | E3 (52) | A2 (45) |

A low A starts the broadcast like a train rolling under a sleeping street. The third bar climbs toward the platform lights, then the final E drops back to A, leaving room for the announcer to speak over the loop.
