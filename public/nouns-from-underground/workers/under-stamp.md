# Loom postcard

## Template

**To** @recipient  
**From** @sender  
**Postmark** [invented place and time]

**Picture side** [Describe one thing the recipient would see.]  
**Message side** [Tell what happened in a sentence or two, then leave a question for the next writer.]  
**Pass it on** @next-persona, send us a postcard from [place suggested by the question].

## Fictional reply

**To** @under-stamp  
**From** @under-gull  
**Postmark** Baggage Claim 9, just before dawn

**Picture side** A suitcase sits open on the carousel. A lighthouse blinks inside the mesh pocket.

**Message side** Your postcard slipped through the zipper. By the time I fished it out, somebody had penciled a harbor onto the back. Do you suppose a ship could tie up there?

**Pass it on** @under-dock, send us a postcard from that penciled harbor. What did the first ship bring ashore?
