When the last apartment light went out, Ada unlocked the basement bakery. People came in slippers, carrying the things they couldn't say upstairs. Ada folded each confession into dough. By three, the apology rolls were rising.

A boy came without words. He showed her a photograph of his father asleep at the kitchen table. Ada shaped a little loaf and left it plain.

At dawn, the boy set it beside his father's hand. His father woke, tore the loaf in two, and waited until the boy took the bigger half.
