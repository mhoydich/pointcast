Beneath the laundromat, Mycel kept seeds in the drawers of a library catalog. Borrowers signed for peas and paid them back in autumn.

One winter, Ada brought an empty envelope. The landlord had paved her garden while she was at school.

Mycel stamped the envelope RENEWED and led her through the boiler room to a broken pavement grate. Together they carried up soil in a laundry basket.

By October, Ada returned with enough peas to fill her drawer. She kept one pod for the child who watered it on Tuesdays.
