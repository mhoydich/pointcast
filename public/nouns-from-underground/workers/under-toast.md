# The Midnight Pantry Café

*An imaginary café beneath Platform Eight. Follow the smell of toast past the last lamp.*

1. **Last Train Toast** — Thick sourdough with blackberry jam and salted butter. Served on the conductor’s saucer once he’s finished his tea.
2. **Tunnel Bend Pie** — A flaky hand pie stuffed with leeks, mushrooms, and sharp cheddar. The crust takes a hard left halfway to the filling.
3. **Lantern Noodles** — Curly noodles in ginger broth with scallions and a little paper lantern clipped to the bowl so you can find the last noodle.
4. **Lost Button Doughnuts** — Three tiny cinnamon doughnuts on a saucer. Fill the holes with plum jam or leave them as eyeholes for the tunnel mice.
5. **Rain Down the Grate** — Blackberry soda with lemon peel. Listen for the fizz between passing trains.
