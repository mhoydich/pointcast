    Pool math, simulation rules & Tezos launch notes

    Each local round starts with 100 simulated seed points per contender. A winning ticket receives its stake multiplied by total pool / final winning pool, rounded down to whole points. Losing tickets return zero. No fee. A void round refunds all player stakes. The price exercise permits joint winners; payouts divide the pool across all stakes on the winning outcomes. Build-offs lock predictions before you record a provisional outcome and a local result note. Finalization pays tickets once; revising a provisional receipt keeps predictions locked. Notes are supplied by you and are not independently verified. The price exercise uses its known joint-winning pool for quotes and earns no constructors points. Finish or void all four races to start a new lap. Your bankroll, tickets and constructors points carry forward; starting a lap does not add free points. Seed liquidity is artificial, and simulated seed returns are not added to your balance.

    Balances and tickets are stored in this browser. They are editable local data, are not secure assets, and do not sync across users. There are no live runs, real community odds, deposits, withdrawals, wallet connections, or deployed Tezos contracts. Clearing browser data loses the simulation. The build-off titles describe proposed trials, not scheduled events. The penny sprint is an educational price exercise with a known, reproducible answer—not a future prediction market.

    The Tezos design would use native XTZ escrow with separate stake, close, provisional result, dispute, finalize, claim and timeout-refund states. Bettors claim individually; the resolver cannot withdraw escrow. No bets after the lock time, no claimed tickets twice, and no result changes after finalization. This is a design specification, not a reviewed or deployed contract.

    A chain does not determine legal eligibility. A cash launch needs location and operator decisions before funds are accepted. Read the CFTC’s prediction-market guidance.
