WHAT IS REAL NOW
This is a researched brand and business planning package. The toy, app,
packaging and campaign are concepts. No working prototype, supplier quotation,
certification, legal clearance, customer order or partnership was obtained.
No outreach was sent and nothing was published.

DECISIONS THAT MATTER
California signed a four-year moratorium on child companion-chatbot toys.
The proposed adult mission planner requires legal classification before
development or an AI sales claim. The physical toy is planned to work offline.
Microduck is a useful benchmark, but its software and model assets have
different licenses; commercial reskin rights have not been established.

The recommended first commitment is $30k-$60k of staged discovery within the
larger program, then legal, play, engineering and supplier gates. The first
5,000 units do not recover the whole development program in the base case.
