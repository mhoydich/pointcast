METHOD AND INPUTS
Five selected images were made with Codex's built-in image generation tool.
The user's four supplied robot-dinosaur PNGs were aesthetic references for the
first mood board. Later images reference that selected mood board to maintain
the ROOK identity. No commercial rights or uniqueness clearance is asserted.
The native SVG logo studies were authored separately as editable graphics.

All images are concept visualizations, not photographs of working hardware.
The visual joint details are exploratory. The costed first toy has only two
expressive motor axes and planted feet, with no walking capability, mic or camera.
Warm amber visor, cream/graphite shell, cobalt panel and signal stripes are
design cues. Production geometry, colors, materials and warnings need approval.
