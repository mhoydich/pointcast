## 2. WordPress

Title: Camping equipment with a home address

Pacific Utility begins with a simple proposition: the objects that make a beach afternoon comfortable should also earn their space at home. The El Segundo Light Works collection imagines a shared family of rechargeable power, solar, cold storage, light and air.

Harbor Base anchors the system. Sunboard collects sunlight and explores a second life as a stand or table frame. Cold Box keeps refrigeration in the energy plan. Dock Fan and Buoy Light move from camp to the desk and bedside. Room Current scales the same visual language into a convertible home circulator.

The style guide is nautical and practical: harbor navy, sailcloth cream, Pacific blue and a small orange control. Replaceable straps, accessible fasteners, removable grilles and serviceable modules make durability part of the design rather than a slogan. Ivrea Office, Swedish Skerry and Vienna Workshop explore three related palettes and material traditions.

The field guide includes a transparent energy calculator and local scene simulations. All hardware specifications remain proposals. There is no device or AI connection, and the images are AI-generated concepts. Concept direction and signature: Michael Hoydich, El Segundo, California.
