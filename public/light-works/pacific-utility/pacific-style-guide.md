# Pacific Utility — Style Guide 02

El Segundo Light Works · Michael Hoydich · October 2026

## Idea
Camping equipment with a home address. Rechargeable light, moving air, cold storage and solar power in one beach-to-home family.

## Creative direction
An imagined 1999 Minneapolis agency brief: Leo Burnett-style warmth and a useful human truth; Ralph Lauren sailing blues; Japanese serviceability; El Segundo's ocean and practical industrial character. These are creative references, not commissioned work, historical attribution or endorsements.

## Identity
Primary wordmark: EL SEGUNDO / LIGHT WORKS. Secondary: PACIFIC UTILITY. Sail-and-hull monoline mark. Keep clear space equal to the height of LIGHT WORKS on all sides. Minimum digital lockup width: 180 px. Use one color on a solid field; no stretching or drop shadows.

## Palette
- Harbor navy: #102D47 — 65% / ANCHOR
- Sailcloth: #F1EEE3 — 20% / SPACE
- Pacific blue: #A5C7DA — 10% / AIR
- Signal orange: #CF552E — 3% / ACTION
- Brushed gray: #BBC1C1 — 2% / HARDWARE

## Typography
Georgia regular and italic for editorial headlines; Arial/Helvetica for interfaces and product names; Courier New for field codes and measurements. Short headlines, generous margins, readable labels.

## Material and form
Matte navy or ivory shell, brushed aluminum structure, replaceable woven straps, tactile physical controls, guarded corners, screw-fastened service access. Durability is a repair plan: removable grilles, replaceable gaskets, spares and documented assembly. No ingress or durability rating is established.

## Voice
Human, direct and useful. Lead with an everyday benefit. Say what an object does. Keep technical detail in the field notes. Campaign lines: A little more outside. Every day. / Take the good room with you. / Made for the long way home.

## Photography
Natural California light, real-scale objects, navy and cream, honest shadows. Show the wire, fastener and handhold. All current imagery is AI-generated concept visualization.

## Equipment register
### PU—01 / Harbor Base

The anchor of the collection: a low, wide power station with replaceable corner guards, covered ports and a proper carry handle. Live on the sand for an afternoon, then dock under the console at home.

- Energy study: 512 Wh nominal; 1,024 Wh extended concept
- Architecture: Protected DC outputs, solar input with MPPT, USB-C
- Battery direction: Serviceable LiFePO₄ module, subject to engineering
- Interface: Physical power switch, rotary dial, energy display

Add-ons: Clip-on cable tidy and labeled connector caps; Sailcloth shoulder sling and raised sand feet; Home charging cradle.

Validation: Validate battery safety, thermal management, output limits, grounding where applicable and safe service access. The 90% usable-energy factor in the planner is an illustration.

### PU—02 / Sunboard

Four framed panels fold into a flat carrying case. A locking support sets their sun angle; an optional separate tray makes a small table when charging is not the priority. A rail-mount study brings it home.

- Solar study: 100 W nominal panel scenario
- Form: Four folding segments with guarded hinges
- Second uses: Kickstand, privacy screen, removable table tray
- Power path: Matched MPPT input at Harbor Base

Add-ons: Removable tray that never bears directly on solar cells; Counterweighted balcony rail concept; Angle card and padded cable roll.

Validation: Test structural loads, panel temperatures, cell protection, stability and wind anchoring. Shading a panel reduces generation. Balcony mounts require a site-specific safe installation.

### PU—03 / Cold Box

An insulated portable refrigerator with a replaceable gasket, removable divider and easy-to-read temperature display. Its power plan starts with pre-chilling at home and giving the compressor priority at camp.

- Volume direction: 20 L internal capacity target
- Power model: 25 W averaged over compressor cycles for illustration
- Construction: Insulated shell, replaceable seal, guarded corners
- Control: Local thermostat and low-battery cutoff concept

Add-ons: Ice-pack divider for pre-chilling; Separate clip-on serving tray; Strap-on cutlery roll.

Validation: Validate actual temperature holding, ambient operating limits, average draw and ventilation clearances. No runtime or food-storage safety performance has been established.

### PU—04 / Dock Fan

A compact air circulator with a folding yoke, removable grille and replaceable small battery pack. Stand it on a table, secure it with an approved clamp, or aim it through a tent opening.

- Energy model: 12 W average in the illustrative planner
- Control: Physical speed dial; Bluetooth pairing concept
- Construction: Serviceable motor assembly and washable grille
- Power: Removable pack or protected base connection

Add-ons: Clamp mount and low, wide sand stand; Sailcloth carry strap; Passive wind scoop for an independent airflow study.

Validation: Measure airflow, acoustic output, stability, finger access protection, battery runtime and salt exposure. The wind scoop channels ambient air; it does not make electricity or refrigerate the air.

### PU—05 / Buoy Light

A warm-white portable lantern with a protected diffuser, replaceable small pack and tactile dimmer. An optional shade keeps the light on the table and out of your eyes.

- Light direction: 2700 K warm-white concept
- Energy model: 4 W average in the illustrative planner
- Power: Small removable pack, rechargeable dock
- Use: Table, bedside, secured hanging mount

Add-ons: Clip-on downward shade; Woven hanging loop; Shared charging rail.

Validation: Validate light quality, thermal behavior, charging safety, hanging strength and water/dust protection before making ratings or runtime claims.

### PU—06 / Room Current

The domestic member of the fleet: a convertible pedestal-to-table circulator. The weighted base holds a removable pack; a future home bridge could coordinate it with light and open-window routines.

- Form: Convertible pedestal / desk head concept
- Control: Local dial, optional Bluetooth and future home bridge
- Service: Removable grille, replaceable motor and base module
- Intelligence: Proposed scenes with explicit owner approval

Add-ons: Short desk stem and wall cradle; Fabric cable sleeve; Optional temperature sensor for future scene logic.

Validation: Validate tip stability, noise, oscillation safety, power consumption and every wireless integration. No live Bluetooth, Matter or AI connection is provided in this preview.

## Wind Scoop
A detachable sailcloth scoop channels ambient breeze into a tent vestibule or open window. A separate passive-airflow experiment, not electricity generation or air conditioning. Test pressure losses, wind anchoring, noise and real comfort.

## Coordinated home
Manual control comes first. Proposed Bluetooth pairing locally; a future home bridge could coordinate lighting, air and energy telemetry. Any Matter support requires engineering and certification. An AI could propose scenes with explicit owner approval. The current site is a local simulation with no device, AI or microphone connection.

## Energy planner assumptions
Usable battery = nominal Wh × 0.90. Cooler = 25 W average; fan = 12 W; light = 4 W; phone = 15 Wh once. Solar scenario = 100 W × equivalent full-sun hours × 0.65. Net budget = usable battery + generation − loads. Reserve = 20% of usable battery. This is a simplified illustrative balance, not a runtime guarantee; it does not model charging timing or storage overflow.

## Special edition studies
### Ivrea Office

Office-machine clarity, brought outdoors. Putty shells, Adriatic-blue faces, precise type and a single vermilion button. Every seam explains how the object comes apart.

Palette: Putty / Adriatic / vermilion. Detail: Tactile indexed controls. Intended setting: Desk, studio, reading room.

### Swedish Skerry

Mist blue, chalk and pale ash. A quieter expression of the same equipment, with linen carrying loops and small wood touch points. A summer-house atmosphere that still takes service seriously.

Palette: Mist / chalk / pale ash. Detail: Replaceable woven handles. Intended setting: Cabin, balcony, family room.

### Vienna Workshop

Midnight navy and parchment with brushed-brass details. Repeated square perforations give the lamp and fan a shared rhythm. Useful domestic instruments with a little ceremonial presence.

Palette: Midnight / parchment / brass. Detail: Square grid + serial plate. Intended setting: Apartment, salon, library.

Ivrea Office references Olivetti; it is not an authorized collaboration. All editions remain independent concepts.
