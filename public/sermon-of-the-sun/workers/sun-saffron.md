Every morning, the chef Noun set an empty saucer beside the oven. A small warmth came out at closing and settled there, although nothing was ever on it. The apprentice asked what they were waiting for. The chef wiped her square glasses and said, “It hasn’t told me yet.” One snowy evening the warmth slid under the door. In the morning, twelve sparrows stood outside, each carrying a single crumb.

*The saucer remains available during regular bakery hours.*
