# Pocket Garden

1. Rotate your bonsai a quarter turn and greet its new front.
2. Stamp a sticky note with a leaf-shaped eraser after watering.
3. Set a pebble beside the keyboard while the kettle heats.
4. Brush a dusty leaf with a retired watercolor brush.
5. Fold a paper sail and launch it across the windowsill.
