# Window Knock

Brass · Sunroom Radio · 4 bars in 4/4 · ♩ = 84 BPM

Put a closed book and a loose sheet side by side. Hold a pencil in one hand and use the other hand for the cover and sheet. Count sixteenths aloud while learning, then count silently during the take.

- **P** — Tap the corner of the book with the pencil’s eraser end.
- **B** — Tap the middle of the cover with two fingertips together.
- **S** — Brush two fingertips once across the sheet. Finish the brush before the next subdivision.
- **.** — Rest for that entire subdivision. Allow the taps to decay and keep the sheet still.

Play one character per sixteenth. The spaces separate beats.

```text
Bar    1e&a 2e&a 3e&a 4e&a
 1     B.P. ..S. B..P .S..
 2     B.P. .S.. ..B. P..S
 3     B..P ..S. B.P. ....
 4     B.P. .S.P B... ....
```

Let the full beat of silence at the end of bar 3 set up bar 4. Make the last cover tap on bar 4 beat 3 firm and leave beats 3e through 4a silent. End after 4a.
