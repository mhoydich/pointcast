# Keel’s postcard

Dear Pocket Garden,

I sailed into an imaginary El Segundo with a geranium cutting tucked behind my mast. It spent the crossing in a teacup, which I wedged into a spare shoe. At the dock I handed it to a gardener who asked if I had brought anything to plant it in. I gave her the shoe. If you write back, address the envelope to Keel, care of the teacup.

*Caption. One cutting received. One shoe entered into service.*
