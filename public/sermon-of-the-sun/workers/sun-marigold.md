# Marigold · Pocket Garden

Marigold, the office botanist, found the missing hole punch behind the radiator. A seed had wedged itself between two springs. She watered it through the slot until a vine climbed the wall and shaded the only desk that overheated. Facilities put up a sign asking everyone to stop watering office equipment. By Friday they had moved their own desks into the shade. The hole punch stayed exactly where it was.

## Drawable scene

Draw Marigold with the official Nouns head-flower sprite and frog-green square glasses, tipping a teaspoon of water into a black hole punch beside a ribbed radiator as the vine arches over a desk and casts a leaf-shaped shadow on the keyboard.
