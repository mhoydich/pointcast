# Prism · Fireside Cards quiet motion

Proposed acceptance criteria for OFF-65, based on the task brief and Ada’s handoff. Tests have not been run by Prism.

1. **Reduced motion on entry.** Set the OS preference to Reduce before loading the sketch. Each card shows the hearth poster, excerpt, and topic. Starting the session leaves the poster still. The flame video, animated card transitions, and swipe-follow effects stay off. Sound remains off until the visitor explicitly opts in.
2. **Readiness uses session time.** Start the same card in motion and reduced-motion modes with a controlled clock. At 29 seconds, Next, Open thread, and Sit longer remain gated, and swipe or arrow input does not change cards. At 30 seconds, the same choices and eligible navigation become available in both modes. Repeat with video playback blocked. Readiness follows the session clock even if the video never plays or ends.
3. **Pause and preference changes preserve state.** At 12 seconds, enable Reduce. The flame becomes still while the card, elapsed time, and sound consent carry over. Pause, advance the test clock by 30 seconds, and verify the card is still paused at 12 seconds with fire audio stopped. After resuming, the remaining 18 seconds lead to readiness. Switching motion preference while paused does not resume the session.
4. **Still navigation after readiness.** In reduced motion, use each supported route to Next in separate runs: button, swipe, and arrow key. Each eligible action switches once to the next card immediately, with its still poster and fresh reading interval. Sit longer keeps the same card, and Open thread points to the displayed excerpt’s source. Changing cards never grants sound consent.
5. **Scripts-off fallback.** Disable JavaScript and load the sketch on a phone-sized viewport. The initial excerpt, topic, and still hearth render without interaction. If the poster fails to load, the excerpt remains readable on the fallback background. The page presents no controls that depend on a running session.
