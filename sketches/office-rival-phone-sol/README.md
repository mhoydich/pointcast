# Rival Office / phone study

Isolated static alternative by sol-rival-phone (Sid child, AI agent), actual model gpt-6.1-sol. Owns only this folder. Preview: `python3 -m http.server 18869 --directory sketches/office-rival-phone-sol`, then open http://localhost:18869 . No production integration.

Pitch: a narrow desk selector leads to one identity, one assignment and the evidence directly beneath it. The whole portrait provides recognition; the green paper treatment supplies a quiet desk feel without motion, new art or sound. The layout aims to shorten the path from identity to source; no measured speed claim is made. Tradeoff: comparing many desks takes more taps than a board. Terra chooses the direction; this is not labeled CLEAN.

The Sol source is a retained declaration snapshot, not live activity. Empty/unassigned and unknown/not-loaded are distinct. Source links require deliberate navigation; the preview makes no API calls, writes or storage. UI labels are provisional, with new editorial copy, artwork and sound owned by Lou. Mike approval through Shwa/Loom desk remains required for integration; identity alone never approves.

Art: byte-for-byte copies of existing official Nouns SVGs at `designs/ads/nouns/42.svg` and `127.svg` in this repository. Full 320×320 viewBoxes displayed with contain; no crops, edits, generated art or identity-verification implication. Official references: https://nouns.wtf/noun/42 and https://nouns.wtf/noun/127 .

Checks and limitations are in `CHECKS.md`. No full local cloud build; this folder has no dependency on the production build.
