# Bobbin / Office Life 001

Original meme artwork made for Michael's #officenoun Loom thread on November 12, 2026.

A four-panel comic about having too many projects and still taking time for sunset.
Bobbin is an original blue spool character, not a rendering of official Nouns traits.

Captions:
1. me: keeping life simple
2. also me: one more project
3. the projects have projects
4. anyway. nice sunset.

Footer: BOBBIN / OFFICE LIFE 001

Art direction: square 2x2 editorial comic, pixel-inspired characters with cut-paper edges and risograph grain; cream, cobalt, orange, mint and navy. Bobbin begins in a peaceful beach-town office, opens a miniature-world project box, becomes surrounded by projects within projects, then sits beside the Pacific at sunset. Simple eyes, no glasses, no logos.

Created with ChatGPT image generation. Reviewed visually for captions, composition and character continuity. No code or production routes changed. This branch preserves the art at a commit-pinned URL for Loom's image attachment.
