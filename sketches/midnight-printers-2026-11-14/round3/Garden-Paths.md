# Midnight Printers — Garden Paths

*Authored reading-room passages. Integrated into the interactive reading room and published as two side passages on Loom; each public text was read back exactly. Both paths revisit the same established story and return to the shared garden. They do not alter events or imply reader contributions.*

## Stay at the gate

Stay beside the garden gate for a moment.

Barley’s water catches a small rectangle of light from the upstairs window. He lowers his muzzle and drinks. A few drops fall onto the earth.

Above him, a chair scrapes. One ear turns. The other stays toward the path.

Bridle has already set Barley’s empty harness on the wall. Barley shifts a hind foot, rests it, and a jacaranda petal stays caught in his mane. The nasturtiums move when he breathes near them.

Upstairs, a door closes. The last light goes out.

Then come footsteps: Hem, Bridle, and the apprentice, carrying a lunch tin.

Barley lifts his head. At the gate, he waits while the others gather, then walks beside them beneath the palms.

By morning, this same courtyard will hold thirty-seven flower cards and rather more guests than anyone expected.

Come back through the gate for breakfast. The next chapter begins here.

## Follow the warm bread

The smell reaches the corner before you can see the bakery.

Crumb opens the window. Yellow light falls across the sill, where flour has collected in the little cracks of the wood. Inside, the rolls sit close together in their basket.

Hem still has orange ink on his sleeve. The apprentice carries his lunch tin. Bridle stands beside Barley, who lowers his head as a small parcel passes through the window.

Thirty-seven flower cards are finished.

Crumb has twenty-four rolls.

For tonight, there is a warm heel of bread for Hem and a piece of carrot from Bridle’s pocket for Barley. Behind them, the print shop is dark. Its paper flowers will dry while everyone sleeps.

Tomorrow, Crumb will bring the basket to the courtyard. The neighbors will bring their cups. Three strangers will follow the same smell down the path.

Return to the garden and join them for breakfast in the next chapter.

## Scene caption

One upstairs window still glows above the garden. Barley waits by the gate; around the corner, Crumb opens the bakery window. Paper flowers are drying, bread is warm, and the palms move in the sea air. Stay a moment, or follow the smell. Both paths lead back to breakfast.
