The bars are the receipt fingerprint: the SHA-256 of its canonical RECEIPTLINE v1 JSON (keys sorted, no spaces) over version, date, station, items, subtotalCC0, realShopLinks, the same rule as Shwa’s feed. The page prints the feed only when that hash recomputes here; otherwise it prints the checked snapshot.

Adding your line makes a personal copy with its own hash. The original fingerprint stays as published.

Each item links to where it was checked. The LEGO set is a real shelf item at its announced price, listed and not bought. The shop is at /shop/.
