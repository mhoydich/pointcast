LAYERS RADIO · ACOUSTIC SPIKE

Local harness, not a product surface. ggwave 0.4.0 is loaded from vendor/ (MIT). Everything heard through the air is public and unverified. Nothing here writes to saved Layers settings.

Start with your volume low. Send plays a data burst through your speakers. Listen opens your microphone only when you press it, and Stop / Esc closes it.

Sender states: Preparing → Playing → Broadcast played. There is no acknowledgement; a receiver may or may not exist. Preview plays only this mood's local motif and wash, no modem bytes. Loopback decodes the generated waveform in software and is codec evidence only, not a room test.
