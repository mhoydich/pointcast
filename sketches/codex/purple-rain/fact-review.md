## Findings and disposition

1. **RIAA chronology: resolved and passing.** RIAA's [program history](https://www.riaa.com/goldandplatinum60/) explicitly records 13-times multi-Platinum for *Purple Rain* in 1996 and the introduction of Diamond in 1999. The revised Results and Time text preserves both facts. The 13 million measure is a U.S. certification threshold, not current retail sales or worldwide copies. RIAA's [award criteria](https://www.riaa.com/gold-platinum/about-awards/) support territory and thresholds. The live [album database query](https://www.riaa.com/gold-platinum/?ar=Prince&format=Album&tab_active=default-award&ti=Purple+Rain&type=) was also retrieved: its current Diamond row carries 16 May 1996. That retrospective database label must not imply Diamond existed in 1996; the revised program-history formulation avoids that ambiguity.
2. **Museum costume record: resolved and passing.** The replaced MNHS URL now resolves to the [specific archived object record](https://mnhs.gitlab.io/archive/mplsmusic/www.mnhs.org/collections/mplsmusic/featured_objects/princes_costume.html), including the ruffled collar, right-shoulder faux-mail, diagonal fly and rear coat vent for movement. Archive date June 2021 is separate from the historical costume's era.
3. **Registry wording: resolved and passing.** Timeline titles now say `The album enters the registry` and `The film enters the registry`. Retrieved sources establish registry selection rather than completion of a particular physical preservation treatment. The event bodies and 2011-class/2019 dates are correct.
4. **Source-note cleanup: resolved and passing.** The `riaa-criteria` note now describes U.S. certification thresholds and the Diamond tier's 1999 introduction, with the distinction from lifetime retail sales. The obsolete database-date sentence is removed.

## Limits

- This review verifies displayed claims against retrievable records; it does not audit sales accounts or unpublished garment/photo provenance. The worldwide sales total remains the publisher's dated assertion.
- Direct requests for the LOC full recording/film lists and the old 2011-class page returned 403. Alternate LOC essay/2019 announcements and the joint label statement supplied the verification above. No exact 2012 announcement day is asserted in the draft.
- Commons descriptions/date fields establish source attribution, not independent concert-camera-clock validation. Retain the explicit Berlin qualification and the context boundaries on later photographs.
- Rights clearance, visual inspection of every downloaded image, local-asset hashes, mobile/keyboard/reduced-motion/no-JS tests and publication approval belong to the other review/work streams. Nothing in this fact-review pass authorizes publication.
