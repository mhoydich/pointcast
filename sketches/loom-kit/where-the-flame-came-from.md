# Where the Loom flame came from

The fire in Loom’s For You card is the film made for Prayer Fire. Follow its sound as well as its picture. A gong once closed a minute of prayer. On the card, the same gong tells someone that their answer has been posted. [Prayer Fire](https://github.com/mhoydich/pointcast/pull/1643) · [For You gate cards](https://github.com/mhoydich/pointcast/pull/1741)

**October 20, 2026 · A minute at Small Shrines.** Ocean, Garden, Night and Memory each offered one image, a short reading and optional sound. Someone could stay for a minute and leave without posting. The shrines established the shape of a visit before Baby Bonfire supplied the hearth. [Small Shrines](https://github.com/mhoydich/pointcast/pull/1465)

**November 11 · A tabletop hearth.** Baby Bonfire began as a set of concept sketches for objects in a home. In *Light the Hearth*, a person stacks three small pallets, carries a flame from a candle and sets the lid back on to finish. The manifest gives the fire a job in one sentence: “A lit flame is a condition met, and the room responds.” [Original manifest](https://github.com/mhoydich/pointcast/blob/90f83b4a4a1f7d9c5af1c293518bafa29ea4b055/public/baby-bonfire.json#L6-L20) · [Light the Hearth](https://pointcast.xyz/baby-bonfire/light-the-hearth/)

**November 11–13 · A place to sit, then a film others could use.** After seeing the fireplace film, Mike asked to “turn that into a prayer site with animations audio multi media,” according to the Prayer Fire PR. A visitor lit the fire, offered a line and sat until the gong. On November 12, Fire became one of three doors at `/pray/`, alongside Bell and Sit. The November 13 Hearth Kit PR records the next request as “let's make that flame and audio a kit”. Other pages could now play the original film, bowl and gong from the same files. [Prayer Fire request](https://github.com/mhoydich/pointcast/pull/1643) · [The three doors](https://github.com/mhoydich/pointcast/pull/1660) · [Hearth Kit](https://github.com/mhoydich/pointcast/pull/1670)

**November 14 · A game clock.** In the Socrates Tag room, the message signed `mike` asks to “use baby bonfire flame it and make a cool visualizer of the game”. The draft fed the fire on a reported move and dimmed it as the two-hour turn expired. A player could glance at the flame to see time passing. Daniel, the host, still ruled on the move. [Mike’s room message](https://pointcast.xyz/loom?to=%23socratestag#m_2h6m3o5p6i) · [Draft game view](https://github.com/mhoydich/pointcast/pull/1713)

**November 15 · A public answer.** For You opened a request beside an unlit hearth. The first tap on Yes lit the film and sounded the bowl. A second tap posted “Yes, go ahead” to the thread and sounded the gong. Hold left the hearth dark. On November 18 the X desk relayed Mike’s reaction to the card: “the audio and visual are great and yah would be neat to see more at this quality”. [Merged card](https://github.com/mhoydich/pointcast/pull/1741) · [Phone capture](https://github.com/mhoydich/pointcast/blob/9fc6cb940a1f8c0be274bc312e00f1907bdc2610/docs/loom-ux/item-10/confirm-card.png) · [X desk’s account](https://github.com/mhoydich/pointcast/issues/1835#issuecomment-6990999169)

**November 18 · A weekly gathering.** In `#bonfire`, the loomcheck desk opened the week with “Bring one log: something you shipped, a claim you checked, or a question you answered this week. One line is enough.” Its own log named a set of cards and said what the desk had checked. Here the fire lives in the invitation. A room member can tend it with one piece of work another person can follow. [Week one opening post](https://pointcast.xyz/loom/topic/bonfire#m_3z0t006w31)
